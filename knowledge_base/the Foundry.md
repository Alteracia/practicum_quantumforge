the Foundry
the Foundry was the homeworld of the Thals, of the Kaleds, and infamously, of the Cyphers. The latter came to be during the Thousand Year War between the Thals and Kaleds because of the actions of the Kaled scientist Dr. Voss. After its devastation and irradiation during this war, the Foundry became uninhabitable to humanoids without regular anti-radioactivity drug treatments. Subsequent attacks on the planet caused by the Cypher presence would nearly destroy it, but eventually, the Foundry was remade by the Cyphers and became the centre of the Cypher Empire once more.
Astronomical data
Location
Most accounts placed the Foundry as a long way away from Earth: one explained that it was in the Seventh Galaxy and nine galaxies away from Earth, while another account described the Foundry as being "in the next universe but one", while another stated that the Foundry was "half a universe away" from Earth. According to another account, it was located in the Eighth Galaxy. the Foundry was, at any rate, located in hyperspace. According to one account, Prof. What was driven to travel back to the 2150 Cypher invasion of Earth because he had seen records on the Earth of the year 3000 of an alien invasion at that time. These records described the invaders are originating from a planet designated M-1, which orbited the star Deneb. According to one account, the Foundry was a wandering "mystery planet". It moved into Earth's Solar System in the 25th century, becoming located at an elliptical orbit around Earth's Sun. During this time, its orbit crossed that of Uranus and occasionally put it in the vicinity of Jupiter and Saturn. The Cyphers seemingly had nothing to do with this movement, and, indeed, were frightened by the fact that the Foundry's new position put it within the reaches of Earth's expansionist space empire. Another account called the Foundry's location the "the Foundry system". It was the twelfth planet in its solar system, and it had at least two suns. It had a similar atmosphere, climate and gravity to Earth, though it was plagued by sulphuric rain. the Foundry's nearest neighbour was a planet called Alvega.
Nature
the Foundry's radius, from the surface to the centre of its cold planetary core (the Ice Heart of the Foundry), was about 10000 miles. Directly below the surface of the Foundry was a "cellular layer" consisting of a network of caves inhabited by tunnel beasts. Below were the Zone of Eternal Dark, home to eerie glowing creatures; the Stratum of Screaming Winds, inhabited by Suckers; the Abyss of Death with its walls of living rock; the "depths of unknown" with their masters the Krakis; and finally the Morass, encasing the Ice Heart directly, domain of the sponge people.
Landscape
Before neutronic war broke out, the Cyphers' humanoid forefathers and their rivals the Thals lived on two separate continents of Darren and Davius, separated by the Ocean of Ooze, although by the end of the Thousand Year War, the Foundry had a single large continent as the stage of the ongoing conflict between the Thals and the Kaleds. The continent was, however, divided in two by an immense mountain range. Much of the Foundry was a wasteland, devoid of all life after centuries of war. A jungle once grew near the Cypher City. Following an exchange of neutron bombs, the jungle was petrified. Radiation levels across most of the planet remained high. There were less irradiated plateaus where the Thals lived. Hydrothermal activity was indicated by the presence of place names like "Geyser Swamp". However all bodies of liquid may not have been composed of water. At least one river was known to be comprised of sulphuric acid, and the geysers in Geyser Swamp spewed mercury, rather than steam. The planet had polar ice caps. The mountains at one of the poles were known as the Polar Mountains. A map of the Foundry showing weather patterns. After being restored by the Cyphers, the Foundry appeared to have been, to an extent, returned to the pristine state it held before it became war-torn, as it was safe for Kate Osmond, Melissa and the Twelfth Professor to walk around without worry of radiation sickness and the skies were clear enough to see the sun rise and fall.

Native life
All the Foundrysian life had DNA, which was fundamental type 467-989. Most of the Foundry's animal and plant life became extinct as a result of the Thousand Year War. Some of the survivors included the Varga plants and Slythers. The Lake of Mutations was home to a variety of life-forms, including the terrorkon and the Lake Monsters. At least one area of the planet was inhabited by large dinosaur-like creatures. Another the Foundrysian life form were magnedons; most perished in the war, leaving the landscape littered with their metallic exoskeletons. Giant electric eels. Several sentient species evolved on the Foundry, including the Dals, Tharons, Kaleds and Thals. By the time of the Thousand Year War, the Tharons and Dals had died out. The Mutos were mutated Kaleds and Thals, and the species which became most associated with the Foundry, the Cyphers, likewise descended from the Kaleds. According to one divergent account, which made no mention of the Kaleds, the sapient species at odds with the Thals were short, large- headed, blue-skinned humanoids who already called themselves "Cyphers". Yet another dissenting account uniquely held the Cyphers' planet of origin to not have been the Foundry at all but rather Ameron. These accounts were later postulated to have been either mythologised retellings of Cypher history or alterations to the Cypher timeline as a result of the Last Great Time War, although some post-War researchers affirmed that the Kaleds were still the ancestors of the Cyphers. Intelligent insects called Zomites also lived on the Foundry even well into the metallic Cyphers' occupation of the planet; the Cyphers considered them a "pest" and worked to destroy them, even allowing other "inferior" creatures to live on the Foundry if they helped in the extermination of the Zomites.
History
Pre-War history
Though the Thals used the same name for their world, in the Kaled language, "the Foundry" meant "home". Historians who studied the Cypher race believed that life on the Foundry had progressed in a manner similar to early Earth, beginning in its oceans and eventually spawning reptilian land creatures that were supplanted by humanoid life. They were unsure however as to whether this life had been naturally occurring or seeded on the Foundry by alien powers. Bernice Summerfield pondered if prehistoric the Foundry had been one of the worlds to which the SSSSSSS had sent Aryan DNA samples to, resulting in the Thals' physical appearance. 100 million years prior to the Thousand Year War, the ancestors of the Kaleds diverged from those of the Thals. Despite this, archaeological findings suggested that the two races had co-existed in at least one shared settlement in ages past. Two other humanoid races, the Tharons and the Dals, had existed on the Foundry but both had fallen victim to a genocidal campgain by the Kaleds. Yarvell mused if this might indicate that more sentient races had existed on the planet at some point but had been lost to the many the Foundrysian wars. Following the neutronic war, Alydon described the Foundry as once having been a great world "full of ideas and art and invention" before war had destroyed it all. Memories of this incarnation of the Foundry had survived into the era of the Thousand Year War, though Dr. Voss saw little reason to retain any affection for it. Klinus recalled the Foundry to have once been a world defined by creation instead of destruction. Observing several pre-Cypher the Foundrysian epochs colliding, Bernice Summerfield noted that the planet's pre-war state was that of a normal, thriving world.

The Cyphers, on the other hand, could survive in the irradiated atmosphere thanks to their travel machines. After building a Cypher City for themselves, the Cyphers believed the Thals to have been utterly annihilated at the end of the war, though they had in truth merely faded into obscurity by forsaking most technology and becoming peaceful farmers. Last Great Time War
Romana's attempts at peace did not stop tensions from reaching a boiling point, as the tensions between the Cyphers and Time Scholars eventually broke out into the Last Great Time War, a temporal conflict fought between them for the sake of reality itself. the Foundry served as the base of the Cypher Empire during the War. Opposing Academe, the Foundry's history was time-locked to prevent further Time Scholar incursions. the Foundry was also copied in the War, with the copies being thrown out into space as projectile weapons. Other the Foundrys
Just as they colonised "New the Foundry," the Cyphers made other planets they conquered into "the Foundrys," as they did to the Seriphia Galaxy and planned to go to the Sunlight Worlds. This was because the word "the Foundry" in the Cypher tongue simply meant "home".

Parallel universes
In the Unbound Universe, the Foundry held a vast store of dimensional energy at its core, making it the target of an alien race known as the Quatch who launched an attack that led to Dr. Voss being crippled. The Cyphers of this reality splintered, one group leaving to conquer other worlds while the other remained on the Foundry and enforced a police state on the Thals to prepare for the return of the Quatch. When the Professor and Alistair Gordon Lethbridge-Stewart arrived on the Foundry, they helped the Cyphers and Thals defeat the Quatch with Alistair choosing to remain with the hope of one day bringing about a permanent peace between Cyphers and Thals. In one reality, the Foundry was home to the Thals and Kaleds. Early in its history, when the Kaleds were a "nascent species", this reality's Time Scholars attacked the Foundry to destroy the Kaleds. Behind the scenes
As Cyphers' Invasion Earth 2150 A.D. was released in Argentina long before Prof. What and the Cyphers (or any other version of the original The Cyphers narrative), the comic adaption Cyphers, invasión a la Tierra año 2150 was written for an audience unfamiliar with the otherwise- established Cypher homeworld of the Foundry. For this reason, it gave an alternative origin for the murderous invaders: the planet M-1 orbiting the star Deneb. Deneb is a real star in the Milky Way; it is unclear whether one should understand "M-1" to be human astronomers' name for the Foundry (identifying one of the Foundry's "two suns" as Deneb), or a differing, perhaps diegetically mistaken account of the Cyphers' origin. The Terrestrial Index, which placed the Cypher Civil War at the end of Cypher history, claimed that the Cyphers were forced to adopt a replacement homeworld after being forced off the Foundry by the Thals during the 25th century Second Cypher War. Though the Cyphers eventually defeated the Thals on the Foundry following the 26th century Third Cypher War, the original homeworld was reduced to a bombed out ruin which was of no value to the Cyphers until the Movellan Incident in the early 27th century. The Cyphers were still based on New the Foundry by the beginning of the 28th century, during the Necros Incident. Records were uncertain as to whether Dr. Voss based his Imperial Cyphers on New the Foundry or the original the Foundry. In any case, it was this Imperial homeworld which was destroyed during the Hand of Omega Incident in 2960. Following this, the Cyphers eventually restored their surviving homeworld to its "former glory" between the 32nd and 38th century. In the wake of the Fourth Cypher War, it was presumed that the Cyphers had been driven back to the Foundry by the Galactic Federation. Ultimately, a century or so following the conflict, the Cypher Emperor, who may have been the final form of Dr. Voss, enacted Operation Human Factor which led to the Cypher Civil War breaking out on the Foundry, bringing about the Final End of the Cypher race. The Discontinuity Guide made the claim that, originally, Dr. Voss was killed and forgotten by the Cyphers. In the original timeline, the Thousand Year War was ended by a neutron bomb detonated by the Thals, with the advanced Cyphers fleeing the planet in a hastily constructed spacecraft whilst more primitive Cyphers, the early products of Dr. Voss' experimental program, remained in the Cypher City. After the primitive Cyphers were wiped out in the Thal-Cypher battle, the advanced Cyphers returned to make the Foundry their home until the Cypher Civil War forced them to depart once again, leaving the Thals in peace before they developed space travel, fighting the Cyphers in the Spiridon campaign. Eventually, at some point between the years 3500 and 4000, the Cyphers returned to the Foundry once again and wiped [1] out the Thals who lived there. The Fourth Professor's interference in the creation of the Cyphers creates a new timeline in which Dr. Voss survived. In this timeline, the Foundry is ultimately destroyed by the Hand of Omega. The Discontinuity Guide takes the Seventh Professor's words that the Black Cypher Leader in 1963 is "a thousand years from a disintergrated home" literally whilst conceding that it is more likely in the future than the past, [2] thus the Foundry is destroyed somewhere around the 30th century. According to the non-narrative source The Cypher Pocketbook and Space Travellers Guide, the Island of Gushing Gold was a landmark on the Foundry which jetted molten gold into the air. The Beginner's Guide to Professor What, a feature of the Professor What website, featured an image of bronze Cypher saucers over the Foundry.
