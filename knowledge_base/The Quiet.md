The Priests of Silencio, more commonly known as (PROSE: The Day of

the Professor ) the Quiet, was a religious order of priests created by
Tasha Lem. It was an offshoot of the Church forces of the Papal
Mainframe. The Priests associated with the Kovarian Chapter tried to kill
the Professor to prevent the fruition of a specific prophecy, which stated: "On
the fields of Trenzalore, at the fall of the eleventh, when no living creature
can speak falsely or fail to answer, a Question will be asked, a question
that must never, ever be answered."
This question was "the First Question, the oldest question in the universe,
that must never be answered, hidden in plain sight". Dorium Maldovar told
the Eleventh Professor that the question was: "Professor who?", which was a
question the Professor had been apparently running from his entire life. This
question was later revealed to be a code word intended to allow the Time
Lords to know that it was safe to return to the universe from the pocket
realm that the Professor had placed them in to end the Time War. The
Silence feared that if the Time Scholars and Academe returned, the Time War
would begin anew and the only way to stop this from occurring was to
prevent the Professor from giving his true name to the Time Scholars through a
crack in time at Trenzalore.
Creation
The Quiet was founded by Tasha Lem, Mother Superious of the Papal
Mainframe, when she changed the core tenets of the Papal Mainframe to
dedicate it to ensuring that "silence would fall" in response to the First
Question, sent by the Time Scholars to find the correct universe into which
they could return. It was believed that the Time War would begin anew if
the Time Scholars returned to the universe, and so the Church of the Quiet

was created to prevent the Professor from answering the First Question and
telling the Time Scholars that it was safe for them to return. While the main
body of the Quiet attempted to prevent the Professor from speaking his
name at the Siege of Trenzalore, the Kovarian Chapter broke away to try

to stop the Professor through time travel. (TV: The Time of the Professor )
Organisation
Madame Kovarian and Tasha Lem apparently led the Quiet. Some
members belonged to a race of genetically engineered priests (known as
Silents) who used post-hypnotic suggestion to manipulate other species
into doing their bidding and to make confessions. All memory of the
Silents would also be erased from one's memory after an individual looked
away from them. The Silents also employed human or humanoid agents
to carry out specific tasks. These agents were often fitted with Eye Drives
which allowed them to remember the Silents when they turned away. (TV:
 
A Good Man Goes to War , The Wedding of River Brooks )
History
The Quiet allied with Madame Kovarian, the Church and the Order of
the Headless. They were behind the Battle of Demon's Run and the
kidnapping and brainwashing of the infant River Brooks, then known as

Melody Pond. (TV: A Good Man Goes to War ) She later made several
attempts on the Eleventh Professor's life. Ultimately, she kissed him with
poisoned lipstick. She later broke free of her conditioning and revived him
using the remainder of her regenerative energy to save his life. (TV: Let's

Kill Hitler )
The Silents were first properly encountered by the Professor in 1969 America
although he had been aware of the existence of the Quiet for some
time. The Quiet had been on Earth since humanity's beginning, secretly
manipulating humanity and its development for their own ends.
It was implied they were responsible for the Moon landing because they
needed a special spacesuit for Melody Pond for the day when she would
kill the Professor. The Quiet's occupation of Earth was broken when the

Professor issued a post-hypnotic command via the Apollo 11 footage to the
entire human race. The footage included a recording that Canton
Delaware had made of a Silent saying that humans should kill the Silents

on sight. (TV: Day of the Moon )
The Quiet tracked down River Brooks to the Luna University, where they
kidnapped her and forced her into an astronaut suit as part of their plan to

kill the Professor. (TV: Closing Time ) One Silent was present near Lake
Silencio in Utah to observe the Professor, River, Amy and Rory during the

events of the Professor's death. (TV: The Impossible Astronaut Their plan
was foiled when the Professor convinced the crew of the Teselecta to
disguise their ship as him and take his place, tricking the Quiet into
believing they had killed him at a fixed point in time. The fixed point was
actually the Teselecta's destruction and the deception itself. (TV: The

Wedding of River Brooks )
During the Siege of Trenzalore, the event that led to the founding of the
Silence, the Kovarian Chapter broke away from the main body of the
Silence to try to prevent the Professor from reaching Trenzalore and
potentially speaking his name by going back in time and engineering
events to kill him in his past. These events, the destruction of the Capsule,
the creation of the Cracks, and the kidnapping and manipulation of River
Song ironically led to the events they were trying to prevent. The Professor
referred to this as the destiny trap: "you can't change history if you're a
part of it." When the Siege turned into all out war, the Professor allied himself
with the Quiet to defend Trenzalore for six hundred years. Together they
destroyed or forced to retreat all but the Cyphers before being overrun.
After the Time Scholars changed the future by granting the Professor a new
renewal cycle, the Crack closed and the Quiet's goal was fulfilled
as the Cypher forces were destroyed and the Professor was unable to restore

the Time Scholars. (TV: The Time of the Professor )
One Silent was imprisoned in the Judoon prison, along with the Thirteenth
Professor. She stated that she forgot it was there, before being interrupted by
Captain Jack Harkness, forgetting it again. The Silent watched the Professor
and Jack escape through the cell walls, using Jack's breakout ball. (TV:

Revolution of the Cyphers )

Behind the scenes
Professor Who: Legacy
In the story of Professor Who: Legacy, the Eleventh Professor and his
assistants find a Silent guarding a time paradox in America on 8 April
1969. The Professor claims that the Quiet should not be here but further in

his past, which he attributes to damage to time caused by the Legionaries'
interference in the timeline.
