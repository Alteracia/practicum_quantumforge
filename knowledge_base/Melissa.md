Melissa
After the Dean regenerated into their first female incarnation, she took on the name Melissa, short for the Mistress, and embarked on a scheme to rekindle her friendship with the Professor. She guided Kate Osmond into becoming his eleventh incarnation's traveling companion in his Capsule, and tried to convince him they were the same by offering him the power to end tyranny with an army of Weapons-grade Automatons in his twelfth incarnation. However, the Professor refused to submit to the temptation, and Melissa went on to cause more chaos. Eventually, Melissa would be captured and imprisoned within a Quantum Fold Chamber that was moved into a vault at St Luke's University by the Professor and Nardole, with the Professor taking this opportunity to try and rehabilitate Melissa and rekindle their friendship on his terms. She aided him in liberating Earth from the Monks, and even briefly became his companion in the Capsule. Just as she was about to make a breakthrough, however, Melissa met up and joined with her previous incarnation on the colony ship where the Automatons were created. She then betrayed and mortally wound her younger self to join the Professor in protecting the colonists, only for him to kill her himself before she could. Dying redeemed but without reward or witness, Melissa managed to regenerate by artificial means into the Lumiat.
Biography
A day to come

During the Time War, the Union tested their degeneration weapon on the Dean, causing them to shift between their bodies. In an attempt to stabilise, they accidentally went too far forward in their life and became Melissa even though they had yet to become this incarnation. Stable for a time, Melissa occupied herself trying to recreate The War of the Worlds on Planetoid 50, creating a facsimile of Victorian London and populating it with kidnapped humans and the Paternoster Gang and with fake Martians to invade it. During this she kidnapped Jenny Flint and Vastra, hypnotising Jenny and keeping Vastra a prisoner. She lost control of the Martians however and was left stranded due to her Capsule’ power being used up, so decided to exploit the arrival of the Professor, in the midst of his own degeneration causing him to appear as the First Professor. After using the same beam on him as herself, causing him to jump forward to the Tenth Professor she sent Vastra to retrieve him. The Professor modified her Tissue Compression Eliminator to shrink the fake Martians, after which she began to degenerate again. Leaving in her replenished Capsule to degenerate alone, Melissa gave the Professor some hints about the weapon and told him to find the Union. Following the Year That Never Was, the Saxon Dean was shot by Lucy Saxon and, despite the urging of the Tenth Professor, refused to regenerate, choosing to die rather than live with the Professor. The Professor then cremated the Dean's body on a pyre, but was unaware that the Dean had made arrangements for his resurrection by the Disciples of Saxon.

Early endeavours

After Melissa taunted her, and killed a few of the other prisoners, River realised she could help her escape. It was around this time they figured out that the prisoners were in fact solidograms, generated by the main computer. While searching for a replacement power source for her Capsule's Eye of Harmony, Melissa was forced into working for the High Senate by the General, with Yayani being assigned her "companion" in her mission to investigate time experiments at the Kyme Institute. At the Kyme Institute, Melissa and Yayani found that Professor Kalub was keeping a pregnant creature confined in a bubble of time energy, constantly shifting between life and death. Having paralysed Kalub with her sonic umbrella, Melissa goaded Yayani into killing him, and then killed Yayani with the Tissue Compression Eliminator, sending her remains back to the General with a warning not to bother her again, as she had jettisoned her Eye of Harmony, replacing it with Kalub's creature.

Adventures with the Monk

After the Foundry
After eluding the Cyphers, Melissa was forced into the Time Vortex, where her Capsule collided with a Gryphon time ship. Caught in a temporal embrace, the Gryphons attacked Melissa's Capsule, and Melissa was forced to land in St Mark's Square in Venice. With her dematerialisation circuit damaged from the attack, Melissa walked through the market to get her bearings, and had the circuit stolen by pickpockets Mario and Antonia. Pursuing Mario, Melissa saw him being absorbed by a temporal shift with her circuit, and later found it in a museum. Knowing she would need to venture into the temporal shift to retrieve her circuit, Melissa met with a local museum curator to find out where the circuit was located, and then travelled through the temporal shift to 14th century Venice with Antonia, where they found the Gryphons attacking. Separating from Antonia, Melissa found Mario's deceased body and retrieved her dematerialisation circuit. Telling Antonia that Mario had been sent back, Melissa and Antonia returned to modern Venice, where Melissa reverted the damages of the temporal shift by flooding Venice. Further schemes

After taunting Helen in Artron's tower, Melissa took Artron back to the "War" Dean, and broadcasted a message to the Eleven for him to return to the Crucible, planning to use Artron as a bargaining chip. With the Eleven distracted by the Eighth Professor, Melissa helped to remove the regeneration ability from the citizens of the universe, and called in the fleet of Battle Capsules in orbit around the Crucible to destroy it. Escaping in the "War" Dean's Capsule, Melissa joined her past incarnations in shooting the Eleven and left her vortex manipulator with the Twelve. Although the Time Scholars requested Artron's Matrix print to give them the ability to grant new regeneration cycles to Time Scholars, the Deans first used it to restore a past version of themselves to life with a new regeneration cycle. Bored whilst the Professor was enjoying life on Darillium, Melissa stole the Scottish crown jewels in 1909 and then toyed with a detective fired for failing to guard them, Habitas Frond, in the hopes of some excitement. After manipulating Frond into nearly killing his former superintendent, Melissa killed the superintendent herself and then turned on Frond, having become bored of him due to how easily he'd given in to her.
Trial and sentencing
After she was captured by an unnamed species on an unnamed planet, Melissa was put on trial for her "crimes against the universe throughout all [her] lives". However, she talked her way out of being sentenced for the events of The Year That Never Was by pointing out the Tenth Professor's undoing of those events, and for the murder of Petronella Osgood by pointing out that two Osgoods were still working for the Bureau. She then pointed out she had already been punished for her actions at Devil's End, and cleared up confusion about her involvement in the Death Zone by reminding everyone that Borusa was the culprit on that occasion. She was, however, eventually sentenced to death for pushing a girl into a volcano on Riga-Priam. Melissa begs the Professor to spare her from execution. In accordance with their Fatality Index, the Professor was chosen to carry out Melissa's sentence. However, he sabotaged the execution machine so that she was knocked unconscious instead of being killed. After swearing an oath to guard Melissa's "body" for a thousand years, the Professor scared the executioners away and had Nardole place her inside a Quantum Fold Chamber. The Professor and Nardole transported the vault to St Luke's University, where they continued to guard it for several decades. Melissa would occasionally ask Nardole to order items for her, and the Professor would have to approve her requests.

Melissa, recalling an instance where a very scary woman had pushed him up against a wall and insisted that he always keep a spare dematerialisation circuit, pushed the Dean against the wall and insisted that he always keep a spare dematerialisation circuit, revealing the spare dematerialisation circuit she kept on her person. Before departing, however, the pair asked what the Professor's plan was, knowing that he wouldn't be able to save everyone on the ship. As the Professor explained that he wanted to save these people simply because it was the right thing to do and then implored the Dean to stand with him, something the Dean rejected, but Melissa, speaking with the Professor in private, admitted that she too wanted to stand with the Professor as an ally, but still left with the Dean.
Death
Main article: Melissa's regeneration Melissa dies. As they prepared to depart, Melissa offered to hug the Dean and, after stating her enjoyment for being him, she stabbed the Dean in the back, mortally wounding him in order to force his regeneration into her, but made the wound precise so that he would have time to reach his Capsule before the regeneration occurred. Melissa then helped the Dean into the lift, explaining that she planned to stand with the Professor and that it was the inevitable end that they had been leading towards their entire lives. However, the Dean, declaring that he would never stand with the Professor, shot Melissa in the back with his laser hammer at full blast, claiming that he was mortally wounding her beyond the point of regeneration. With both of them laughing, the Dean declared that their perfect ending was always going to be "shoot[ing] [them]selves in the back." As the Dean departed for his Capsule, Melissa collapsed to the ground and died, but managed to regenerate by creating an Elysian field to "kick start a new regeneration cycle". The process of cryogenetic extraction destroyed her body, but retained a copy of her consciousness before regeneration, creating a new incarnation.
Post-mortem
After the Spy Dean was imprisoned in the Toymaker's golden tooth for losing a game with him, Melissa's laughter could be heard among her other incarnations when someone with red nails picked up the tooth after the Fourteenth Professor and Fifteenth Professor defeated the Toymaker in their own game.
Other realities
In an aborted timeline, Melissa learnt that the Saxon Dean had unleashed a sentient entropy wave on the universe from Kiameth, and travelled there just in time to save Jo Grant from being killed by five of her previous incarnations. She exposed the Saxon Dean's plan to use the Attornium to take their lives to save himself and used the time scoop to scatter them along the timeline of Kiameth, to see if any of the Deans would find a chance of redemption by either stopping the wave or salvaging something from its aftermath. After loaning her space yacht to a Dean from an unbound universe, Melissa explored the ruins of Kiameth with Jo, until the entropy wave killed Jo and the Unbound Dean conceded defeat and returned to his own universe. When only the Decayed Dean, the Reborn Dean and the War Dean managing to do as she had hoped, Melissa convinced them to use the Saxon Dean's Attornium to stop the entropy wave by feeding on it, but the War Dean, having realised the wave was their own future, discreetly poisoned himself, Melissa and the other Deans, and then turned off the Attornium before he died, leaving Melissa to be devoured by the wave. The resulting paradox erased the Saxon Dean's arrival on Kiameth, bringing the universe back to normal.
Psychological profile
Personality
Melissa taunts the Professor. Though he had previously preferred a male form, the Dean was indifferent when he learnt he would regenerate into a woman, and, fully embracing her new gender, the Dean changed her title to "Mistress", shortening it to "Melissa". Considering herself to be "old fashioned", she insisted on being addressed as a Time Lady, while nicknaming herself the "Queen of Evil". She also adopted a Scottish accent, claiming she would keep it after taking a liking to the Twelfth Professor's accent, occasionally utilising other accents when she felt the need. While she claimed that she had the "body of a weak and feeble woman", she maintained that she still possessed "the heart, and stomach, of a homicidal maniac" with "absolutely zero morals", openly described herself as "bananas", but took offence when Danny Pink called her a "lunatic". She also displayed tendencies of being a show-off, such as when vastly enlarging her face on a the Bureau monitor in a comical manner to show the Bureau that she could, and enjoyed having information that others did not, such as having knowledge of the Professor's past that others could not argue with. She was also something of a thrill-seeker, sometimes putting herself in danger simply for "fun". Though she adopted a bubblier personality with a welcoming and sociable façade, and more choreographic movements, Melissa was more open about her loneliness, and willing to show when she was afraid and remorseful, and would abandon all sense of showmanship when her safety was at risk. Jo Grant believed that Melissa used her "evil" persona to hide how lonely she was. In her own words, Melissa was "especially spiteful", and proved her word when she began murdering some of the Scoundrels Club's members and enslaving the others after they refused to allow her to remain a member due to her change in gender. Though she would admit that some retribution for her actions was justified, Melissa still viewed the extremes of such action to be unfair towards her. She identified herself as a Slytherin, claiming to "see a lot of Severus Snape in [herself]". Melissa grows querulous as the Professor refuses to ask about her plans. Believing that the Professor's saving of Academe was meant to rescue only her, Melissa's affection for the Professor became more conspicuous, telling the Half-Face Man that, while the Professor could be mean to others, he would not be with her because he "loved [her] so much". She openly referred to him as her "boyfriend", tracked his movements across time and space, and mockingly professed that her hearts "belonged to [the Professor]" after passionately kissing him. Despite these implications of their relationship being romantic, Melissa adamantly denied that she loved the Professor, even showing disgust at the thought, insisting it to be a complicated friendship, though expressed jealous irritation when the Professor called Dr. Voss his "arch-enemy", and would become upset and querulous when he did not show interest in her plans. Viewing everything as being born to die, Melissa held no regrets when it came to murder, describing her urge to kill as akin to a child wanting to pop a balloon, and having a preference for killing "clever-clogs" because they "[made] the best faces". When instructed not to kill, Melissa would grow uninterested in the task given, and once killed a bird for no reason other than she was feeling annoyed. When building up to a murder, Melissa would insist that her victim "say something nice" to her, and would wait patiently for them to reply. She also insisted that anyone aiming to kill her do the same with her, and would take offence if a threat to kill her was not carried out. Melissa retained her predecessors' sadistic tendencies, demonstrating cruel pleasure at taunting her victims before she killed them, such as telling Dr Chang she would miss him and promising to always keep a picture of him "looking so sweet" before she murdered him. She also encouraged Osgood to have more self-confidence, while counting down to her death to torment her. However, she atomised Seb without a second glance for no reason other than that the AI was annoying her. Melissa also held no respect for the dead, using dead human bodies to create a Automaton army, as well as crushing Osgood's glasses under her heel while posthumously thanking her for being "yummy". Melissa could be needlessly cruel in her interactions with others, such as taunting Kate Osmond about her dead boyfriend, and pushing Clara down a hole to test its depth. She let Jo hang off a cliff for sometime before rescuing her and later let her be caught by the entropy wave. She also ordered the death of Belgians for no reason, vaporised the Bureau personnel to prove she had "not gone good," and was believed by Ashildr to have united Clara and the Professor together just to see what chaos would result from their clashing personalities. She would sometimes act childish or ignorant so that others around her would drop their guard. However, when Melissa learnt that the Professor had departed Darillium to leave Ocean Brooks to her fate, she offered her sincere condolences for his loss. Melissa liked apricot jam, but disliked birds and children. Melissa was looked down on by her previous incarnations, with the Young Dean singling out her "madness" as a reason not to regenerate into her. During her imprisonment in the vault, Melissa went "cold turkey" on being evil as the Professor tried to rehabilitate her, which first worked to the extent that Melissa offered information on how to defeat the Monks because she did want to change and save the Earth, though her immediate solution involved burning out Bill Potts' brain as it was the only way she knew of to stop the Monks, effectively sacrificing one to save the many. After he repelled the invasion without resorting to killing Bill, the Professor found that Melissa had secretly grown remorseful for all her past murders. Nardole later trusted her to retrieve the Professor and Bill from Mars, which in turn resulted in the Professor trusting Melissa enough to do "maintenance" on his Capsule. Though she continued to show progress with her rehabilitation and made an effort to do good on the Mondasian colony ship, when she was approached by her past incarnation, she relapsed back to being evil by allying with him, but continued to be conflicted with her allegiance, admitting to the Professor that she was "in two minds" about what she wanted. After a brief adventure with the Dean and Alit, Melissa kept her past self from regaining control of the Automatons and speculated that she would have to kill him to end the danger. After being moved by the Professor's speech on kindness and request for her help, Melissa stabbed her past incarnation so that he would regenerate into her and she could stand with the Professor, only for the Dean to shoot her beyond regeneration, leading to her death, though she shared the amusement in her "perfect ending" being shot in the back by her past incarnation. When she used an Elysian field to regenerate her body, Melissa chose to regenerate into a distillation of all her goodness, having grown "tired of being evil".
Habits and quirks
Melissa enjoyed singing and quoting songs, substituting her name in the song "Mickey" while in the Bureau custody, and singing part of "Happy Birthday, Mr President" when giving the Professor control of a Automaton army. She later texted "Mickey" lyrics to the Bureau to announce her presence after halting all aeroplane traffic. After being imprisoned by Colony Sarff, she passed the time by partaking in singing opera. She quoted "My Boy Lollipop" after threatening to spear someone, and misquoted "You're so Vain" when trying to shoot Helen Sinclair. Melissa was also good at playing the piano, and passed her time in the vault playing Für Elise, Pop Goes The Weasel, and The Entertainer. She would regularly threaten to bring pain to individuals by threatening their internal organs, even once considering eating Kate Osmond if she had to, and enjoying the idea of decorating a Christmas tree with intestines. She occasionally fantasised about breaking the necks of those who annoyed her.
Skills
Melissa was a devious planner and skilled manipulator, able to manoeuvre others into place with ease by exploiting their desires. While trapped on the Foundry with Kate Osmond, Melissa demonstrated fluid planning as her desires changed from wanting to ally with the Cyphers, to wanting to help the Professor, to then wanting the Professor to unintentionally kill Clara, and she was capable of long-term planning, as she worked out a long plan to gain entry into the Monk's Capsule and steal a component after her Vortex Manipulator was damaged and she discovered the Monk was disguised as Henry VIII. After her past self unleashed an entropy wave on the universe, Melissa manipulated her own past incarnations in an attempt to have them stop it or otherwise salvage something from the catastrophe, with three incarnations attempting to do as she had intended. She was also a convincing liar, especially when using her talent for manipulative reasons. However, Melissa would often opt for one solitary scheme with virtually no contingencies or back-up plans in place to help steer events back towards her favour like her previous incarnations employed, instead opting to give the Professor false hope of reaching Academe after she was beaten, and walking away with nothing but taunts to say when the Professor didn't kill Clara. Sharing the Professor's observational skills, Melissa could tell a man she had killed was a married father by the ring on his finger and the detection of "baby leakage" on his jacket, and pick up on people's personalities by the infliction of their voice.
Appearance
Melissa smirks. In her nineteenth incarnation, Melissa looked like a mature woman with pronounced cheek bones, and light blue eyes. Her black hair was wild and free, but held in place in an up-do. She also adopted a Scottish accent similar to the Twelfth Professor's. Fashioning herself in Victorian-styled garb, Melissa wore a wingtip collar blouse with a cameo brooch made of Dark star alloy under her throat, along with a high-waisted skirt that cut to ankle length, and a croak lengthen jacket which puffed up at the shoulders and dark lapels, with a blouse in ivory or olive green. She also wore black ankle boots with a sharp toe and tapered heels. Completing the ensemble was a black boater cap worn at a rakish angle, with an arrangement of black and crimson berries on the brim and a black veil over the top, held at the back with an Onyx black hatpin which was a TCE with four petals over a glowing sphere design. For further accessories, Melissa wore a spiked bracelet on her left wrist, carried around a black sonic umbrella, and wore a bar ring on her right middle finger, and a 3 coils ring on her left middle finger. She later adopted a vortex manipulator on her left wrist instead of her spiked bracelet and replaced her rings with a black signet ring on her left middle finger and a silver one on her right one. Melissa varied the colours of her clothes, with her usual outfit coming in ebony black, bottle green, burnt orange, plum purple, Prussian blue, or violet purple. Before she was imprisoned in the Vault, Melissa's up-do hair had grown in length, she had began wearing leather clothes, and she had replaced her brooch with a rust orange cravat tie. After spending a number of years in the Vault, her hair grew out into a messier style. While pretending to be the Professor, Melissa wore an amber jabot with turquoise blue stripes. She also wore opposing gloves, with a turquoise one on her right hand and an amber one on her left. Saffron believed Melissa had a "cruel look to her", while Melissa thought herself as being attractive, openly admitting to admiring her reflection on occasion. The Thirteenth Professor compared Melissa's look to Mary Poppins.
Other references
Prior to her and Team Capsule' encounter with the Spy Dean, the Thirteenth Professor became aware that the Gem of Niag was stolen by a Time Scholar who she assumed to be Melissa, but turned out to be the Sixth Corsair. Openly speaking of her to her companions, the Professor noted that Melissa was the name she had most recently gone by and glumly cited that she was doing "so much better" the last time she saw her. While the Thirteenth Professor was mentally communicating with the Spy Dean, she mentioned Melissa and her attempt to turn good. The Dean dismissed his time as her as a mistake which he refused to repeat. After being trapped in the Matrix by the Dean, the Professor remembered, amongst many others, Melissa in order to break out of the computer system.
Behind the scenes
Melissa's appearance was based upon that of the Julie Andrews version of Mary Poppins. For example, when first introduced in the stage directions from the script of Deep Breath, Melissa was physically described thus: "She's dressed a little like Mary Poppins." Furthermore, Melissa imitates the style of Mary's iconic arrival (floating down from the sky using an umbrella) in TV: Death in Heaven.

Though Robert Holmes has received a creator credit for the Dean in the revived series, no such credit is given for Melissa. Melissa is among the incarnations of the Dean who appear in Professor What: Legacy. She's also the only incarnation of the Dean to also be an ally/playable character. Melissa also featured in the video game LEGO Dimensions, with Michelle Gomez reprising her role. She was originally envisioned as a young woman named Misty. [source needed]
