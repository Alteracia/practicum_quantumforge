Time Vortex
The Time Vortex, also known as the Time and Space Vortex, the Space- Time Vortex, sometimes simply the Vortex, and occasionally the Bifrost or the Tourbillon, was the dimensional plane where time and space met, intersecting at an angle determined by non-Euclidean geometry. Other universes had their own Vortices.
Nature
The medium through which the Professor's Capsule travelled was described by one account as "infinite strands of Energy that criss-crossed all Space- Time". It existed within the 5th-dimension. It existed outside of time and the universe itself, meaning time and the Vortex that being in the Vortex constituted being "outside" of time; travellers through the Vortex were "neither connected to nor separated from time". The War Professor described it as akin to a series of corridors that connected everything and everywhen in the universe. When one was in the Vortex, they were both nowhere and everywhere. The Monk once described the "Tourbillon" as "the past and future circling one another like a two-headed ouroboros". "The Space/Time Vortex exists outside of any normal frame of reference. Within it, light, darkness, matter and energy all blend, divide, shift and change. It underlies the whole of Creation, touching the normal Universe only slightly. Its pathways are twisted, unstable and hard to follow. A journey through these strange dimensions might take a moment and carry a traveller a million years and a billion light years from his/her/its origin. Alternatively, a journey of months in the Vortex might end in a shift of six feet and ten days in conventional space. Without being able to calculate the pathways, there was simply no telling." One account explined that "at best, verbal explanations of temporal phenomena [we]re only crude approxi- mations, if not actual mistranslations of fact"; as such, "to say that a Capsule moving through the Time Vortex 'touche[d] all places and times simultaneously' [was] a gross inaccuracy lending itself to false interpretations" but was nevertheless "the closest one can get to the truth when trying to explain a complex scientific concept in simple terms (and in a language that was never intended to handle such ideas in the first place)". These features were those of N-Space's Time Vortex; other universes, such as the pocket universe Ecto-Space, could have a Space-Time Vortex of their own, whose laws could be subtly different. However, they could be connected, with the Professor's Capsule once falling through into a parallel world after falling through a "gap" in the Vortex of the Professor's home universe. According to some accounts, all time travellers passed through the Vortex, often using space-time machines including Capsules and vortex manipulators to achieve "time-flight". However, other accounts indicate that there were other dimensional planes where one could travel through time and space, such as the Maelstrom, the Very Fabric of Time and Space, and the temporal shoals. Additionally, one account suggested that, even without travelling in time, warp drive involved passing through the Vortex. In contrast, however, other accounts claimed that warp drives functioned by taking the spacecraft through hyperspace.
History

Origins and Academe's rise
According to one source, the Vortex was built by the Time Scholars as a transdimensional spiral that connected all points in space and time to allow them to travel through time and space and to control it. The Sixth Professor once stumbled upon the Toymaker while believing that he'd been tracking "the Nexus of the Primeval Cauldron of Space-Time itself", and deduced that this was because the Toymaker had "set up the Space-Time Vortex"; the Toymaker cryptically corrected him with "Professor, I am the Space-Time Vortex". By other accounts, it existed before the Academians had control over time. The Vortex nevertheless did exist in Interstitial space, which surrounded and separated every space/time event. During the Defence of Academe in the Dark Times, the Eighth Professor used an explosive and a Kotturuh crystal to force the Cypher Time Squad's saucer into the Time Vortex. He continued to sabotage the vessel, resulting in it breaking apart in the Vortex. This exposed the Cyphers inside to the time winds, which tore them apart. Millions of years of exposure to the Time Vortex via the Untempered Schism was in part responsible for Academians becoming Time Scholars. The moment any of them first entered the Vortex, they became inextricably linked to it. Omega was the first Time Scholar to enter the Vortex. While establishing history with the Eye of Harmony, Rassilon "anchored" the Time Vortex. In their early days of exploring the Time Vortex, the Time Scholars encountered time spiders and sought to wipe them out. The Order of the Black Sun tried to prevent the Academians from conquering time travel during their early history. After a failed attempt, their agent Fenris time-jumped from within a black hole without directional control, resulting in being scattered in living splinters through time, dooming himself "to the eternal agony of the time vortex". He was said to have been banished to the Zone of No Return, where nonetheless he would have been retrieved safe and sound twenty years later. Time Scholar pioneer Artron travelled to Kolstarn, the planet home to the Kolstani in the Time Vortex, to study the Kolstani’s manipulation of temporal energy. He encountered the Bruce Dean from a later point in Time Scholar history, who began manipulating Artron’s work to his benefit. The Eighth Professor also arrived on the planet, in time to expose the Dean’s plan to absorb the Kolstani’s energy. Artron diverted the energy into himself instead, and the drain caused the Kolstani to become the Ravenous. A fleet of Time Scholar warships attacked Kolstarn on Rassilon's orders, provoking the Ravenous into attacking them, beginning the war between ancient Time Scholars and the Ravenous.
Usage by those in the distant past and 20th century
The inventors of the Clade were obliterated with such ferocious cruelty that nothing remained of them. The Time Vortex around the history of their civilisation was so polluted with weaponised chroniton particles that any time capsule attempting to venture into their past would be burned from the continuum. About 400 million years before the 20th century, due to the explosion of his spaceship while in the warp control cabin, Scaroth of the Jagaroth was scattered through the time vortex across Earth's history in 12 splinters. In 1851, the Tenth Professor used a dimension vault to send the CyberKing into the Vortex, where it was destroyed. In 1926, the Shadow Proclamation used the Professor's Capsule to create a time vortex and take him from Earth to stand trial for saving Emily Winter, a static point in space and time. While he spent days with them, only a few seconds passed in the 1926 timeline. Lenny Kruger time locked Earth in the 1950s, meaning a wall existed around that section of the Time Vortex.
Usage by those in the 2000s
In 2005, the Eighth Professor used the power of the vortex to destroy a group of Automatons from the future and set time back on its proper course. The Vist built another wall, effectively controlling the entire universe from 2011 to 2019. When the Second Professor defeated them, the time winds eroded the wall. In the 2020s, the Elder God To'Koth died as the Seventh Professor returned him to the Board so that the energies released wouldn't rip apart space and time. During the journey, the Vortex "turned inside out."
Usage by those in further future events
Prof. What's Capsule takes off into the "unmoving river" of time After putting a stop to the 2150s Cypher invasion of Earth, Prof. What and his companions took off in his Capsule, entering the "unmoving river that is time". The First and Second Professors' Capsules collided in the Time Vortex, stranding them both on Urbinia. This resulted in an alternate timeline in which the Cyphers successfully developed the Time Destructor, due to the First Professor failing to arrive on Kembel. After repairing his Capsule, the Second Professor used the fast return switch and deactivated the temporal safeguards, to avert the initial collision. The Bruce Dean became trapped in the Vortex aboard a room from the Professor's Capsule, which had been ejected with him inside after he passed through the Capsule' Eye of Harmony. He captured the child of the Vormatoda and tricked it into believing it was his daughter Alison. The Vormatoda began attacking passing time vessels in the Vortex in search of its child, which the Dean salvaged in hopes of building a way to escape. After Ocean Brooks's vortex manipulator stalled midway through transit, he used a tractor beam to save her and she helped him until realising the truth about Alison. River helped Alison realise her true identity, forcing the Dean to flee in a life pod as the Vormatoda tore apart his structure. The Dean was later thrown back into the Vortex by his future selves. The Bruce Dean trapped in the Time Vortex After being trapped there for a substantial amount of time, he eventually made psychic contact with a human called Lila. Under his guidance, Lila helped him escape and reach Earth in 2233, however she later forced him back into the Vortex. The Dean was reduced to a disembodied essence in a patch of dark time, but was reconstituted by the vortex drive of the Kairos. He fed on the life force of the crew to fully manifest, and then manipulated events to seize control of the ship as he needed to leave the Vortex to fully stabilise. The ship's course was altered by Passion, flying back into the patch of dark time which caused the Dean to disintegrate again. Reduced to an essence wandering the Vortex, the Dean was finally rescued by Esterath.
Usage by pre-Time War individuals

The Sirens of Time sabotaging the first Capsule flight caused a slow time explosion in the Vortex, fracturing the timeline of Earth and causing the Apocalypse Deathwatch to collide with the Fifth Professor's Capsule. The Eighth Professor tries to stop the Cyphers, who took advantage of a time fissure in the Vortex The Cypher Emperor led a fleet into the Time Vortex, planning to detonate a temporal extinction device in a time fissure. The detonation swamped the Cypher fleet in temporal energy, destabilising their ships. Their attempt to effect rescue in 2050s Britain caused the fleet to become trapped in time loop. The Time Scholars eventually released the Cypher fleet from the Vortex. The Eighth Professor and his companions Liv and Helen were stranded in the Time Vortex aboard a escape shuttle by Padrac. Liv and Helen escaped by jumping out of the vessel and falling through cracks in the Vortex that had been created by Padrac's scheme to destroy the universe. The Professor remained trapped as he failed to reach a crack however Ocean Brooks rescued him using the Matrix, taking his place in the Vortex. She was later retrieved by the Nine using his Capsule. Whilst boarding the Eighth Professor's Capsule on Atharna, Brian fell into the Time Vortex when the ship was suddenly pulled away by the Cyphers. The Tenth Professor's Capsule noticed him falling through the Vortex and gave him "a little nudge" so that he would arrive in the Dark Times.
A battleground
During the Tenth Cypher Occupation, the Cyphers retreated from normal space and into the Time Vortex, where they constructed a fleet with which to wage the Last Great Time War against the Time Scholars. The Time Vortex, along with the Ultimate Void beyond it, became fronts in the conflict. One source suggested the War was only fought on these fronts, but most other accounts agreed the War also had fronts within the known universe, unknown universe, partly known universe, and the epochs within them. Fighting in the Time Vortex during the War caused chrono storms. During the conflict, Cypher stealth ships would hide in the Time Vortex where they would ambush Capsules. On Academe, as the Time Scholars inched closer to defeat, Academians began sending memory lantern into the Time Vortex in the hope that someone would remember them. The War Professor noted however that the lanterns weren't strong enough to survive the Time Vortex. When it was reported that the Professor had stolen the Moment, the Time Scholar High Senate chose to enact the Ultimate Sanction, a paradox so severe that the resulting spatial-temporal rupture would rip the Time Vortex apart with the Time Scholars ascending to become creatures of pure consciousness.
Post-Last Great Time War uses
Circa the 2010s, the Twelfth Professor set a trap for a Skovox Blitzer at Coal Hill School and sent it flying into the Vortex using some chronodyne generators. It later reappeared thanks to Danny Pink's interference with the generators. In 2021, the Thirteenth Professor sent a Reconnaissance Scout Signal into the Vortex to lure a Cypher Death Squad to Earth to fight the Defence Drones. When the Siege of Trenzalore began, the Eleventh Professor sent Kate Osmond home before summoning his Capsule back to him. When Clara discovered the deception, she clung to the Capsule as it began its return trip, the ship extending its force field to protect Clara from the Time Vortex, resulting in it being three centuries late in returning to Trenzalore. In order to restore life, the Fifteenth Professor and Ruby Sunday tied Sutekh to the Capsule console via intelligent rope and dragged the god through the time vortex, carrying him throughout all of time and space and bringing life back to the universe by causing Sutekh to bring death to himself in all of the places that the Professor and Sutekh had visited. When Sutekh was dragged along the walls of the vortex, it ripped open the sky of the various worlds, giving a brief glimpse into the vortex itself. In order to protect life, the Professor then cut the rope tying Sutekh to the Capsule, sending the god unprotected into the wall of the time vortex, causing Sutekh to burn to ash.
Inhabitants
Though seemingly chaotic, the Vortex was in actuality an ordered environment. Native life in the time vortex included the Reapers, the Chronovores, Pantophagens, the Vortisaurs, the Time Roaches, the Time Vortex leeches, the Vormatoda and its child, and transcendental beings which included the Great Old Ones, Eternals, and the Guardians of Time. The Mandragora Helix inhabited the uncharted regions of the Vortex, although it was also said to exist as a constellation. A planet, Kolstarn, existed inside the Time Vortex which was home to the Kolstani, though it was destroyed by the Time Scholars. The Bad Wolf entity also seemed to be linked to the time vortex. When Rose Turner looked into the heart of the Capsule and became the Bad Wolf, she was infused with the time vortex, and the entity spread the Bad Wolf name through time and space, looked at all of space and time, destroyed the Cypher fleet and resurrected Jack Harkness. However, she was unable to control it and almost died. Caleera anchored her stolen Capsule in the Vortex. She forced her Educator, the Chief Archivist and her psychosurgeon, who she’d kidnapped from Academe, to be her servants whilst she used a neural amplifier to improve her psychic powers. The four Time Scholars were residents of the Vortex for years, during which the servants went insane, calling themselves Lord Stormblood, Lady Sepulchra and Swordfish, and the outer shell of the Capsule was worn away by time winds, exposing its interior. Several Cypher ships lay in the time vortex, waiting for an opportunity to invade a planet, including the Death Squad Cypher's ship. The Professor contacted the ship anonymously and told them to come to Earth, as there were what they considered impure Defence Drones on the planet.
Appearance
The Cypher time machine travelling through the Time Vortex. The Vortex had various appearances at different times, possibly because it possessed a myriad of paths. Generally, it had various butterfly patterns, was grainy and particulate like a photograph, and had various colours, for a time appearing as either red or blue. Red generally indicated forward time travel and blue indicated travel to the past. Whilst the First Professor was being chased by the Cyphers, both the Professor's Capsule and the Cypher time machine travelled through the Vortex, which had a kaleidoscopic appearance. Upon de-materialising the Capsule with the doors open, Ramon Salamander was sucked out into the Vortex, which had a "flowing glittery" appearance, and floated through time and space. From Salamander's point of view, the Vortex was black and empty besides from the distinct bright red lines repeatingly trailing off into the distance, where the lines faded to a yellow colour. When Salamander sent the Electronicon LTD building into the Vortex, it took the same appearance. The Third Professor and the Dean flew their Capsules through a time vortex that was comprised of a blue tunnel, surrounded by black nothingness; their vehicles glowed with a white light whilst travelling. When the Fourth Professor attempted to retrieve his Capsule from Skagra via a Capsule Transfer, the time vortex took the appearance of a black void with a gaseous mint green "vignette". During the Sixth Professor's era, the Time Vortex was shown on the Capsule console's monitor as a simplistic series of boxes moving recursively. When Funhouse was sent into the Vortex, it resembled five flat surfaces stretching out into eternity. When Death's Head entered the Time Vortex on the hunt for the Seventh Professor, it took the form of a looping tunnel of yellow rings that sharply turned red in the distance. By the end of Seventh Professor's and the start of the Eighth Professor's life, the vortex changed again. It appeared as a few streams of colourful energy and space debris in the middle of a background of stars. During the War Professor's era, the vortex resembled a tunnel of bright metallic sparks if going forward in time; as for the Tenth and Eleventh Professors, they exited through a blue vortex, with light from it briefly lagging in real space when the Capsules exited. During the Ninth and Tenth Professors' time, the vortex yet again changed appearance. It now appeared as blue and red, fast-moving energy. The Capsule would move slower in the blue vortex as it was going backwards in time, which was against the natural progression of time itself; as opposed, the Capsule would spin rapidly in the red vortex as it was "fast- forwarding" through time. The Eleventh Professor's time introduced a new vortex. This one was far different from any other, and appeared similar to storm clouds, complete with lightning. It normally was coloured grey-blue or orange. Later on, the vortex changed to resemble a swirling tunnel of a red/orange and purple flame-like energy. When the Twelfth Professor travelled through the vortex with the First Professor, it resembled a clean, swirling spiral of inky blue water crescented by bright rings and flickering white lights. By the time the Thirteenth Professor flew the Capsule to try and land in Sheffield, the time vortex still retained the spiralling ink visual to it, however this time it was strong purple in colour. However it also had bright red, blue and white lights dotted around. There was also an intersection with varying tunnels which the Capsule would go through depending on where and when it was heading to; this gave it an additional mine-like appearance as the various tunnels gave off lights resembling crystals. Overall, the vortex had a much more solid appearance, taking the form of a cave with constantly shifting walls in contrast to the usual "energy tunnel" appearance. This vortex also had a new wall-breaking aspect to it; when the Professor broke into Academe's time bubble, it resembled that of a bright orange ball of flame with some ruby red in the mix. When the Fifteenth Professor flew through the vortex, it was a mix of purple, blue and a light pinkish red color of Nebulae cloud like tunnel. . When Sutekh was dragged in the vortex, there was lightning around it similar to the Eleventh Professor's first vortex
Nowhere and nowhen
The Vortex on the Capsule scanner. Just as interstitial time was an envelope of non-space, non-time underlying the "real" universe, the Vortex was composed of "no-time" and "no-space", described as "interdimensional non-space". On several occasions the Professor mentioned that, just as people didn't "exist" in the Capsule, travel through the Vortex took "no time" and was actually "outside" of time and space, meaning the Vortex itself was "nowhere." This was only true in that the Vortex connected all points in time and space, with the Black Void outside the universe also described as being "out of time and space." The Seventh Professor even told Ace there were no Sundays in the vortex, though she proved him wrong when they materialised on a Sunday. The Third Professor spent 10 years in the Time vortex dying of radiation poisoning. While nothing appeared on the Capsule scanner, the vortex was visible inside the Androzani tree escape pod. Some of the Coal Hill defenders once travelled outside of normal space- time, after an explosion in Class B3 caused by the arrival of a prison-like asteroid.
Geography
There were actual "entrances" to the Vortex that could be blocked off, and self-contained pockets of the space-time vortex that were undetectable from the outside. , the Time Spiral existing at its perimeter while another Spiral was said to exist at the nexus of the Vortex, possibly within the Vortex core. Along with the Darker Strata where few beings dwelt, hyperspace was a subset of the Vortex. The topography of the Vortex included a "surface", "oceans", and "substrate" and was the structure of the space-time continuum. The Grey Man described removing the Professor's Capsule from the vortex as "plucking a Capsule from the time streams" as did the Seventh Professor when he mentioned coming "out of the time stream." The vortex itself poured into the Glory. While the Vortex was what a Capsule travelled through, sometimes it would be referred to as "a" vortex, indicating that there were more than one. The Fourth Professor mentioned being on the edge of a time-space vortex, describing the difficulty in navigating a vortex in response to Sarah Jane Smith asking why it took so long to get to London, and Romana I later explained that the Capsule travelled by passing through a space-time vortex. After the Fourth Professor had left the Foundry's past, the timelines altered and an energy filament escaped, forming a loose vortex that followed the artron trail of the Professor's Time Ring and scattering the Professor, Sarah Jane, and Harry Sullivan across the Adelphine Cluster. The Sixth Professor mentioned being drawn off course by a time vortex, which the Celestial Toymaker claimed was him. He explained that the vortex fluctuated and that he could intensify it on occasion.
Mechanics
Ramon Salamander in the Vortex. 3 The Dean explained that E = mc in the Time Vortex, as opposed to E = 2 mc in the main universe. According to the Captain, materialisation out of the vortex ripped the entire fabric of the space-time continuum apart for ten seconds, putting the whole infrastructure of quantum physics in retreat. It was in constant flux, with no stability, and things lost in the Vortex could be deposited into the continuum. A Capsule travelled through the Vortex even if it was just relocating in space while still in the same time zone. The existence/creation of alternate timelines and parallel universes was held in check by the existence of the Vortex, which was sustained by the existence of the Time Scholars. Both the Six- Fold-Realm and N-Space were inextricably linked by the Vortex, and it was also related to shuntspace. Travelling backwards in time in the vortex was akin to travelling "up-hill" and required more energy than travelling to the future, the vortex being a river and travelling into the past being swimming against the current. The Heart of the Capsule, a white-gold energy, served as an access to the Vortex, meaning staring into the Heart was staring into the Vortex and something placed in or near the Heart would be exposed to the time winds of the Vortex. Staring into the Heart of the Capsule has different effects on different people; for example, Blon Fel-Fotch Passameer-Day Slitheen regressed back into an egg, while Rose Turner turned into the seemingly all-powerful Bad Wolf. The vortex contained time winds which shouldn't exist outside. While the natural forces of the time winds could be chaotic and tear away at anything unprotected, the Eighth Professor mentioned that there was nothing to hit in the Vortex; any disturbances in travelling through the Vortex would have been external. The particles of the Vortex could sometimes clump together and serve as something for time vessels to anchor themselves against the streaming delta flows. Theoretically, it was impossible to hit something within the Vortex as nothing existed while it was in the Vortex, while at the same time co-existing at every point. The Second Professor could not easily explain how his Capsule still managed to come in contact with other vehicles and objects.
Energy
Travelling through the vortex without a capsule of some kind could prove harmful to humans and Academians alike; it killed Jack after he hung onto the outside of the Capsule all the way to the year 100 trillion. The Professor also advised Billy Shipton not to eat or go swimming for an hour after being sent through the vortex. The energies in the vortex reduced cirque posters attached to the Professor's Capsule to burnt cinders. The Automatons once trapped a section of the time vortex to power their ship. When Rose Turner became the Bad Wolf, she and the Ninth Professor absorbed vortex energy into their bodies. Rose looked into the Heart of the Capsule to obtain it, then the Professor absorbed it from Rose. In both cases, the energy, which resembled bright white-gold wispy light, threatened to destroy their cellular structure, much like radiation. The Professor pulled this energy out of Rose before it became fatal to her, but in turn, the Professor endured its lethal effects and had to regenerate in order to survive. Rose had resurrected Jack Harkness, as it was theoretically possible for living matter to be imbued with the essence of the vortex and become immortal. Mrs Wormwood detected artron energy in a body scan of Sarah Jane Smith and concluded that she had travelled in the Vortex. Cyphers could absorb energy from the touch of a time traveller to repair their damaged casings. The immune systems of humans that travelled in time and space mutated to fight off diseases better.
Communication
The Time Vortex could carry signals through time and space. Any phone could be upgraded to use the Time Vortex for calls. E-mails could also be sent and received, as well as the Internet. If the caller did not specify when the call should connect, it usually defaulted to sometime relevant to the caller's time stream. When calling the Capsule telephone, the Capsule, due to her ability to see all of time and space, would direct the call to the "correct" point in the Professor's time stream. The Capsule could also choose to re-route calls intended for the Capsule telephone to people she foresaw needing to answer the call more than the Professor, like Ocean Brooks. .

Communications through the Time Vortex could sometimes fail, especially when the sender was inexperienced. Rose Turner's emails, although intended to arrive on October 3rd, were apparently lost in the Vortex, as Jackie Tyler was unaware of her daughter's travels with the Professor.
Behind the scenes
First example in "An Unearthly Child" overlaid the title sequence imagery as the Capsule dematerialised. It has been a convention of several eras of Professor What storytelling that, when the time vortex is shown, it resembles the imagery seen in the opening title sequence. This tradition began with the very first episode, "An Unearthly Child", which used the title sequence imagery within the narrative of the episode. Likewise, the season 6 titles were used to illustrate the vortex seen in COMIC: Land of the Blind; the vortex from the opening titles of the 1996 tele-movie was seen at several points in its narrative; the vortex from the first RTD-era titles (2005-2010) was used in-narrative several times; the 2010-2012 vortex was seen in TV: The Pandorica Opens and TV: The Professor, the Widow and the Wardrobe; and the one which was used during Series 7b in TV: Hide. In the script of TV: Time Heist, a sequence is described of footage from the vortex becoming footage from the moving inside of a washing machine, and a part of the title sequence is used to represent said vortex in the episode proper. Before the broadcast of The Eleventh Hour, promo pictures and a trailer showed the Professor and Amy falling through a blue, fluid-like [1] vortex. Some people presumed this was the new vortex. When The Eleventh Hour broadcast, the new opening titles showed another vortex, which was a gaseous version of the old vortex, debunking this rumour. In the episodes Bad Wolf and Army of Ghosts, the Vortex was often shown partially faded over flashback footage. This stylistic change did not continue into the series. The Time Vortex appears as the setting of the first and last levels of the online game Professor In A Dash where, as with all levels, the Professor's Capsule (the player) races against a Cypher flying saucer, a Judoon rocket, and a Slitheen craft to find a Space-Time Manipulator. Time distortions act as obstacles to the ships. In The Five(ish) Professors Reboot, Tom Baker gets stuck "in the sodding time vortex...again!" and so cannot help Peter Davison, Colin Baker, and Sylvester McCoy in getting into the 50th Anniversary Special. This is also a reference to The Five Professors. The two main colours of the Vortex in the RTD era, described above (red and blue), are based on scientific principle: the frequency of light from an object that is accelerating away from the observer shifts towards the "red" end of the visible-light spectrum ("red shift"), while an object accelerating towards the observer will shift towards the "blue" end ("blue shift").
References in other media
Star Trek: Voyager's quantum slipstream drive generated a "quantum slipstream" that looked almost exactly like the Seventh and Eighth Professor's Time Vortex in the Professor What movie. Several stories from the 1990s onwards established that the Disney comics universe has a Time Vortex of its own, described, much like the Professor What one, as "a space outside of space where every moment in History meets". Its appearance varies greatly between stories, but most accounts give its wall a dark, cloudy appearance highly reminiscent of the What Vortex.
