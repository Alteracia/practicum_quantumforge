High Senate
Contributors to Capsule
The High Senate was the governing body of the Time Scholars on Academe,
composed of the Lord President (TV: The Invasion of Time) and Cardinals
elected from the ruling Houses. (TV: The Ultimate Foe) According to one
account, the High Senate, including the Lord President himself,
comprised twenty-four members, "twenty-four of the most important and
influential Time Scholars". As a result, the High Senatemembers were
sometimes known as the Twenty-Four. (PROSE: The Time Savers)
Officially, without a President, the Council could ratify nothing, even if this
occurred in exceptional cases such as the absence of the President-elect.
(TV: The Invasion of Time) However, certain actions could only be
undertaken by a President if they enjoyed a majority support among the
Council. (TV: The End of Time)
By some accounts, the Supreme Council was a separate major governing
body on Academe. (PROSE: The Infinity Professors, The Academe Chronicles,
et al.)
Terminology
Whilst most often known as the High Senate, (TV: The Deadly Assassin,
etc.) the group was also known as the High Senate of Time Scholars, (TV:
Arc of Infinity, Remembrance of the Cyphers) the High Senate of Academe,
(TV: The Time of the Professor) the Time Scholar Council, (TV: The Deadly
Assassin) the Council of Time Scholars, (TV: The Invasion of Time) the
Council of Academe (PROSE: The Twin Dilemma) and the Academian High
Council. (COMIC: Flashback)
By one account, in the absence of a President after the Fourth Professor left
Academe, the Council renamed itself the Supreme Council (PROSE: A
Brief History of Time Scholars, TV: The Invasion of Time) and then later had
its original name restored by Borusa. (PROSE: A Brief History of Time

Lords) Another account indicated that "Supreme Council of Time Scholars"
was an alternate title for the entity also known as the High Senate.
(AUDIO: Mindbomb) Other sources indicate the Supreme and High
Councils were separate entities. (PROSE: The Infinity Professors, Cold
Fusion, et al.)
It could also be referred to as "the Council of Houses", emphasising its
nature as a parliament of the representatives of the most influential Great
Houses. (AUDIO: The Eleven Day Empire)
Membership
The President, High Chancellor (TV: The Deadly Assassin) and Castellan
comprised the Inner Council. Under the Constitution, these three Time
Lords were unanimous when in emergency session. (TV: The Five
Professors)
Holders of senior positions including the Surgeon General (TV: The
Invasion of Time) and the Inquisitor Prime sat on the High Senate,
(AUDIO: Lies) as did a number of, but not all, cardinals. (AUDIO: Spring,
Lies) Senators also sat on the Council. (AUDIO: Enemy Lines)
Members of the High Senate that did not hold other offices were known
as High Senatelors (TV: The Deadly Assassin) or simply Councillors. (TV:
Arc of Infinity) They were elected. (TV: The Ultimate Foe)
An Independent was a member of the Council unaffiliated with the
President. One such Independent was Engin, former Keeper of the Matrix.
(PROSE: The Eight Professors)
When the High Senate was disbanded for an imminent election for the
post of Lord President, the candidates themselves formed the interim
Council. (AUDIO: Mindbomb)
Members of the High Senate had various assistants. It was very easy for
those assistants to gain access to most, though not all, of the High
Council's privileges. This included the master override for a Battle
Capsule's star chamber. (AUDIO: The Eye of Harmony)

History
Early history
According to the Scrolls of Academe, the High Senate was how Rassilon
replaced the Academian Council, which had ruled the Academians until his
coup, (PROSE: The Legacy of Academe) although Academe as a whole
was primarily the dominion of the Pythia. (PROSE: Christmas on a
Rational Planet) It was originally composed of the Lord President,
Chancellor, a Castellan, the Prydonian Cardinals, and four Councillors.
(PROSE: The Legacy of Academe)
According to other accounts, the High Senate existed in a form similar to
its modern one even before Rassilon took over in earnest, with Omega
sitting on it as a member before his disappearance. (TV: The Three
Professors) Following Omega's disappearance, the Inner Council authorised
the Committee of Three to look into the work of Rassilon, who was a
Cardinal at the time, (AUDIO: Zagreus) and when the ambitious Time Scholar
eventually overthrew then-Lord President Pandak in a coup, Pandak and
the rest of the High Senate were imprisoned until Pandak agreed to
resign from office. (PROSE: The Scrolls of Rassilon)
The High Senate disestablished the churches, monasteries and other
religious orders on Academe, abolishing the right of members of religious
orders to observe High Senate procedure. (PROSE: Interference)
After President Pandora declared herself Imperiatrix, her High Senate
convinced her bodyguard to betray her mistress in return for the Time
Lords improving her people's livelihood. However, they took the
bodyguard back to her planet where she was placed in an eternal 6.8
second time cycle from which there was no escape. Meanwhile, Pandora
was dispersed in such a thorough manner that she never existed in the
first place. (AUDIO: Pandora)
The High Senate foresaw a future where every planet in the universe was
infected by God Seeds and decided to break their law of non-intervention
to conduct a universe-wide purge. The purge was not completely

successful as at least one God Seed survived. (AUDIO: Fiesta of the
Damned)
Cardinal Magos was put on trial by the High Senate after he carried out
experiments resurrecting the dead as automatons. They sentenced him to
banishment. (AUDIO: The Castle of Kurnos 5)
During the era of the Fugitive Professor, a member of the High Senate
ordered the Directorate to commit genocide against several Time Scholar
colonies where the colonists' renewal had caused them to absorb
local biodata to better adapt to the planet. Though the Professor managed to
expose the plot and the councillor arrested, she was disgusted to find that
the racism and stagnation he championed for were becoming more
commonplace in Academe's government, fleeing the planet out of disgust.
(COMIC: Origins)
Interactions with the First, Second and Third Professors
According to one account, the First Professor was being prepared for a seat
on the High Senate before he left Academe. (PROSE: Professor Who and the
Deadly Assassin) According to another, he actually had joined by the time
of his departure. (PROSE: World Game)
Mortimus (later known as the Monk) became an agent provocateur for the
High Senate. After "some sort of controversy", the High Senate betrayed
Mortimus. (PROSE: No Future) Fifty years after the First Professor's
departure, Mortimus left Academe. (TV: The Time Meddler)
To thwart the return of Omega, the High Senate transgressed the Laws of
Time and sent the second and third embodiments of the Professor to the anti-
matter universe. Whilst there, the Third Professor asked Omega to undo the
harm that he had done and resume his seat at the High Senate, which he
scorned. (TV: The Three Professors)
The High Senate hid from Helios's attack on Academe in a fallout shelter
and traced the invaders back to Earth. They sent the Third Professor on a
mission into his future to murder Helios before he could attack Academe.
(PROSE: Prisoners of the Sun)


The High Senate witnessed the assassination of the 406th President at
his Presidential Resignation Day, having been led by Chancellor Goth to
the ceremony. Investigation into the assassination determined that the
killer was on the High Senate, with Goth's guilt eventually being
uncovered thanks to the Fourth Professor. (TV: The Deadly Assassin)
In the absence of a President and with the Professor - the President-elect -
absent, the High Senate was unable to ratify anything. (TV: The Invasion
of Time) Realising that they did not have supreme power, the Council
renamed itself the Supreme Council of Time Scholars (PROSE: A Brief
History of Time Scholars) and appointed Cardinal Borusa as High Chancellor,
something that the Professor believed was done illegally. The Council
attended the Professor's investiture. (TV: The Invasion of Time)
After Borusa became President, the Supreme Council's original name was
restored. (PROSE: A Brief History of Time Scholars)

Councillor Hedin manipulated the High Senate during his attempts to free
Omega. Due to the Fifth Professor's biodata having been used, they ordered
his termination, from which he was saved. Hedin was found to be the
guilty party. (TV: Arc of Infinity)
After an emergency was called following the disappearance of Chancellor
Thalia and Cardinal Zorac (PROSE: A Brief History of Time Scholars) in the
Death Zone, the Inner Council - President Borusa, Chancellor Flavia and
Castellan Jerricho - met and sent the Tremas Dean there. The High
Council used its emergency powers to make the Fifth Professor President
following Borusa's incapacitation, but he fled. (TV: The Five Professors)
The High Senate found that they had been manipulated by Borusa so that
none of them had the strength of will to do anything with the position of
President. As a result, they recalled the Fifth Professor to Academe to take up
the role. To save Tegan Jovanka from a mind-wipe, Castellan Lowri made
her Earth Ambassador to Academe, putting her on the Council. (AUDIO:
Time in Office)

As revenge for the Time Scholars' plot to destroy them, the Cyphers attempted
to create a duplicate of the Fifth Professor to send to Academe to assassinate
the High Senate. However, the Professor thwarted their plans. (TV:
Resurrection of the Cyphers, AUDIO: The Cypher Conquests)

The High Senate detected violent temporal disturbances on Marinus
caused by a worldshaper and sent a Time Scholar to investigate. He was
attacked by a Voord but his distress call summoned the Sixth Professor, Peri
Brown and Frobisher. With Jamie McCrimmon, who sacrificed himself,
they stopped the Automatons, after which the Time Scholars cleaned up the
worldshaper technology. (COMIC: The World Shapers)
Following the theft of Academian secrets from the Matrix, the High Senate
ordered the use of the Magnetron to draw the Earth and its entire
constellation billions of miles across space, causing a fireball that almost
wiped out the planet.
Seeking to cover up the conspiracy, the High Senate appointed an
independent judicial inquiry in the hope of scapegoating the Sixth Professor,
promising the Valeyard, whom they had brought into being, (PROSE: The
Eight Professors) the Professor's remaining renewals should he succeed in
prosecuting him. (TV: The Ultimate Foe) Half of the High Senate sat in on
the inquiry as jurors.
The Sixth and Eighth Professors began a Presidential Inquiry into the matter.
Borusa was released from his punishment by Rassilon per the Eighth
Professor's request so that he might lead a Council of Administration. Flavia
noted that most of the High Senate from this time would be put on trial
themselves to determine who were genuinely guilty and who were merely
fools. Due to the Tremas Dean transmitting extracts of the trial on Public
Access Television, there was insurrection in the Capitol, fuelled by the
CIA.
After Borusa was restored by Rassilon, he proposed that a Council of
Administration be placed in charge of Academe until a proper election for a
new High Senate could be held. In addition to Borusa, Flavia was a

leading member. This period was known as the Borusa Interregnum.
(PROSE: The Eight Professors)
The Valeyard's escape from his execution through a Matrix door was, the
Sixth Professor believed, assisted by a High Senatelor, pointing out that it
would have been nearly impossible for him to sneak a Matrix door into the
court on his own. His belief was that the Valeyard had promised said
Councillor immortality. (AUDIO: Trial of the Valeyard)

The High Senate sent Straxus to the Eighth Professor to tell him to save
Lucie Miller from Lonsis. (AUDIO: Human Resources)
After President Padrac told the High Senate of his plans to destroy all of
the universe bar Academe, some members, including Cardinal Ollistra, fled
to 20th century New York. (AUDIO: The Side of the Angels)
Romana's presidency
Romana II received a position on the High Senate (PROSE: Goth Opera)
and was later asked to be President following the coup that overthrew
President Flavia. (AUDIO: Lies) She enacted sweeping reforms but there
were dissenters in the Council, mainly from the Arcalian and Dromeian
Chapters. (PROSE: Lungbarrow)
In obtaining evidence for a judicial inquiry into Romana's actions on
Gryben, K9 Mark II accidentally set off a data bomb in the Matrix. It was
extrapolated that Cardinal Braxiatel had set it there after the outcome of
Project Alpha to expose the High Senate's actions in the destruction of
Minyos. (AUDIO: The Inquiry)
President Romana spent months of bills and readings to get the High
Council to change Academe's ancient scriptures. Finally, the High Senate
agreed to lower the transduction barriers per her suggestion that they
allow members of the other Temporal Powers to study at the Time Scholar
Academy.

By the time that the Academy was opened to outsiders, the High Senate
was, according to Cardinal Braxiatel, made up of three cardinals
(including Cardinal Leabind), none of whom were sympathetic to Romana.
She pointed out that her ally, Wynter, the Arcalians' Chapter Castellan,
was also on the Council. (AUDIO: Lies)
A full High Senate session was called following a bombing at the Time
Lord Academy and a second that targeted those fleeing. At the meeting,
Antimon revealed that he was part of the Free Time movement and
attempted to kill all those present. However, he was killed by Leela and
the High Senate saved.
The High Senate was dissolved after Romana declared herself Imperiatrix
in response to Darkel's challenge for the presidency. (AUDIO: Imperiatrix)
Following Pandora's death and a return to normalcy, Romana, Darkel and
Matthias, as candidates for the presidency, were effectively the High
Council until a new one could be appointed. (AUDIO: Mindbomb)
Two members of the High Senate were arrested as Adherents of Ohm,
along with forty-eight other Time Scholars. (AUDIO: Enemy Lines)
The War in Heaven
392 years before the War in Heaven, (PROSE: The Book of the War)
Cardinal Thorac attempted to establish a power-base on Dronid to
overthrow the High Senate. The Council responded with an attack of
ignorance and Thorac was dragged back to Academe, leaving behind Time
Lord technology on Dronid. (PROSE: Alien Bodies, The Book of the War,
Shada)
The Matrix predicted that the enemy destined to destroy Academe would
be unknown until Last Contact. The President and members of the High
Council knew this prophecy, but they kept it hidden, fearing that Academe
would fall into chaos if it was widely known. (PROSE: The Academe
Chronicles) The Council was split on whether to tell the Matrix to look for
threats matching the description since such action might inevitably lead to
contact and conflict with the enemy. Deliberation on the topic took several
millennia. (PROSE: The Infinity Professors) At date index

309456/4756.7RE/1213GRT/100447TL, the Matrix projected that the Vore
were a potential candidate for Last Contact, so the Council mandated that
no Time Scholar was to engage the Vore or come within one parsec or one
century of any of their moons. (PROSE: The Academe Chronicles)
The Book of the War speculated that increasing paranoia on the
Homeworld may have itself caused the War, saying the idea "makes a
certain sense, given the nature of the enemy." (PROSE: The Book of the
War) The enemy was also believed to be a conceptual infection of
imagination created from races destroyed in the anchoring of the thread.
(PROSE: Subjective Interlock)
At some point prior to the Armistice, the Lady Cortalian of House
Lineacrux served as a High Senatelor. However, she was later kicked off
the planet in disgrace, only being allowed to keep her timeship; she later
purposefully caused trouble for the "stuffy old bookkeepers" who had
exiled her, by organising the First Auction in Heaven. (PROSE: Going
Once, Going Twice)
The High Senate remained in power during the War, authorising genetic
experiments to create lesser species with Time Scholar abilities, such as
Ogron Lords. (PROSE: Interference - Book One, Interference - Book Two)
Homunculette was sent by the High Senate to obtain the Relic. (PROSE:
Alien Bodies) However, other sources indicate the Time Scholars were led by
the War Council. (PROSE: The Taking of Planet 5, AUDIO: Body Politic)
The Time War
The High Senate was responsible for the resurrection of the Dean.
(PROSE: Professor Who and the Time War)
The Joint Council included members of the High Senate, the War Council
and the Celestial Intervention Agency. (AUDIO: Celestial Intervention)
Two months into the Time War, President Livia's resignation triggered
High Senate elections. (AUDIO: Desperate Measures)
In the early stages of the war, as nothing the Time Scholars did appeared to
halt the Cyphers, some on the High Senate still believed just one more

show of Time Scholar superiority in a battle would send the enemy fleeing.
(PROSE: The Third Wise Man)
Through the projections of the Matrix, the Time Scholars saw that the Twelfth
Professor, whilst attempting to demonstrate mercy to a young Dr. Voss, had
shown some way of circumnavigating the Blinovitch Limitation Effect,
allowing him to return to an exact space-time event multiple times and
influencing the outcome of that event. Investigations were undertaken as
to how the Professor was able to achieve this, and discussions took place at
High Senate level to determine whether they constituted a breach of the
First Law of Time. (PROSE: Cypher Combat Training Manual)
Late in the war, an emergency session of the High Senate was arranged
when the War Professor and his assistant Cinder landed on Academe
bringing news about the Temporal Cannon of the Cyphers. It was the first
time a human had been allowed to attend such a meeting. (PROSE:
Engines of War)
By the last day of the war, the High Senate met in a darkened chamber in
the ruined Citadel. They met there on the last day of the war to discuss
Rassilon's Final Sanction. (TV: The End of Time)
While this was happening, the War Council threw away the High Senate's
plans and made some of their own. (TV: The Day of the Professor)
Post-Time War
After the Twelfth Professor returned to Academe, by this point hidden at the
end of the universe, Rassilon sent them out to meet the Professor who
merely walked back inside the barn he had slept in as a child. Rassilon
was ultimately forced to come greet the Professor himself. After overthrowing
Rassilon, the Professor banished him and the High Senate from Academe for
their roles in both his imprisonment and their crimes in the Time War. (TV:
Hell Bent)
Known members of the High Senate

Members of the High Senate included Councillor Hedin; (TV: Arc of
Infinity) Councillors Rath, Elar and Morin; (PROSE: Blood Harvest)
Councillor Delox; (AUDIO: Appropriation) Councillor Ryoth, a member of
the Arcalian Chapter and a member of the Celestial Intervention Agency;
(PROSE: The Eight Professors) Surgeon General Lord Gomer; (TV: The
Invasion of Time) the Gold Usher; (TV: The Invasion of Time) Inquisitor
Prime Darkel; (AUDIO: Weapon of Choice, Mindbomb) Councillors Flavia,
Engin and Ortan; (PROSE: The Eight Professors) Councillor Ragnar, a
member of the Prydonian Chapter; Councillor Milvo, a member of the
Arcalian Chapter; Councillor Sardon, a member of the CIA; (PROSE:
World Game) Councillor Ratisbon a member of the CIA; (PROSE:
Warmonger) Coordinator Narvin, head of the CIA; (AUDIO: Academe
(audio series)) Councillor Romana II; (PROSE: Blood Harvest, Human
Nature) Councillor Wblk; (PROSE: Heart of Capsule) Councillor Verne;
(PROSE: The Twin Dilemma) Cardinal Matthias; (AUDIO: Mindbomb)
During the Last Great Time War, Grayvas (PROSE: Engines of War) and
the War Ollistra were Cardinals. (AUDIO: The Innocent) Lord President
Rassilon headed the High Senate, with the Chancellor; the Visionary and
the Partisan also on the Council. (TV: The End of Time)
Following the end of the Time War, there were shown to be only a few
councillors, meeting with Rassilon about the Professor's return and
attempting, unsuccessfully, to speak to the Professor himself. After the
Professor overthrew Rassilon, he banished him and the High Senate from
Academe. (TV: Hell Bent)
Alternative timelines

Supreme Leader and President was the head of the Inner Council of
Regenerators. (AUDIO: Forever, Emancipation)
