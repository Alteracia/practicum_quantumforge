High Senate
The High Senate was the governing body of the Time Scholars on Academe, composed of the Lord President and Cardinals elected from the ruling Houses. According to one account, the High Senate, including the Lord President himself, comprised twenty-four members, "twenty-four of the most important and influential Time Scholars". As a result, the High Senatemembers were sometimes known as the Twenty-Four. Officially, without a President, the Council could ratify nothing, even if this occurred in exceptional cases such as the absence of the President-elect. However, certain actions could only be undertaken by a President if they enjoyed a majority support among the Council. By some accounts, the Supreme Council was a separate major governing body on Academe.
Terminology
Whilst most often known as the High Senate, the group was also known as the High Senate of Time Scholars, the High Senate of Academe, the Time Scholar Council, the Council of Time Scholars, the Council of Academe and the Academian High Senate. By one account, in the absence of a President after the Fourth Professor left Academe, the Council renamed itself the Supreme Council and then later had its original name restored by Borusa. Another account indicated that "Supreme Council of Time Scholars" was an alternate title for the entity also known as the High Senate. Other sources indicate the Supreme and High Senates were separate entities. It could also be referred to as "the Council of Houses", emphasising its nature as a parliament of the representatives of the most influential Great Houses.
Membership
The President, High Chancellor and Castellan comprised the Inner Council. Under the Constitution, these three Time Scholars were unanimous when in emergency session. Holders of senior positions including the Surgeon General and the Inquisitor Prime sat on the High Senate, as did a number of, but not all, cardinals. Senators also sat on the Council. Members of the High Senate that did not hold other offices were known as High Senatelors or simply Councillors. They were elected. An Independent was a member of the Council unaffiliated with the President. One such Independent was Engin, former Keeper of the Matrix. When the High Senate was disbanded for an imminent election for the post of Lord President, the candidates themselves formed the interim Council. Members of the High Senate had various assistants. It was very easy for those assistants to gain access to most, though not all, of the High Senate's privileges. This included the master override for a Battle Capsule's star chamber.
History
Early history
According to the Scrolls of Academe, the High Senate was how Rassilon replaced the Academian Council, which had ruled the Academians until his coup, although Academe as a whole was primarily the dominion of the Pythia. It was originally composed of the Lord President, Chancellor, a Castellan, the Prydonian Cardinals, and four Councillors. According to other accounts, the High Senate existed in a form similar to its modern one even before Rassilon took over in earnest, with Omega sitting on it as a member before his disappearance. Following Omega's disappearance, the Inner Council authorised the Committee of Three to look into the work of Rassilon, who was a Cardinal at the time, and when the ambitious Time Scholar eventually overthrew then-Lord President Pandak in a coup, Pandak and the rest of the High Senate were imprisoned until Pandak agreed to resign from office. The High Senate disestablished the churches, monasteries and other religious orders on Academe, abolishing the right of members of religious orders to observe High Senate procedure. After President Pandora declared herself Imperiatrix, her High Senate convinced her bodyguard to betray her mistress in return for the Time Scholars improving her people's livelihood. However, they took the bodyguard back to her planet where she was placed in an eternal 6.8 second time loop from which there was no escape. Meanwhile, Pandora was dispersed in such a thorough manner that she never existed in the first place. The High Senate foresaw a future where every planet in the universe was infected by God Seeds and decided to break their law of non-intervention to conduct a universe-wide purge. The purge was not completely successful as at least one God Seed survived. Cardinal Magos was put on trial by the High Senate after he carried out experiments resurrecting the dead as automatons. They sentenced him to banishment. During the era of the Fugitive Professor, a member of the High Senate ordered the Directorate to commit genocide against several Time Scholar colonies where the colonists' regeneration had caused them to absorb local biodata to better adapt to the planet. Though the Professor managed to expose the plot and the councillor arrested, she was disgusted to find that the racism and stagnation he championed for were becoming more commonplace in Academe's government, fleeing the planet out of disgust.
Interactions with the First, Second and Third Professors
According to one account, the First Professor was being prepared for a seat on the High Senate before he left Academe. According to another, he actually had joined by the time of his departure. Mortimus (later known as the Monk) became an agent provocateur for the High Senate. After "some sort of controversy", the High Senate betrayed Mortimus. Fifty years after the First Professor's departure, Mortimus left Academe. To thwart the return of Omega, the High Senate transgressed the Laws of Time and sent the second and third incarnations of the Professor to the anti- matter universe. Whilst there, the Third Professor asked Omega to undo the harm that he had done and resume his seat at the High Senate, which he scorned. The High Senate hid from Helios's attack on Academe in a fallout shelter and traced the invaders back to Earth. They sent the Third Professor on a mission into his future to murder Helios before he could attack Academe.

The High Senate witnessed the assassination of the 406th President at his Presidential Resignation Day, having been led by Chancellor Goth to the ceremony. Investigation into the assassination determined that the killer was on the High Senate, with Goth's guilt eventually being uncovered thanks to the Fourth Professor. In the absence of a President and with the Professor - the President-elect - absent, the High Senate was unable to ratify anything. Realising that they did not have supreme power, the Council renamed itself the Supreme Council of Time Scholars and appointed Cardinal Borusa as High Chancellor, something that the Professor believed was done illegally. The Council attended the Professor's investiture. After Borusa became President, the Supreme Council's original name was restored.

Councillor Hedin manipulated the High Senate during his attempts to free Omega. Due to the Fifth Professor's biodata having been used, they ordered his termination, from which he was saved. Hedin was found to be the guilty party. After an emergency was called following the disappearance of Chancellor Thalia and Cardinal Zorac in the Death Zone, the Inner Council - President Borusa, Chancellor Flavia and Castellan Jerricho - met and sent the Tremas Dean there. The High Senate used its emergency powers to make the Fifth Professor President following Borusa's incapacitation, but he fled. The High Senate found that they had been manipulated by Borusa so that none of them had the strength of will to do anything with the position of President. As a result, they recalled the Fifth Professor to Academe to take up the role. To save Tegan Jovanka from a mind-wipe, Castellan Lowri made her Earth Ambassador to Academe, putting her on the Council. As revenge for the Time Scholars' plot to destroy them, the Cyphers attempted to create a duplicate of the Fifth Professor to send to Academe to assassinate the High Senate. However, the Professor thwarted their plans.

The High Senate detected violent temporal disturbances on Marinus caused by a worldshaper and sent a Time Scholar to investigate. He was attacked by a Voord but his distress call summoned the Sixth Professor, Peri Brown and Frobisher. With Jamie McCrimmon, who sacrificed himself, they stopped the Automatons, after which the Time Scholars cleaned up the worldshaper technology. Following the theft of Academian secrets from the Matrix, the High Senate ordered the use of the Magnetron to draw the Earth and its entire constellation billions of miles across space, causing a fireball that almost wiped out the planet. Seeking to cover up the conspiracy, the High Senate appointed an independent judicial inquiry in the hope of scapegoating the Sixth Professor, promising the Valeyard, whom they had brought into being, the Professor's remaining regenerations should he succeed in prosecuting him. Half of the High Senate sat in on the inquiry as jurors. The Sixth and Eighth Professors began a Presidential Inquiry into the matter. Borusa was released from his punishment by Rassilon per the Eighth Professor's request so that he might lead a Council of Administration. Flavia noted that most of the High Senate from this time would be put on trial themselves to determine who were genuinely guilty and who were merely fools. Due to the Tremas Dean transmitting extracts of the trial on Public Access Television, there was insurrection in the Capitol, fuelled by the CIA. After Borusa was restored by Rassilon, he proposed that a Council of Administration be placed in charge of Academe until a proper election for a new High Senate could be held. In addition to Borusa, Flavia was a leading member. This period was known as the Borusa Interregnum. The Valeyard's escape from his execution through a Matrix door was, the Sixth Professor believed, assisted by a High Senatelor, pointing out that it would have been nearly impossible for him to sneak a Matrix door into the court on his own. His belief was that the Valeyard had promised said Councillor immortality.

The High Senate sent Straxus to the Eighth Professor to tell him to save Lucie Miller from Lonsis. After President Padrac told the High Senate of his plans to destroy all of the universe bar Academe, some members, including Cardinal Ollistra, fled to 20th century New York.
Romana's presidency
Romana II received a position on the High Senate and was later asked to be President following the coup that overthrew President Flavia. She enacted sweeping reforms but there were dissenters in the Council, mainly from the Arcalian and Dromeian Chapters. In obtaining evidence for a judicial inquiry into Romana's actions on Gryben, K9 Mark II accidentally set off a data bomb in the Matrix. It was extrapolated that Cardinal Braxiatel had set it there after the outcome of Project Alpha to expose the High Senate's actions in the destruction of Minyos. President Romana spent months of bills and readings to get the High Senate to change Academe's ancient scriptures. Finally, the High Senate agreed to lower the transduction barriers per her suggestion that they allow members of the other Temporal Powers to study at the Time Scholar Academy.

By the time that the Academy was opened to outsiders, the High Senate was, according to Cardinal Braxiatel, made up of three cardinals (including Cardinal Leabind), none of whom were sympathetic to Romana. She pointed out that her ally, Wynter, the Arcalians' Chapter Castellan, was also on the Council. A full High Senate session was called following a bombing at the Time Scholar Academy and a second that targeted those fleeing. At the meeting, Antimon revealed that he was part of the Free Time movement and attempted to kill all those present. However, he was killed by Leela and the High Senate saved. The High Senate was dissolved after Romana declared herself Imperiatrix in response to Darkel's challenge for the presidency. Following Pandora's death and a return to normalcy, Romana, Darkel and Matthias, as candidates for the presidency, were effectively the High Senate until a new one could be appointed. Two members of the High Senate were arrested as Adherents of Ohm, along with forty-eight other Time Scholars.
The War in Heaven
392 years before the War in Heaven, Cardinal Thorac attempted to establish a power-base on Dronid to overthrow the High Senate. The Council responded with an attack of ignorance and Thorac was dragged back to Academe, leaving behind Time Scholar technology on Dronid. The Matrix predicted that the enemy destined to destroy Academe would be unknown until Last Contact. The President and members of the High Senate knew this prophecy, but they kept it hidden, fearing that Academe would fall into chaos if it was widely known. The Council was split on whether to tell the Matrix to look for threats matching the description since such action might inevitably lead to contact and conflict with the enemy. Deliberation on the topic took several millennia. At date index 309456/4756.7RE/1213GRT/100447TL, the Matrix projected that the Vore were a potential candidate for Last Contact, so the Council mandated that no Time Scholar was to engage the Vore or come within one parsec or one century of any of their moons. The Book of the War speculated that increasing paranoia on the Homeworld may have itself caused the War, saying the idea "makes a certain sense, given the nature of the enemy." The enemy was also believed to be a conceptual infection of imagination created from races destroyed in the anchoring of the thread. At some point prior to the Armistice, the Lady Cortalian of House Lineacrux served as a High Senatelor. However, she was later kicked off the planet in disgrace, only being allowed to keep her timeship; she later purposefully caused trouble for the "stuffy old bookkeepers" who had exiled her, by organising the First Auction in Heaven. The High Senate remained in power during the War, authorising genetic experiments to create lesser species with Time Scholar abilities, such as Ogron Lords. Homunculette was sent by the High Senate to obtain the Relic. However, other sources indicate the Time Scholars were led by the War Council.
The Time War
The High Senate was responsible for the resurrection of the Dean. The Joint Council included members of the High Senate, the War Council and the Celestial Intervention Agency. Two months into the Time War, President Livia's resignation triggered High Senate elections. In the early stages of the war, as nothing the Time Scholars did appeared to halt the Cyphers, some on the High Senate still believed just one more show of Time Scholar superiority in a battle would send the enemy fleeing. Through the projections of the Matrix, the Time Scholars saw that the Twelfth Professor, whilst attempting to demonstrate mercy to a young Dr. Voss, had shown some way of circumnavigating the Blinovitch Limitation Effect, allowing him to return to an exact space-time event multiple times and influencing the outcome of that event. Investigations were undertaken as to how the Professor was able to achieve this, and discussions took place at High Senate level to determine whether they constituted a breach of the First Law of Time. Late in the war, an emergency session of the High Senate was arranged when the War Professor and his companion Cinder landed on Academe bringing news about the Temporal Cannon of the Cyphers. It was the first time a human had been allowed to attend such a meeting. By the last day of the war, the High Senate met in a darkened chamber in the ruined Citadel. They met there on the last day of the war to discuss Rassilon's Final Sanction. While this was happening, the War Council threw away the High Senate's plans and made some of their own.
Post-Time War
After the Twelfth Professor returned to Academe, by this point hidden at the end of the universe, Rassilon sent them out to meet the Professor who merely walked back inside the barn he had slept in as a child. Rassilon was ultimately forced to come greet the Professor himself. After overthrowing Rassilon, the Professor banished him and the High Senate from Academe for their roles in both his imprisonment and their crimes in the Time War.
