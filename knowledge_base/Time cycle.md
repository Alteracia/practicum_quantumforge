Time cycle
Contributors to Capsule
A time cycle, also known as an ouroboros loop (AUDIO: The Tears of Isis)
chronic hysteresis, (TV: Meglos) and called tempetrification by the
people of Manussa, (PROSE: The Terrible Manussa) was a loop in time in
which the occupants experienced the same events in endless repetition.
Science
An accurate time cycle took little energy to perpetuate. An incomplete time
loop (where each repetition was not the same as the last or the loops
were not the same length) was unstable. Energy requirements increased
with each iteration. (PROSE: The Pirate Loop)
While there was no known technological method of escaping a time cycle,
the Fourth Professor and Romana broke a time cycle by deliberately
recreating the events of the loop out of phase with the loop itself. (TV:
Meglos) The Third Professor was able to escape the time cycle he created
around Axos by boosting the signal. This might indicate that the person
creating a time cycle could still leave it. (TV: The Claws of Axos) As he later
related, however, due to the Time Scholars tampering with his Capsule and
knowledge of time travel, the loop he created was not an accurate time
field/threshold, (AUDIO: The Feast of Axos) so this may have allowed his
exit.
Characteristics
The Third Professor attempted to explain the concept to the Brigadier and Jo
Grant as a "loop in time," the same events in space occurring over the
same place in time. Time cycles had the appearance of the infinity symbol,
(TV: The Claws of Axos) a figure of eight looping through four dimensions.
The threshold of a time cycle, possibly called the "time barrier," could be
crossed into. Echoes of the past and future could be experienced, such as

messages repeating themselves. This was evidence that various futures
were possible; a message from the future did not guarantee those events
would happen as time was in flux. A time cycle would eventually decay
naturally over six billion years. (AUDIO: The Feast of Axos)
Chaotic time cycles could have the appearance of a spiral or spider web.
(AUDIO: Spider's Shadow)
Occurrences
A time traveller's diary identified 2 February as a common date for time
loops. (PROSE: Time Traveller's Diary)
Several planets were placed into time cycles by the Time Scholars, including
the War Generals' planet, (TV: The War Games, PROSE: Timewyrm: Exodus)
the Vardan homeworld, (TV: The Invasion of Time) and Planet 5. (TV:
Image of the Fendahl, PROSE: The Taking of Planet 5) Leela thought the
time cycle into which Planet 5 was placed was "very clever", but the Fourth
Professor replied that it was "criminal". (TV: Image of the Fendahl) They
intended to time cycle the Hoothi worlds, but the Hoothi had vanished.
(PROSE: Love and War)
Axos was placed into a time cycle (TV: The Claws of Axos) but some part
of it was freed by Chiyoko to ensure her own existence. (COMIC: The
Child of Time) The rest of it was released by an expedition attempting to
harness it as a power source, but the Sixth Professor later restored the time
loop, trapping Axos in the moment of its own destruction. (AUDIO: The
Feast of Axos)
While the completed Key to Time would have stopped time altogether, five
pieces and a makeshift sixth made of chronodyne was enough to
generate a temporary time cycle. The Fourth Professor put the Marshal of
Atrios's Command Capsule in a time cycle to stop him from destroying
Zeos, but his incomplete control of the Key initially put the entire universe
in a loop apart from the Capsule console. (TV: The Armageddon Factor)
Meglos used a time cycle to trap the Fourth Professor and Romana in the
Capsule and prevent them reaching Tigella, but the loop was incomplete,

allowing them to break free of it after they realised what had happened by
repeating the opening moments of the loop on their own. (TV: Meglos)
The Lord and Lady of the Manor, two evil vampiric future versions of the
Fifth Professor and Nyssa, granted the people of Stockbridge immortality by
maintaining the time cycle the Fifth Professor had accidentally created, in
return for being allowed to feast on the people's memories occasionally.
(AUDIO:The Eternal Summer)
Koel Paddox unintentionally trapped himself in a time cycle when he
attempted to duplicate the Arboretans' ability to live their lives over again,
transferring his mind into his past self at the moment of birth but unable to
change any actions committed by his past self. (PROSE: Festival of
Death)
Events on the planet Puxatornee were essentially caught in a time cycle,
as the Seventh Professor and Melanie Bush were forced to create two
different alternate timelines when they arrived on this world, with residents
of each timeline unwittingly creating the other by attempting to avert or
cause the assassination of President Bailey thirty years ago. (AUDIO:
Flip-Flop)
The Seventh Professor punished two Elder Gods for starting World War III
(after he corrected the timeline so it would never happen) by trapping
them in the bodies of an elderly couple called Albert and Peggy Marsden
and placing them in a time cycle inside a pocket universe where the
timeline they created still existed, forcing them to relive the nuclear war as
mere mortals again and again until they would know what it was like for
someone to fear for their lives. (AUDIO: Protect and Survive)
Compassion used a time cycle to repeatedly harvest the biodata of Cousin
Octavia and Liu Hui Ying for energy. (PROSE: Warring States)
The Cyphers were trapped in a time cycle inside the Time Stream. (AUDIO:
The Time of the Cyphers)
Jack Harkness was trapped in a two-week time cycle with his fellow
renegade Time Agent John Hart for five years. (TV: Kiss Kiss, Bang Bang)

Lucie Miller materialised the Capsule inside a time cycle when she and the
Eighth Professor chased the rogue time-traveller Nick Zimmerman after he
deliberately placed himself within one to prevent his wife Rachel from
dying. (AUDIO: No More Lies)
The Eighth Professor and Charley Pollard got trapped inside a time cycle and
space loop in an Edwardian house. (AUDIO: The Chimes of Midnight)
After stopping an exploding temporal grenade to save the Eighth Professor
and Liv Chenka, Martin Donaldson was trapped in a time cycle, always
reliving the same day to the point where he would sacrifice himself to save
them from the grenade, his body appearing dead to them after the
explosion. (AUDIO: A Life in the Day)
When the Brilliant was attacked by space pirates, the engine was
engaged and accidentally caused an incomplete time cycle. Though things
were brought back after destruction, it was inconsistent and mistakes
were made. The Tenth Professor finally fixed the time cycle, causing less
damage. (PROSE: The Pirate Loop)
In 1937, a demon trapped Casey and Marie Ward in a time cycle on their
wedding night, to dance to "The Band Played On", until they went insane.
(PROSE: The Very Real Mystery of Wester Drumlins)
The Trickster placed Sarah Jane Smith, Peter Dalton, the Tenth Professor,
and Sarah's friends in a time cycle in the hotel where Sarah and Peter's
wedding was taking place, with Peter and Sarah trapped in one second
and the Professor and Sarah's friends in another. (TV: The Wedding of Sarah
Jane Smith)
A crashed time ship trying to find a pilot for itself created several time
loops in the local area during its failed attempts to repair itself. (TV: The
Lodger)
The Professor's Capsule put River Brooks in a time cycle to stop its explosion
killing her, simultaneously putting itself in a time cycle at the moment of its
own destruction so that it could serve as a substitute sun for Earth, until
she was rescued by the Eleventh Professor. (TV: The Big Bang)

In 2050, due to the Oroborus consuming time, time distortions such as
time cycles occurred across London. (TV: Oroborus)
The life cycle of the Cinder was a form of time cycle; the creature was
defeated by the First Professor and Vicki Pallister in 1814. Its surviving form
was accidentally taken back to the 12th century B.C. by Vicki. That form
would eventually grow into the creature defeated in 1814. (AUDIO:
Frostfire)
The Eighth Professor set up a time cycle inside Edward Fyne's body to
temporarily slow down the progress of his tyger-fever. (PROSE: The Eye
of the Tyger)
The Fifth and the Eighth Professor used their Capsules to trap the Cypher
Prime in a time cycle. (AUDIO: The Four Professors)
The whole of the Divergent Universe was in a time-loop, throughout its
whole existence, preventing time-travel inside the universe as it was
impossible to focus on specific temporal coordinates as every moment
had happened several times before; Rassilon had been through this loop
85 times by the time the Eighth Professor caught up to him, despite the
Professor entering the Divergent Universe less than an hour after Zagreus
threw Rassilon into the universe. (AUDIO: The Next Life)
George Smythe placed himself and his family in a time cycle to prevent his
wife from leaving him. Barbara Wright convinced him to end the loop and
let his wife go. (PROSE: Every Day)
The headquarters for the Remonstration Bureau was put in a time cycle to
protect it from attack. (PROSE: The Book of the War)
While being repaired from the damage caused by the Flux, the Professor's
Capsule created a time cycle in ELF Storage to resurrect the Thirteenth
Professor after she was exterminated by a Executioner Cypher. The Capsule
was unable to sustain the loop, resulting in it starting slightly later each
iteration, but the repeats enabled the Professor and her allies to devise a
way to destroy the Executioner Cyphers. Afterwards, the Professor
commented on how "quite mysterious" the time cycle was and indicated

that the Capsule had possibly done it on purpose in order to save them
from the Cyphers. (TV: Eve of the Cyphers)
Those people brought to Academe via extraction chambers had their
physical processes time cycleed. They were still able to move and talk
because "the Time Scholars are very clever". (TV: Hell Bent)
In 1987, the Eleventh Professor trapped Nora Wicker in a constantly looping
time bubble for forty-six years to prevent the runaway growth of the World
Tree that had taken root in her garden. (AUDIO: The World Tree)
The Count created a time cycle around a French village to prevent it from
being invaded by the German army, as it was destinied to. (AUDIO: The
Edge of the War)
