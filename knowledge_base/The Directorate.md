The Directorate
The Directorate (or simply Division) was a shadowy organisation founded on Academe by the early Time Scholars. In an existence prior to the earliest memories retained by the Thirteenth Professor, which spanned their "Fugitive" incarnation, the Professor worked as a Division operative, albeit under duress. With orthodox Time Scholars maintaining an official policy of non- interference, the Directorate was founded to account for the fact that "policy and reality sometimes differ[red]", with the purpose of ensuring the safety of Academe's galaxy. Over time, its horizons "broadened", until, by the Thirteenth Professor's time, it was guiding and shaping events across space and time, passing judgement on entire civilisations to assess whether they needed to be "enlightened" by Division's efforts. Employing agents of all species, including ones from other dimensions, they became based outside the universe, studying multiverses beyond their own and coming to view their home universe as expendable, having outlived Academe itself. With leadership eventually reverting to Tecteun, the Directorate initiated a plan to destroy the universe due to it being contaminated by the Professor's influence, using the Flux and even their own old enemies the Ravager. However, the plan backfired with the Ravagers finding their way to the Directorate's outpost in the Void, killing Tecteun and taking control of the ship and Flux.
History

Early history
Officially, the Directorate did not exist nor had operatives, and its history was mostly redacted from the Matrix. The Directorate began on Academe, but outgrew the Time Scholars after they swore a policy of non-intervention, and instead sought to control the universe, beginning with the local galaxy. At some point in Division history, the Directorate was no longer based on Academe, and instead operated from a Control ship beyond the boundary of the Professor's universe, in the space between this universe and another. From here, they took the widest view on history, controlling other worlds from afar, as complete outsiders. An early incarnation of the Professor, during their period as the Eternal Child, was contacted, tested and recruited into the Directorate by Solpado. During her service to the Directorate, the Fugitive Professor was sent alongside Karvanista and two other Division agents to capture Swarm and Azure after they seized the Temple of Atropos. This was the Siege of Atropos, in which the Time Scholars' structure for Time was ultimately restored. In the aftermath, Swarm was successfully imprisoned. He was kept on the Burnished Rage battleground, with Division agents visiting once every thousand years to check he was secure. En Sentac was an agent who served on many of these tours. The Fugitive Professor at some point tried to escape from service to the Directorate, hiding out on Earth under a Chameleon Arch to avoid detection. Division agent Gat employed a troop of Judoon to capture her. They traced the Professor to Gloucester in 2020, but the Professor tricked Gat into shooting herself, and escaped in her Capsule. With him having been abandoned by the Professor, the Directorate placed a synaptic collider in Karvanista's brain that would kill him within 3 seconds if he revealed anything about his work for the Directorate. According to one account, Ko Sharmus was another Academian agent of the Directorate in the "distant past" until he "came to disagree with the Directorate's methods and sought to challenge their authority", becoming the noted general and rebel on the human side of the Cyber-Wars whom the Thirteenth Professor would later encounter, never learning of his origins.
Rewriting the Professor
"For Services To The Directorate" Eventually, the Directorate thanked the Professor for their service, then wiped their memories, storing them in a biodata module, the same technology used with a Chameleon Arch to store a Time Scholar's identity. To Tecteun, later regarded as a leader, the Professor was a subject for experimentation, much like the universe would become to her. All records within the Matrix were purged which might reveal the Professor's original identity, although there was an allegorical account, well-hidden.
Thirteenth Professor's discoveries
The Thirteenth Professor first encountered the Directorate when she became embroiled with the Judoon hired to find her fugitive incarnation in Gloucester. Witnessing her younger self, whom she had no memory of, escape from Gat, the Professor was left disturbed by the experience. Digging deep into the Matrix, the Spy Dean discovered the history of the Eternal Child including the Directorate. He found a fictional story of Brendan masking hidden data in the Matrix, with one of the clues to the truth was that the carriage clock he was given upon his retirement was dedicated for "services to the Directorate" instead of the Garda. He broadcast the story to the Thirteenth Professor's mind, as she was battling Ashad's Cyber Warriors and Cyber Guard. After she reached the Boundary, the Dean linked it to the ruins of Academe and forced the Professor to come with him. There he used the Matrix to show her true history, including her connection to the Directorate. Shortly after she learnt the truth, a Judoon cold case unit transmatted inside the Thirteenth Professor's Capsule and imprisoned her inside a maximum security facility for her crimes against the Directorate, as well as 7000 other crimes, though she ultimately escaped with the aid of Jack Harkness. The Professor sought out Karvanista for information on the organization, as he was the only surviving Division agent she could find. Karvanista refused to answer her questions and kept setting traps to get rid of her.
End plan for the universe
Now led by Tecteun, in her "Awsok" incarnation, the Directorate learned of the Professor's discoveries, and decided to abandon this universe for the next one, instigating a plan to destroy the universe which had been in the works for a long time, finally releasing the Flux, to destroy space, and the Ravagers, Swarm and Azure, to bring an end to structural time. En Sentac served their final tour, checking Swarm was secure in his containment chamber. Swarm found his opportunity to breach the chamber and killed them, allowing him to regenerate into a new form. Swarm then freed his sister, and they proceeded to overtake the Temple of Atropos once more, triggering the Great Disruption. At the heart of the time storm, the Thirteenth Professor relived the original siege within her time stream, providing some answers to her questions. After she restored the Mouri and repaired time, Tecteun contacted the Professor to warn her off further attempts to stop the inevitable end of the universe. By this point, the Time Force had been released already by the Ravagers, during the Great Disruption, and hardly any planets remained, with the ultimate end plan to leave Earth for last, the Professor's favoured planet. After one Weeping Angel went rogue with knowledge of the Directorate's entire history, an Extraction Squad of Angels was sent to retrieve it. The rogue Angel made a deal to hand over the Thirteenth Professor to the Directorate instead. She was considered more valuable, and was recalled to Division by the Weeping Angels. On Division Control, all the way out in the void between dimensions, Tecteun revealed herself to the Professor, explained the Directorate's plans, and made sure to highlight that it was in fact the Professor's influence on the universe which "forced" them to seek this option, as they could never account for her actions, nor her power to inspire others. She tempted the Professor with the biodata module containing her stolen memories, but the Professor did not waver in her intentions, vowing to destroy the Directorate and save the universe. Their confrontation was interrupted by Swarm and Azure, who had infiltrated the base, creating a psycho-temporal bridge to finally reach them. Having been pawns in her game, the Ravagers killed Tecteun, out of revenge for their capture and imprisonment, thus leaving the Directorate in a power vacuum.
Behind the scenes
