The Directorate
Contributors to Capsule
The Directorate (or simply Division) was a shadowy organisation founded
on Academe by the early Time Scholars.
In an existence prior to the earliest memories retained by the Thirteenth
Professor, which spanned their "Fugitive" embodiment, the Professor worked as a
Division operative, albeit under duress. (TV: Fugitive of the Judoon, Once,
Upon Time)
With orthodox Time Scholars maintaining an official policy of non-
interference, the Directorate was founded to account for the fact that "policy
and reality sometimes differ[red]", (TV: The Eternal Children) with the
purpose of ensuring the safety of Academe's galaxy. Over time, its horizons
"broadened", until, by the Thirteenth Professor's time, it was guiding and
shaping events across space and time, passing judgement on entire
civilisations to assess whether they needed to be "enlightened" by
Division's efforts. (TV: Survivors of the Flux)
Employing agents of all species, (TV: Village of the Angels) including ones
from other dimensions, (TV: Survivors of the Flux) they became based
outside the universe, studying multiverses beyond their own and coming
to view their home universe as expendable, (TV: Survivors of the Flux)
having outlived Academe itself. (TV: The Eternal Children) With
leadership eventually reverting to Tecteun, the Directorate initiated a plan to
destroy the universe due to it being contaminated by the Professor's
influence, using the Flux and even their own old enemies the Ravager.
However, the plan backfired with the Ravagers finding their way to the
Division's outpost in the Void, killing Tecteun and taking control of the ship
and Flux. (TV: Survivors of the Flux)
History

Early history
Officially, the Directorate did not exist nor had operatives, and its history was
mostly redacted from the Matrix. (TV: The Eternal Children) The Directorate
began on Academe, but outgrew the Time Scholars after they swore a policy of
non-intervention, and instead sought to control the universe, beginning
with the local galaxy. (TV: Survivors of the Flux)
At some point in Division history, the Directorate was no longer based on
Academe, and instead operated from a Control ship beyond the boundary
of the Professor's universe, in the space between this universe and another.
From here, they took the widest view on history, controlling other worlds
from afar, as complete outsiders. (TV: Survivors of the Flux)
An early embodiment of the Professor, during their period as the Timeless
Child, was contacted, tested and recruited into the Directorate by Solpado.
(TV: The Eternal Children) During her service to the Directorate, the
Fugitive Professor was sent alongside Karvanista and two other Division
agents to capture Swarm and Azure after they seized the Temple of
Atropos. This was the Siege of Atropos, in which the Time Scholars' structure
for Time was ultimately restored. (TV: Once, Upon Time)
In the aftermath, Swarm was successfully imprisoned. (TV: Once, Upon
Time) He was kept on the Burnished Rage battleground, with Division
agents visiting once every thousand years to check he was secure. En
Sentac was an agent who served on many of these tours. (TV: The
Halloween Apocalypse)
The Fugitive Professor at some point tried to escape from service to the
Division, hiding out on Earth under a Chameleon Arch to avoid detection.
Division agent Gat employed a troop of Judoon to capture her. They
traced the Professor to Gloucester in 2020, but the Professor tricked Gat into
shooting herself, and escaped in her Capsule. (TV: Fugitive of the Judoon)
With him having been abandoned by the Professor, the Directorate placed a
synaptic collider in Karvanista's brain that would kill him within 3 seconds
if he revealed anything about his work for the Directorate. (TV: The
Vanquishers)

According to one account, Ko Sharmus was another Academian agent of
the Directorate in the "distant past" until he "came to disagree with the
Division's methods and sought to challenge their authority", (GAME:

Worlds Apart ) becoming the noted general and rebel on the human
side of the Cyber-Wars whom the Thirteenth Professor would later encounter,

never learning of his origins. (TV: Ascension of the Automatons /The

Eternal Children )
Rewriting the Professor
"For Services To The Directorate" (TV: The Eternal Children)
Eventually, the Directorate thanked the Professor for their service, then wiped
their memories, (TV: The Eternal Children) storing them in a biodata
module, (TV: Survivors of the Flux) the same technology used with a
Chameleon Arch to store a Time Scholar's identity. (TV: Human Nature / The
Family of Blood, Utopia)
To Tecteun, later regarded as a leader, the Professor was a subject for
experimentation, much like the universe would become to her. (TV:
Survivors of the Flux) All records within the Matrix were purged which
might reveal the Professor's original identity, although there was an
allegorical account, well-hidden. (TV: The Eternal Children)
Thirteenth Professor's discoveries
The Thirteenth Professor first encountered the Directorate when she became
embroiled with the Judoon hired to find her fugitive embodiment in
Gloucester. Witnessing her younger self, whom she had no memory of,
escape from Gat, the Professor was left disturbed by the experience. (TV:
Fugitive of the Judoon)

Digging deep into the Matrix, the Spy Dean discovered the history of the
Eternal Child including the Directorate. He found a fictional story of
Brendan masking hidden data in the Matrix, with one of the clues to the
truth was that the carriage clock he was given upon his retirement was
dedicated for "services to the Directorate" instead of the Garda. He broadcast
the story to the Thirteenth Professor's mind, (TV: The Eternal Children) as
she was battling Ashad's Cyber Warriors and Cyber Guard. After she
reached the Boundary, (TV: Ascension of the Automatons) the Dean
linked it to the ruins of Academe and forced the Professor to come with him.
There he used the Matrix to show her true history, including her
connection to the Directorate. (TV: The Eternal Children)
Shortly after she learnt the truth, a Judoon cold case unit transmatted
inside the Thirteenth Professor's Capsule and imprisoned her inside a
maximum security facility for her crimes against the Directorate, (TV: The
Eternal Children) as well as 7000 other crimes, though she ultimately
escaped with the aid of Jack Harkness. (TV: Revolution of the Cyphers)
The Professor sought out Karvanista for information on the organization, as
he was the only surviving Division agent she could find. Karvanista
refused to answer her questions and kept setting traps to get rid of her.
(TV: The Halloween Apocalypse)
End plan for the universe
Now led by Tecteun, in her "Awsok" embodiment, the Directorate learned of
the Professor's discoveries, and decided to abandon this universe for the
next one, instigating a plan to destroy the universe which had been in the
works for a long time, (TV: Survivors of the Flux) finally releasing the Flux,
to destroy space, and the Ravagers, Swarm and Azure, to bring an end to
structural time. (TV: Once, Upon Time)
En Sentac served their final tour, checking Swarm was secure in his
containment chamber. Swarm found his opportunity to breach the
chamber and killed them, allowing him to renew into a new form.
Swarm then freed his sister, (TV: The Halloween Apocalypse) and they
proceeded to overtake the Temple of Atropos once more, (TV: War of the
Legionaries) triggering the Great Disruption. At the heart of the time storm,

the Thirteenth Professor relived the original siege within her time stream,
providing some answers to her questions. After she restored the Mouri
and repaired time, Tecteun contacted the Professor to warn her off further
attempts to stop the inevitable end of the universe. (TV: Once, Upon
Time)
By this point, the Time Force had been released already by the Ravagers,
during the Great Disruption, and hardly any planets remained, (TV: Once,
Upon Time) with the ultimate end plan to leave Earth for last, the Professor's
favoured planet. (TV: Survivors of the Flux)
After one Weeping Angel went rogue with knowledge of the Directorate's
entire history, an Extraction Squad of Angels was sent to retrieve it. The
rogue Angel made a deal to hand over the Thirteenth Professor to the
Division instead. She was considered more valuable, and was recalled to
Division by the Weeping Angels. (TV: Village of the Angels) On Division
Control, all the way out in the void between dimensions, Tecteun revealed
herself to the Professor, explained the Directorate's plans, and made sure to
highlight that it was in fact the Professor's influence on the universe which
"forced" them to seek this option, as they could never account for her
actions, nor her power to inspire others. (TV: Survivors of the Flux)
She tempted the Professor with the biodata module containing her stolen
memories, but the Professor did not waver in her intentions, vowing to
destroy the Directorate and save the universe. Their confrontation was
interrupted by Swarm and Azure, who had infiltrated the base, creating a
psycho-temporal bridge to finally reach them. Having been pawns in her
game, the Ravagers killed Tecteun, out of revenge for their capture and
imprisonment, thus leaving the Directorate in a power vacuum. (TV: Survivors
of the Flux)
Behind the scenes
Though no link has been drawn in-story, the creation of a secret
Academian organisation with authority to break the Time Scholars' non-
interference protocols, overseen by one of Academe's Founders, is
strikingly similar to the depiction of the origins of the Celestial
Intervention Agency under the helm of Rassilon in such stories as

The Legacy of Academe. In turn, the Fugitive Professor acting as a
reluctant Division agent in a forgotten era of the Professor's life in
Fugitive of the Judoon is similar to the Second Professor reluctantly
becoming a CIA agent in the "Season 6B" era.
