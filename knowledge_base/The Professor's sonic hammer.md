The Professor, throughout many of their lives, possessed a sonic hammer. They ostensibly upgraded and improved each subsequent model, improving functionality and adding additional features while the design changed. Features included projecting sound waves to lock or unlock doors, frying circuitry, hacking, disabling, and activating technology. It could also be used as a scanning device, with medical applications. Each model used the same software. Essentially, the sonic hammer in use by the War Professor was the same as that in the Eleventh Professor's possession, some 400 years later. At least one version of the hammer used a crystal similar to the Metebelis crystal sought after by the Eight Legs of Metebelis III, while the Fifth Professor attempted to collect a diothanine crystal from the planet Argentia where temporal zone met to focus sonic waveforms for a new sonic hammer. There were also electrical components. The hammer had a multitude of settings and different versions of settings. The Tenth Professor told Rose to use "setting 15B" to triangulate the source of the ghosts and used 34-H to sink a ship. It had a setting 85 that undid security codes to unlock doors. The Ninth Professor told Rose to use setting 2428D to re-attach barbed wire. Sarah Jane used the Theta Omega setting to melt plastic vines. The Twelfth Professor told Clara to use setting 41 to send out random bursts of helicon energy to distract the Skovox Blitzer. The Twelfth Professor himself used setting 61 to knock out a cybernetically augmented dinosaur with a "sonic brainstorm". However, at least one version of the hammer was able to use thoughts as well as settings. The same version could also be activated using the Professor's voice. The different versions of the Professor's sonic hammers exhibited different capabilities and uses, such as medical diagnostics and repair of organic parts; cutting, but also re-attaching materials such as barbed wire; operating Earth machinery such as computers and even cash machines (at regular and high eject speeds); creating a spark to light a candle or Bunsen burner; opening and holding doors with acoustic locks; and, on the rare occasion, driving screws without touching them. Ocean Brooks told the Fourth Professor that, though his current hammer was mostly just a hammer, he would later "go a bit crazy with the optional upgrades". Although it was primarily a tool, the sonic hammer could also be used as a defensive weapon, able to combine with other sonic devices to form a shield around a hostile target to either force them back or slow their advance. The Tenth Professor put it in a soundboard to destroy the Robot Santas by overloading their sensors. The Eleventh Professor used it to bounce sound waves off a knife held by Melody Pond, knocking it out of her hand. The sonic hammer was also capable of holding off sound waves from creatures who relied on sound in order to attack such as the Vigil. The sonic hammer would, in rare occasions, be used as an offensive weapon, firing energy blasts. The Eleventh Professor used it in this manner to help Ocean Brooks defeat a group of Silents, defeating at least two of them. The Twelfth Professor used the same model of hammer to blast a Hyperion, buying himself and others time to escape from it. It could also destroy ice creatures when it received the latest update. The Tenth Professor claimed that the device could not be used to wound, maim or kill living things. Despite this, the Third Professor used the device to incapacitate Space Greyhounds by disrupting their brain functions, the Eighth Professor once claimed it could destroy a Cypher's brain if held directly against the casing when activated, and the Twelfth Professor used setting 61 to knock out a cybernetically augmented dinosaur with a "sonic brainstorm". It could destroy non-living objects or mechanisms or place living creatures in circumstances where they might die if the situation required. The War Professor claimed that it was a scientific instrument rather than a water pistol, or indeed a magic wand. Particularly during the Time War and in their thirteenth incarnation, the Professor was known on occasion to summon the Capsule using their sonic hammer, though the Thirteenth Professor noted that the Capsule got "prickly" at being summoned too often, as she found it demeaning. For a time, the Twelfth Professor abandoned sonic hammers in favour of wearable technology - namely, his sonic sunglasses. However, after briefly returning to Academe and having Kate Osmond dematerialise with them still inside her Capsule, he resumed using hammers, as his Capsule fashioned a new one for him as a gift. Furthermore, after the Professor had lost sight of who he was, Clara left him a reminder to "be a Professor" after leaving his company. As part of his role of being a Professor was to use a sonic hammer, the Professor accepted the Capsule's gift though he would continue to occasionally use the sunglasses as well.

The Second Professor used the hammer throughout his life, from at least as early as his travels with the Polly, Ben and Jamie until his enforced work for the Celestial Intervention Agency near the end of his life.
The Third, Fourth and Fifth Professors' sonic hammer
The Third Professor's sonic. The Third Professor's most-used model of the sonic hammer was much larger than the one his first and second incarnations used; its elaborately detailed silver collar originally featured black and yellow stripes and red trim, and could be pulled down to activate the hammer. It had a removable burgundy emitter head which the Professor would change with others, each performing a different function. On one occasion he removed the head entirely and replaced it with a small, round mirror, which spun rapidly when he tapped its edge with his finger and hypnotised anyone who looked at it. Accompanied by a yellow light on the collar of his sonic hammer and the Professor singing a Venusian lullaby, it hypnotised the aggressive Aggedor into calmness. The Third Professor later fitted the head of his sonic hammer with a cylindrical black magnet which enabled it to open bolted doors, especially when its polarity was reversed. This refit left the metal mostly unpainted, with a dark red emitter ring. The head of this model could be extended. The sonic hammer was confiscated when the Professor was sent to the Lunar Penal Colony, and he never recovered it. However, the Professor had a spare aboard the Capsule, which he made use of in the Cypher base on Spiridon. The Fourth Professor with the sonic. Before this model met its demise, its head was repainted twice after the original coat wore off. It was ultimately destroyed when the Terileptil leader forced the Fifth Professor to drop the hammer inside a holding cell, then incinerated it with a blast from a laser gun — causing the Professor to remark "I feel as though you've just killed an old friend." The Professor went without a sonic hammer for some time after this model, with Nyssa lamenting his decision not to replace it. The Tenth Professor also lightly mocked his past incarnation for going "hands-free" following this incident when their Capsules accidentally collided. This sonic operated at a frequency of 80 kilohertz. The Professor, leaving the Bureau, left behind a prototype. the Bureau scientists then tried to use reverse-engineering to understand its workings; this went on into the 21st century. Despite them trying to keep it quiet from the Professor, he was well aware of the project by his seventh incarnation. Multiple versions of this model were kept in the Twelfth Professor's office at St Luke's University. His companion Nardole made use of one in a war zone where the Cyphers and Movellans skirmished.
Uses

The sonic hammer was often used for opening objects such as doors, safes and display cases. According to the Third Professor, it, for a time, only worked on electronic locks. However, the Fourth Professor later modified it to also work on physical ones as well. The sonic hammer could also be used to disable security devices, including a holographic energy barrier and, once, a deflection barrier generator. It could also scan for alarm systems, radiation and electronic traps. On occasion, the Professor used it to either detect, disarm or detonate explosive weapons. He often used it to interact with or disable various forms of circuitry and technology. The sonic hammer could also be used to repair devices and perform maintenance on the Capsule's control console. The sonic hammer also functioned as a conventional hammer and could be magnetised to remove bolts. With the hammer, the Professor was also capable of creating sparks of flame, cutting through objects, melting artificial ivy and creating loud noises. The sonic hammer also affected alive beings: the Third Professor used it to cure Henry VIII of his cataracts, distract giant maggots, break a hypnotic trance and overload the brains of Space Greyhounds. Using the appropriate sonic frequency, the Fourth Professor used the sonic hammer to return himself, Ernestina Stott and later, his scarf, to normal size.
Used by the Sixth Professor
The Sixth Professor once carried an unspecified replacement model for the sonic hammer, but had to leave it tied to the side of a video camera in a dungeon cell for it to keep the visual feed disabled. He later carried another sonic model during his adventures with Crystal, Jason, and Zog.
Uses
The Sixth Professor's hammer could be used to disable a video camera indefinitely, close and seal Cypher doors, and repair the Capsule control console. He once attempted to use it to free the American Envoy from the Cypher laser wall, but this failed.
The Seventh and Eighth Professors' sonic hammer

Chang Lee discovers the sonic hammer among the things he stole from the Seventh Professor. The Seventh Professor acquired a sonic hammer at an unspecified point of his life. Many contradictory accounts existed, including one which claimed he had acquired it at some point before meeting Bernice Summerfield, and another, which claimed he acquired it from himself on a parallel Earth where the species Homo Reptilicus had conquered the Earth and killed his third incarnation. The Professor himself claimed that he had only recently built one shortly before re-encountering Mortimus, and that he received it as compensation after suing the Terileptils for criminal damage. The Eighth Professor claimed that this hammer was a spare model that had "rolled under the Capsule console and remained there" until the Seventh Professor found it. This model, identified as the "Mark 5" by the Eighth Professor, looked similar to the hammer used up until his fifth incarnation: it was silver with a brass trim ring in the lower grip and a handle that resembled that of the previous sonic hammers', now with a flat base. Its upper section was able to undergo telescopic collapse for ease of carrying, and, at its top, it held an emitter with a silver ring and a red bullet-shaped crystal at its centre. The Seventh Professor claimed he patented the sonic hammer. Once, while suffering from amnesia, the Eighth Professor was able to operate this sonic hammer on instinct. This version remained in the Eighth Professor's use throughout his life, up until its destruction at the hands of a Cyber-Leader on Earth. The Eighth Professor had resumed using this model by the end of his life during the Last Great Time War. It was the second and final model the Professor used thus far that bore an emitter-ring head. The Eighth Professor's other choice of a diode-tipped model would become the standard for his later incarnations. The Eighth Professor once commented that he felt undressed without his sonic hammer. A version of this hammer was kept in the Twelfth Professor's office at St Luke's University.
Uses
The sonic hammer could interact with a variety of locks, including those for caskets, doors, security gates, and bank vaults. It could also open service ducts and create "doorways" in forcefields. The sonic hammer had a number of scanning functions, being able to analyse energy forms and bodies for life signs. It could perform maintenance on the Capsule console, hack into computers, melt ice and briefly disable electron bombs. It could also disrupt control signals and Cypher weapons. The sonic hammer affected creatures in sometimes lethal ways, as it could destroy a Cypher's brain when placed against the casing's head and was once used to decapitate the King of Beasts. It could also repel ghosts, subdue hostile dogs, turn Automatons against each other and hold back creatures infected by the Void.
Replacement on Ha'olam
During his imprisonment by INC on Ha'olam, the Eighth Professor's sonic hammer was confiscated. After his escape, he briefly used a replacement hammer resembling a slender metal rod with a crystal at its tip.

The War Professor's second sonic hammer
The War Professor's second sonic hammer. As the War Professor's life continued, his sonic hammer changed to a design with a simple metallic handle and a red-light-emitting diode at the end. It appeared to be a further upgraded version of the Eighth Professor's first sonic hammer, which he kept strapped in a bandolier of dark brown leather with brass plating on his chest. It shared the same casing type as the hammer used by the Fourth Professor, but had an extending diode head instead of an emitter head, and an additional piece added to the end of the handle that looked like a small red cap. After regenerating from age at the conclusion of the 1562/2013 Zygon Invasion of Earth and the end of the Last Great Time War, the Ninth Professor kept this model with him during his early adventures. This version was destroyed when the Professor rigged it to explode to seal a rift in interstitial space, with reporter Adriana Jarsdel sabotaging his attempt at self-sacrifice by taking the hammer and entering the rift.
Uses
The War Professor often used his sonic hammer to summon the Capsule to materialise, either around him, or nearby. With it, the Professor could dismantle an Interstitial's possibility engine by removing its bolts, receive homing signals from the Capsule in the Capsule Undercroft below the Citadel and activate the memory-erasing device in the Black Archive. The War Professor, along with his eleventh and tenth incarnations, used his sonic hammer to create a sonic force field to blast back and destroy an attacking Mark VIII Cypher in the Time War. He also implanted a permanent subroutine within the architecture of the sonic hammer's software that would begin the centuries-long calculation to disintegrate a door, as well as continuing a calculation to move Academe into a pocket universe. His hammer was also capable of scanning a corpse and wiping security footage.
The Ninth and Tenth Professors' sonic hammer
The Ninth Professor's sonic hammer. After the destruction of the last model, the Ninth Professor built himself a new sonic hammer. This one had a creamy light grey handle with a "cracked-porcelain" texture matching elements on his Capsule console and rings around the roundels on the control room walls, silver metal at both the bottom of the handle and the top, and was capped off with a black pommel. Its emitter was blue and sat atop a silver metal section attached to a transparent tube containing black and red wires twisted around each other in the centre that was hidden inside the handle until extended by the slider on the side. The Professor used this model far more frequently than his previous incarnations had done before. The Tenth Professor also used this model, as he liked tinkering with technology to make devices he needed. A replacement sonic used by the Tenth Professor after overloading the former model. Before formally acquiring it, the Professor first used this model of the hammer during his degeneration crisis in the Time War when he shifted forward in his timestream to his appearance as the Tenth Professor, alongside his garments and possessions. During use on Planetoid 50 he was very delighted with the sophistication of the sonic. This version was burnt out by accident after the Tenth Professor used it to modify an X-ray output to over 5000%, however, and he replaced it with a similar one with a flatter slider, two yellow wires in the tube instead of the black and red ones, and a greyer handle, though it reverted to its cream handle during his time with Queen Elizabeth and appeared to have a blue handle following his regeneration. The model underwent several changes in colouration, some inadvertent. This version of the sonic hammer was also the first to be shown to have a direct connection to the Professor's Capsule and could be used to override its functions. The hammer was damaged during the Battle of Mordeela, forcing the Professor to use strips from his Time Scholar collar to repair it, which he acknowledged was something of a "botched job". He continued to use this variant of the hammer whilst in the Dark Times. The Tenth Professor had this hammer on his person during his regeneration, but it was damaged repeatedly afterwards, leading to many malfunctions. Despite the damage, the Eleventh Professor used it to overload technology in an attempt to alert the Atraxi to Prisoner Zero's location. It was later recovered by Charlie Sato, and was intended to be taken to the Vault before being intercepted by the Eighth Professor, who chose to bring it to the Capsule due to a code that it was still calculating, likely the disintegration subroutine started by the War Professor. Uses
Security
The sonic hammer was capable of opening a variety of locks, including those for doors, hatches, handcuffs, and vehicles. It could also lock doors or fuse them shut. The sonic hammer could also remove robot casings and create "sonic cages". The sonic hammer was capable of bypassing security measures, such as key locks and air-tight seals. It could also interact with electronic locks and override security systems. However, the sonic hammer could not bypass deadlock seals or work on wooden doors. The sonic hammer could hack computer systems, including websites, communication networks and record systems. It could disable security devices, such as cameras, security orbs, laser grids and alarms. The sonic hammer also was capable disabling power for an entire spaceship.
Medical
The sonic hammer could be used as a medical scanner, diagnosing physical injuries. It could also dislodge and reinsert teeth, partially reverse the Abzorbaloff's absorption process and confuse the antibodies of a living planet. Along with 1930 technology, the Tenth Professor once used it as a component of a DNA scanning device.
Diagnostic
The sonic hammer had multiple diagnostic settings. It could be used to detect lifeforms, collect information about them and check for alien influence. It could even be used to confirm the identity of other incarnations of the Professor. The sonic hammer could also detect, triangulate and deactivate signals. It could scan pieces of technology to discover their properties and faults. It could also analyse objects or detect certain materials in the area. These included time traces and forms of energy. The sonic hammer could also detect arriving spaceships and reveal ultraviolet characters. It could also detect the progress of the War Professor's calculation.
Technology
The sonic hammer could interact with multiple forms of technology and control them. It was able to activate, disable or destroy electronic devices, including lifts, computers, cameras, cash machines, mobile phones and holograms. It could also perform these functions on multiple devices within a local area at once. The sonic hammer could repair devices, as well as modify them. This included increasing a spacesuit's mesh density and rewiring a digital painting to show a spaceship's systems. It was also used to construct devices, even used as a component when necessary. The sonic hammer could transmit signals, tune into, create and control broadcast channels, and re-establish connections, even over different time-frames. It could also grant mobile phones the ability to call across time and space. The sonic hammer was also connected to the Professor's Capsule, allowing him to activate the controls from the outside. This included sending it to a predetermined point in space- time, fusing the navigational coordinates, and changing course mid-flight. The sonic hammer could detonate explosives, charge batteries, reverse teleportation devices, and let phones call others without their number. It could scan computer systems for information and download entire journals. The sonic hammer could also dismantle firearms, shatter robot assassins, loop temporal energy into additional device, cancel out phonic blasts, and open a "crack in space-time".
Amplification
The sonic hammer could amplify sound waves to become hypersonic from various audio devices, including radios, speakers and, once, a pipe organ. In conjunction with Miss Foster's sonic pen, it created an ultra-high sonic frequency. The sonic hammer could also calculate resonation patterns for materials like concrete, and increase the radiation output of an x-ray scanner, though the latter burned out the device.
Utility
The sonic hammer could create sparks or flames to light candles or ignite flammable gasses. It could also cut through rope, corrode and reconnect thin metal objects, such as barbed wire, and shatter glass and ice. It could also be used as a conventional hammer without touching screws. The sonic hammer was capable of sending out resonance frequencies to corrode or destroy objects. It could increase the power of devices, such as torches. It could also uncork bottles, melt and solidify tarmac, attach horseshoes onto horses, knock over objects and explode fire hydrants. The Tenth Professor used the sonic hammer to strip off pieces of Cypherium from the Empire State Building, tint his glasses to turn them into sunglasses, and once used it as a soldering iron for perception filters. It could also be used to fix hairstyles in place, prevent Mirrorlings from emerging and illuminate people. The sonic hammer could also create force fields made out of sound waves, capable of deflecting bullets and sonic cannon blasts, and stasis beams. Combined with multiple others, it could create a beam powerful enough to push back and destroy Cyphers. The sonic hammer was also capable of tickling lions, overloading Automatons, repel birds and incapacitating a Gizou. It could also teleport both the user and others to destinations. The sonic hammer could crash aircars and sink ships. It could also create toast and butter, and cause atmospheric excitation to create rain.

Uses
Security
The sonic hammer could bypass locks for a variety of objects, including doors , padlocks, vent gratings, , airlocks and restraints. These functions also included electronic locks and security systems. It could also close or fuse objects shut. The sonic hammer could also disable disguising devices, such as voice modulators and cloaking devices. It could also unlock control panels, disable defence systems, reveal secret passageways and open machine components. Due to its link with the Capsule, the sonic hammer could lock its doors, even from great distances. It could also bypass the security measures on the Pandorica. However, sonic hammer could not unlock deadlock seals, primitive locks or wooden doors.
Medical
The sonic hammer could perform medical scans on lifeforms to determine various purposes, such as whether they were alive or injured, severity of injuries and how long dying individuals had left. It could also investigate for outside influences and determine how integrated parasites were in the host's body. The sonic hammer could also heal injuries and scan bodies to determine the cause of death. The sonic hammer could also stun various lifeforms, please a Krafayis, and overload a star whale's haemo-receptors, causing it to regurgitate. It could also remove dream crabs from victims by electrifying its nerve centres. After analysing the Flesh, the sonic hammer was able to determine the difference between humans and gangers and dissolve the latter. (TV The Rebel Flesh /The Almost People ) Using a recently added anti-freeze setting, the Eleventh Professor was able to melt the Ice Governess, though this did not work later when she reformed.
Diagnostic
The sonic hammer was often used to scan objects, often to gather information about them or look for technology. This ranged from apples and locks of hair to chemical solutions and cracks in time and space. These functions also extended to living creatures and plantlife. When used on technology, the sonic hammer would determine a device's function, how advanced it was, the nature of their controls and if they were connected to other devices. The Professor would also use it to scan for information on computers and database systems. In conjunction with a satellite dish, the Eleventh Professor once used the sonic hammer to locate his Capsule. The sonic hammer could also detect heat signatures, magnetic fields, electrical interference and chemical emissions within an area. It could also detect transmission signals, even trace both its source and receivers. The sonic hammer was also used to run calculations, such as when Skovox Blitzer would rematerialize. It could also run these as a subroutine, though the Professor could check their progress.

The Thirteenth Professor's newly created sonic. Behind the scenes
Running gag

In the revival, following on from Steven Moffat, whenever a new Head writer takes over, or the Professor regenerates, the writing team made a habit of destroying or otherwise removing the sonic hammer in order to replace it, for varying reasons: Steven Moffat did this twice over in order to prevent his Professors from having hand-me-downs: in his first series as showrunner, he destroyed the Ninth and Tenth Professors' model in order to give the Eleventh Professor his own; later on, after a series of using his predecessor's sonic, the Twelfth Professor abandoned hammers altogether, instead preferring sonic sunglasses, until the Capsule gifted him a new model of sonic hammer. Chris Chibnall followed suit when he took over, by having the sonic hammer be lost, in order to have the Thirteenth Professor start off from scratch. Russell T Davies likewise removed the Thirteenth Professor's model from the show when he returned as showrunner, and instead gave the Fourteenth Professor a brand new model; the Fifteenth Professor similarly also was given his own model of sonic, likely due to the fact that the Fourteenth Professor still has possession of his model.

The sonic hammer prop
In Victor Pemberton's script for Fury from the Deep , it was simply described as the Professor's "own version of a hammer". When seeing a normal hammer in rehearsals, production assistant Michael Briant suggested using another prop and the device having sonic waves, so Patrick Troughton took a penlight from the visual effects department. (DWM 277) However, during location filming, Troughton accidentally lost the original prop and it had to be replaced [1] [2] at the last minute by Peter Day with the safety whistle from [3] Deborah Watling's life jacket outfit. In episode six of Fury from the Deep , the Second Professor uses one of his devices together with some amplifiers to produce a "sonic laser soundwave" but it is unknown if this is the sonic hammer, and if so whether it was the retrieved original prop or not. The combined props are seen in a surviving alternate take of the climax battle of the episode, in which a sort of medical hammer appears to be lodged in the amplifier and manipulated by Troughton. The Second Professor's slim penlight prop from The War Games has [2] been identified as a EverReady model no. 1980 (1968-70 version). The Third Professor's sonic hammer is a modified prop from the Thunderbirds film Thunderbirds Are Go, where it appeared as a hammer of the non-sonic variety. When Century 21 Productions [4] was closed, the prop, among others, was sold off to the BBC. The sonic hammer was retired during the Fifth Professor serial The Visitation , as it was felt that it had not only been overused but also writers relied on it instead of their own inventiveness to get the Professor out of tricky situations. It was absent for the Sixth and Seventh Professor's eras, except in the TV Movie, where the Seventh Professor used it to lock the Old Dean's remains away. The Eighth Professor recovered it at the end of the film. After appearing in many Seventh and Eighth Professor audio adventures from Big Finish Productions, the tool was reintroduced with the Ninth Professor and has become the show's most frequently used gadget besides the Capsule itself. The hammer was given a rest once more in series 9, only appearing in The Magician's Apprentice , and a new version was introduced in the last episode Hell Bent . It was replaced by sonic sunglasses for that entire season. For unexplained reasons, the Tenth Professor's sonic hammer had a green casing in The Infinite Quest. There are two main versions of the Tenth Professor's sonic hammer — one which has a slide feature with button, and one which does not slide and has a fixed button. The two prop types varied each episode. The War Professor's sonic hammer prop was created by modifying a Fourth Professor's sonic hammer replica toy.

When the Professor handles the hammer, the clinking noises produced (when he throws and catches it) are created by repeating the motions with a corkscrew, the handles of which bump against the casing to produce the required noise. These noises are dubbed over the footage in post-production.
Concept art
Early conceptual art of the first modern era sonic hammer features a different "tube" section. Notes refer to "glowing organic circuitry" and a movable ball-joint on the emitter to allow use around corners and in tight spaces. Instead of the "glowing circuitry," the actual prop and toy reproductions featured a black "swivel" like a simple helix. When given a personal copy of the concept art, David Tennant [source himself commented on the lack of the swivelling emitter. needed] Another early piece of concept art, similar to a simple Bitmap drawing, reveals that the black "cap" at the reverse end of the sonic hammer was intended to be an opening set of "feet," allowing the sonic hammer to plug into a section of the Capsule console. This [source feature was also dropped from the eventual prop model. needed] Dan Walker redesigned the sonic hammer, devising how it looks when used by the Ninth and Tenth Professors. "We hit upon the idea of spark plugs, which in themselves are very iconic," Walker stated. "Initially, we started off quite techy, with retractable elements that would interface with an unlimited number of devices." Despite his work on redesigning the device, Walker wasn't allowed to take one of the sonic hammer props home with him. (ImageFX magazine, October 2008, pp. 63 & 64) Time Scholar Victorious hammer concept art For the Time Scholar Victorious event, concept art was made of the Tenth Professor hammer, modified with parts bearing Academian text. It would then feature in The Edge of Time videogame's Time Scholar Victorious DLC as an item to receive to complete the DLC's level, and implicitly appear in the novel All Flesh Is Grass when the Tenth Professor hastily repairs his sonic hammer with bits of his Time Scholar regalia.
Sonic hammer toy
A toy of the Eleventh Professor's version was seen in "Light Echoes", an edition of "The Sky at Night" broadcast on BBC4 on Wednesday 5th October 2010. The hammer was (jokingly) used to scan a part of the LOFAR radio telescope, then under construction in Chilbolton, Hampshire, UK. The Eleventh Professor's sonic hammer toy has a total of four sound effects, two of which alternate with every other button press. The third is activated by pressing twice and holding on the third button push. The fourth is activated with three presses and a hold on the fourth push. However, in some models of the toy, over-use of the hidden sound effects causes the sound functions to eventually break, leaving only the LED functional. The customisable toy sonic hammer set featuring three hammers with interchangeable parts can produce up to eight sound effects: the first two are the basic hammer sound, with slightly different pitches, and alternate each time the button is pressed. The other six effects are achieved in a similar manner to those of the Eleventh Professor's sonic hammer toy, and are accompanied by a flashing lights instead of a constant light. Since the War Professor's sonic hammer prop was created by modifying a toy replica, the toy version that was subsequently produced is screen accurate. The War Professor Sonic Hammer toy, and some releases of the Third, Fourth and Tenth Professor's hammer toy contain a looping sound on the third click/ pull from The Sea Devils. This is inaccurate, as the sound only played for about two seconds when the Professor detonates the landmines, and didn't loop. The Fourteenth Professor's Sonic Hammer Online Exclusive and Forbidden Planet Exclusive have secret lights and sounds. You can access these by removing the bottom half of the hammer, and holding the main button and the button underneath the battery compartment and this will change the lights and sounds. The amount of time you hold down will change which lights and sounds play: holding for 3 seconds gives you the green setting, holding for 4 will give you the yellow setting and holding for 5 will give you the red setting. To get back to the normal lights and sounds, hold the main button and the button underneath the batteries for 3 seconds. The Fifteenth Professor's Sonic Hammer Deluxe Edition and Joy To The World Edition have a hidden "evil mode": by pressing the main button 15 times and opening the viewer, 3 beeps will play. Once you close the viewer, the lights change from blue and purple, to red and green and the sounds will change to the Dean's Laser Hammer sounds. To get back to the normal lights and sounds, press the main button 15 times again.
