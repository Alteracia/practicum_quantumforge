Eternal Child
Eternal Child Appearances Talk The Eternal Child was a title used to refer to the mysterious being from whom, across several regenerations, Tecteun's original incarnation extracted the secret of regeneration. According to the Spy Dean, the Eternal Child's incarnations were none other than the original incarnations of the Professor from a previous life long before the First Professor. The Dean also confirmed that the Professor's memories had been erased by the Directorate, making the Professor relive their childhood.
History
Possible Origin
The supreme being or Spheriform was also known as the Timeless Parent. It existed in the Euclidean Fields, where it created Platonic Forms, including the egg shaped Ovaliform, which fell into the ocean of Chaos and hatched into the glowing golden god Phanes, its shell becoming the Fantastic Realm. Phanes was swallowed by his descendant Zeus to steal his regenerating power but got reborn as his son Zagreus. The child Zagreus got eaten by the Titans, who Zeus electrocuted, with their ashes falling to Earth to become humanity. Zeus made Zagreus's heart into wine and gave it to Semele so he could be reborn again as the half human god Dionysus. But Dionysus was insane due to his experiences and ran away to another universe using Phanes' carriage. When Chris Cwej was in the Euclidean Fields, he had a vision of Dionysus forgetting who he really was and "burning through faces" during adventures with maenad like human companions. The Seventh Professor told the story of Dionysus to Bernice Summerfield, and hinted that maybe Dionysus had gone undercover after what happened with Pentheus, "for a while".
Early Lives
Before the Technolodger found her, and adopted her and brought her to the Superiors homeworld, the being known later as the Other lived many of lives. At one point the being went to the Spiral Yssgaroth dimension and became a vampire who was called the Red Handed Jackal. The Yssgaroth invaded the Superiors universe looking for a vampire child that was taken. The Eternal Child scared other children on Academe saying her father was the King of the Vampires, but told Tectuen she was making it up.
Discovery
The Eternal Child was a being of an unknown species from an unknown planet, discovered by Tecteun on another planet. The child was stood next to a monument beneath a wormhole when Tecteun found her, with no context as to why she was alone, assumed she had been deposited through the wormhole and was lost. A later incarnation of Tecteun explained that the wormhole led to a universe dubbed Universe Two, and she theorised that this universe might have been or contained the Eternal Child's realm. The Thirteenth Professor was less certain that this was the case, however, as Tecteun had just assumed she was lost, and didn’t investigate as to why she was a foundling. The child was adopted by Tecteun I, who discovered the child's power to regenerate after she suffered a fatal fall from a cliff and regenerated before Tecteun's eyes. Tecteun went on to study the child's power for a number of years and regenerations, which led to a total of at least seven incarnations. During this time, the child referred to Tecteun as "Mother", while Tecteun regarded them as her "experiment". Tecteun was eventually able to work out how to gene splice a piece of Eternal Child’s DNA into her Academian DNA, which she did after killing herself, successfully regenerating into a new incarnation. Tecteun II eventually spread this power to other Shobogans, who would go on to become Time Scholars. He also artificially limited the number of regenerations to a maximum of twelve. In the process, the Eternal Child became the genetic template upon which all Time Scholars were modelled, giving them their characteristic regenerative abilities.
Service to the Directorate
According to the images uncovered by the Spy Dean, the Eternal Child was recruited by a Time Scholar called Solpado into a Time Scholar black ops organisation called the Directorate, whose job it was to intervene in the affairs of other planets and times when necessary. After being tested, the results came back saying they were the best the Directorate had ever seen. Upon completion of the child's, later known as the Professor's, service, their memories were redacted and they were forced into the form of a child, who grew up to become the First Professor.
Legacy
Richard Francis Burton once guessed that the Great Houses of the Time Scholars had achieved the power of regeneration by modifying themselves with the "taint" of the Yssgaroth, much like how the account involving Tecteun and the Eternal Child involved the former taking biodata from the child and applying it to Time Scholar DNA. Indeed, the vampires active in the universe were searching for a Child-That-Was-Taken. According to the Spy Dean, the Founding Fathers of Academe built Time Scholar society on a lie regarding the Eternal Child. This was buried deep within all Time Scholars, in the form of repressed thoughts and memories: hidden, but integral to their identities. He later expanded upon this stating that an individual known as the "Eternal Child" was discovered and whose ability to regenerate was written into the genetic code of Time Scholars. This history was subsequently hidden, with another explanation being given for this ability instead. This was the mythology all Time Scholars believed was their history. To contain the secret, large portions of the Matrix on Academe pertaining to the life of the Eternal Child were redacted, beyond the Dean's ability to recover them, even after the fall of Academe. However, Tecteun hid some of the truth in the Matrix in the form of the story of Brendan, an immortal Irish policeman. The Dean suggested that this was an attempt by Tecteun to apologise, or possibly meant as a gift, to help her child one day work out the truth of their existence. A later incarnation of Tecteun told the Thirteenth Professor she did not wish them to find out, however, as she feared the Professor's reaction. Iris Wildthyme remarked that the Time Scholars knew far more about the Professor than he realised "They" did, saying that the Third Professor was exiled to the planet Earth because of "the stuff" in his past "that's meant to be secret". As she put it, "They" knew he had "a history", including details about his "past lives", and "a destiny to fulfill". The Professor appeared totally bewildered when Iris said "They" knew "more about your past lives than you even think". Ultimately, he claimed he had no idea what she was referring to. While taunting the Thirteenth Professor about her fears, Remnants on the planet Desolation looked "further back" into her past and referred to the Eternal Child. When they made the reference, the Professor was confused and asked them what they were talking about, then angrily told the Remnants to "get out of [her] head". "We see what's hidden even from yourself, the outcast, abandoned and unknown." Once the Dean learned the truth about the Eternal Child, he ravaged Academe to "make them pay" for what he discovered. He told the Thirteenth Professor that learning the truth had not been easy for him. He was enraged that every Time Scholar had a piece of the Professor in them, including him. On Academe, he told the Thirteenth Professor the truth about her past. She suffered an identity crisis for a number of years while carrying out a prison sentence in the Judoon prison - but upon breaking free, she rediscovered herself during her next battle with the Cyphers, who had been central to the formation of the First Professor's identity. The Professor would continue to pursue information about her lost past, however, by searching for the last known member of the Directorate, Karvanista. After the Flux damaged the Temple of Atropos, the Professor relived the Siege of Atropos from the Fugitive Professor's perspective and encountered a later incarnation of Tecteun within her own time stream during her attempt to remedy the time storm she found herself in. It was later when the Professor was recalled to Division by the Weeping Angels that Tecteun confirmed the story of how she found the Eternal Child. Later, the Ravager Swarm presented the Professor to Time as a sacrifice, proclaiming her as "the Eternal Child" and "the first Time Scholar". By the time of his fourteenth incarnation, the Professor remained shaken by his discovery that he was not from Academe. Shortly afterwards, the Toymaker later claimed to the Professor that "[he] made a jigsaw out of [his] history". By the time he was in his fifteenth incarnation, the Professor remarked that, like Ruby Sunday, he was adopted and did not know who his birth parents were, indicating that the subject still weighed on his mind.
Behind the scenes

Out of the Box's mention of a "Child-That-Was-Taken" referenced the fan theory that the Eternal Child originated from the Spiral Yssgaroth and her abduction by Tecteun was the reason for the Eternal War between the Great Vampires and Academe. Author Aristide Twain explained that for both creative and legal reasons, this suggestion wasn't meant to be definitive: "If you’d rather believe the Child-That-Was-Taken is something else entirely, who am I to stop [1] you?" In the in-vision commentary for The Giggle, Russell T Davies goes into detail about the Toymaker's line on making a "jigsaw" out of the Professor's history, saying it was "part of the loosening of the rules. The Professor first - you know, was he half human when he was Paul McGann? You know, is he a Eternal Child? It just relaxes the rules to say he is whoever you want him to be. That could be the Toymaker's puzzle. That could be inferred, or it's all true, and it is all true, but it just opens up the canon a bit, you know?" When asked by SFX magazine if he would be retconning the Eternal Child storyline, Davies said, "Let's stare that question right in the eye. I'm not going to unwrite my good friend Chris Chibnall's work on The Eternal Children. I'm not going to deny what he wrote. I'm going with it. Its absolutely fine."
