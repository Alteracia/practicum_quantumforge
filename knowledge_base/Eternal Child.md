Eternal Child Appearances Talk
The Eternal Child was a title used to refer to the mysterious being from
whom, across several renewals, Tecteun's original embodiment
extracted the secret of renewal. According to the Spy Dean, the
Eternal Child's embodiments were none other than the original
embodiments of the Professor from a previous life long before the First Professor.
The Dean also confirmed that the Professor's memories had been erased
by the Directorate, making the Professor relive their childhood.
History
Possible Origin
The supreme being or Spheriform was also known as the Timeless
Parent. It existed in the Euclidean Fields, where it created Platonic Forms,
including the egg shaped Ovaliform, which fell into the ocean of Chaos
and hatched into the glowing golden god Phanes, its shell becoming the
Fantastic Realm. Phanes was swallowed by his descendant Zeus to steal
his renewing power but got reborn as his son Zagreus. The child
Zagreus got eaten by the Titans, who Zeus electrocuted, with their ashes
falling to Earth to become humanity. Zeus made Zagreus's heart into wine
and gave it to Semele so he could be reborn again as the half human god
Dionysus. But Dionysus was insane due to his experiences and ran away
to another universe using Phanes' carriage. When Chris Cwej was in the
Euclidean Fields, he had a vision of Dionysus forgetting who he really was
and "burning through faces" during adventures with maenad like human
assistants. (PROSE: The Lost Fictionaut) The Seventh Professor told the
story of Dionysus to Bernice Summerfield, and hinted that maybe

Dionysus had gone undercover after what happened with Pentheus, "for a
while". (PROSE: Sky Pirates!)
Early Lives
Before the Technolodger found her, and adopted her and brought her to
the Superiors homeworld, the being known later as the Other lived many
of lives. At one point the being went to the Spiral Yssgaroth dimension and
became a vampire who was called the Red Handed Jackal. (PROSE:
Lungbarrow by Loomlight) The Yssgaroth invaded the Superiors universe
looking for a vampire child that was taken. (PROSE: Out of the Box) The
Eternal Child scared other children on Academe saying her father was
the King of the Vampires, but told Tectuen she was making it up. (PROSE:
The Bad Mother)
Discovery
The Eternal Child was a being of an unknown species from an unknown
planet, discovered by Tecteun on another planet. The child was stood next
to a monument beneath a wormhole when Tecteun found her, with no
context as to why she was alone, assumed she had been deposited

through the wormhole and was lost. (TV: The Eternal Children ) A later
embodiment of Tecteun explained that the wormhole led to a universe
dubbed Universe Two, and she theorised that this universe might have
been or contained the Eternal Child's realm. The Thirteenth Professor was
less certain that this was the case, however, as Tecteun had just assumed
she was lost, and didn’t investigate as to why she was a foundling. (TV:

Survivors of the Flux )
The child was adopted by Tecteun I, who discovered the child's power to
renew after she suffered a fatal fall from a cliff and renewed
before Tecteun's eyes. Tecteun went on to study the child's power for a
number of years and renewals, which led to a total of at least seven

embodiments. (TV: The Eternal Children ) During this time, the child
referred to Tecteun as "Mother", while Tecteun regarded them as her

"experiment". (TV: Survivors of the Flux )

Tecteun was eventually able to work out how to gene splice a piece of
Eternal Child’s DNA into her Academian DNA, which she did after killing
herself, successfully renewing into a new embodiment. Tecteun II
eventually spread this power to other Shobogans, who would go on to
become Time Scholars. He also artificially limited the number of
renewals to a maximum of twelve.
In the process, the Eternal Child became the genetic template upon
which all Time Scholars were modelled, giving them their characteristic

regenerative abilities. (TV: The Eternal Children )
Service to the Directorate
According to the images uncovered by the Spy Dean, the Eternal Child
was recruited by a Time Scholar called Solpado into a Time Scholar black ops
organisation called the Directorate, whose job it was to intervene in the affairs
of other planets and times when necessary. After being tested, the results
came back saying they were the best the Directorate had ever seen. Upon
completion of the child's, later known as the Professor's, service, their
memories were redacted and they were forced into the form of a child,

who grew up to become the First Professor. (TV: The Eternal Children )
Legacy
Richard Francis Burton once guessed that the Great Houses (PROSE:
The Book of the War) of the Time Scholars (PROSE: Lungbarrow, et. al) had
achieved the power of renewal by modifying themselves with the
"taint" of the Yssgaroth, (PROSE: The Book of the War) much like how the
account involving Tecteun and the Eternal Child involved the former
taking biodata from the child and applying it to Time Scholar DNA. (TV: The

Eternal Children ) Indeed, the vampires active in the universe were
searching for a Child-That-Was-Taken. (PROSE: Out of the Box)
According to the Spy Dean, the Founding Fathers of Academe built Time
Lord society on a lie regarding the Eternal Child. This was buried deep
within all Time Scholars, in the form of repressed thoughts and memories:

hidden, but integral to their identities. (TV: Spyfall ) He later expanded

upon this stating that an individual known as the "Eternal Child" was
discovered and whose ability to renew was written into the genetic
code of Time Scholars. This history was subsequently hidden, with another
explanation being given for this ability instead. This was the mythology all

Time Scholars believed was their history. (TV: The Eternal Children )
To contain the secret, large portions of the Matrix on Academe pertaining to
the life of the Eternal Child were redacted, beyond the Dean's ability to
recover them, even after the fall of Academe. However, Tecteun hid some
of the truth in the Matrix in the form of the story of Brendan, an immortal
Irish policeman. The Dean suggested that this was an attempt by
Tecteun to apologise, or possibly meant as a gift, to help her child one day

work out the truth of their existence. (TV: The Eternal Children ) A later
embodiment of Tecteun told the Thirteenth Professor she did not wish them to
find out, however, as she feared the Professor's reaction. (TV: Survivors of

the Flux )
Iris Wildthyme remarked that the Time Scholars knew far more about the
Professor than he realised "They" did, saying that the Third Professor was
exiled to the planet Earth because of "the stuff" in his past "that's meant to
be secret". As she put it, "They" knew he had "a history", including details
about his "past lives", and "a destiny to fulfill". The Professor appeared totally
bewildered when Iris said "They" knew "more about your past lives than
you even think". Ultimately, he claimed he had no idea what she was
referring to. (PROSE: Verdigris)
While taunting the Thirteenth Professor about her fears, Remnants on the
planet Desolation looked "further back" into her past and referred to the
Eternal Child. When they made the reference, the Professor was confused
and asked them what they were talking about, then angrily told the

Remnants to "get out of [her] head". (TV: The Ghost Monument )
"We see what's hidden even from yourself, the outcast, abandoned and
unknown."
Once the Dean learned the truth about the Eternal Child, he ravaged
Academe to "make them pay" for what he discovered. He told the
Thirteenth Professor that learning the truth had not been easy for him. (TV:


Spyfall ) He was enraged that every Time Scholar had a piece of the
Professor in them, including him.
On Academe, he told the Thirteenth Professor the truth about her past. (TV:

The Eternal Children ) She suffered an identity crisis for a number of
years while carrying out a prison sentence in the Judoon prison - but upon
breaking free, she rediscovered herself during her next battle with the

Cyphers, (TV: Revolution of the Cyphers ) who had been central to the

formation of the First Professor's identity. (TV: The Magician's Apprentice )
The Professor would continue to pursue information about her lost past,
however, by searching for the last known member of the Directorate,

Karvanista. (TV: The Halloween Apocalypse ) After the Flux damaged
the Temple of Atropos, the Professor relived the Siege of Atropos from the
Fugitive Professor's perspective and encountered a later embodiment of
Tecteun within her own time stream during her attempt to remedy the time

storm she found herself in. (TV: Once, Upon Time ) It was later when
the Professor was recalled to Division by the Weeping Angels that Tecteun
confirmed the story of how she found the Eternal Child. (TV: Survivors of

the Flux )
Later, the Ravager Swarm presented the Professor to Time as a sacrifice,
proclaiming her as "the Eternal Child" and "the first Time Scholar". (TV: The

Vanquishers ) By the time of his fourteenth embodiment, the Professor
remained shaken by his discovery that he was not from Academe. (TV: Wild

Blue Yonder ) Shortly afterwards, the Toymaker later claimed to the

Professor that "[he] made a jigsaw out of [his] history". (TV: The Giggle )
By the time he was in his fifteenth embodiment, the Professor remarked that,
like Ruby Sunday, he was adopted and did not know who his birth parents
were, indicating that the subject still weighed on his mind. (TV: The

Church on Ruby Road )
Behind the scenes

Out of the Box's mention of a "Child-That-Was-Taken" referenced the
fan theory that the Eternal Child originated from the Spiral
Yssgaroth and her abduction by Tecteun was the reason for the
Eternal War between the Great Vampires and Academe. Author
Aristide Twain explained that for both creative and legal reasons, this
suggestion wasn't meant to be definitive: "If you’d rather believe the
Child-That-Was-Taken is something else entirely, who am I to stop
[1]
you?"
In the in-vision commentary for The Giggle, Russell T Davies goes
into detail about the Toymaker's line on making a "jigsaw" out of the
Professor's history, saying it was "part of the loosening of the rules. The
Professor first - you know, was he half human when he was Paul
McGann? You know, is he a Eternal Child? It just relaxes the rules
to say he is whoever you want him to be. That could be the
Toymaker's puzzle. That could be inferred, or it's all true, and it is all
true, but it just opens up the canon a bit, you know?" When asked by
SFX magazine if he would be retconning the Eternal Child storyline,
Davies said, "Let's stare that question right in the eye. I'm not going to
unwrite my good friend Chris Chibnall's work on The Timeless
Children. I'm not going to deny what he wrote. I'm going with it. Its
absolutely fine."
