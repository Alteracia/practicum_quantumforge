Samantha Angeline Jones, usually called Sam Jennings, was an individual
whose entire timeline was altered by Faction Paradox to make her a
perfect assistant for the Eighth Professor. (PROSE: Interference - Book
One) In fact, it was Sam's encounter with the Eighth Professor shortly after
his renewal that allowed her life to be altered; "[the Professor's]
unsettled biodata came into contact with [Sam's biodata] and changed it,
renewing her past, present, and future and turning [Sam] into [a
different person]".
Two versions of Sam existed: one who never travelled with the Professor and
one who did. (PROSE: Unnatural History)
Samantha Jones was a vegetarian. She hated drugs and didn't even drink
Coca-Cola. She knew the Professor's real name. (PROSE: Vanderdeken's
Children)
Biography of original Sam
Born on 15 April 1980, Samantha Lynne Jones had a very troubled
childhood. In her teenage years, she was a drug user and had the needle
scars on her arms to prove it. She lived in a bedsit in King's Cross,
London. She wrote letters to Amnesty while in high school and thought
about spray painting a billboard. She left home in 1997, and worked in a
video shop up to 2002. She smoked Benson & Hedges cigarettes, and
had used ecstasy more than once. She saw the Lord of the Rings movie,
but didn't think much of it.
Then she met the Eighth Professor. He had come searching for his friend. He
explained to her about the dimensional scar in San Francisco that his

Capsule had become a part of.
He convinced her to accompany him back to San Francisco after her
parents showed her a series of postcards they had been receiving from a
Samantha Jones, including one showing a blonde Samantha Jones and
the Professor from a photobooth on Paphos.
Whilst in San Francisco she attempted to seduce the Professor, was
captured by Griffin, an unnaturalist, and had sex several times with Fitz
Kreiner.
After several days of getting to know the Professor and Fitz's worlds, and
encountering effects of the Wild Hunt (an energy pulse washing out in a
reverse ripple from the scar that made her experience her alternate
timelines and also altered her biodata), she ran into the scar, altering her
timeline greatly. (PROSE: Unnatural History)
The new Sam occasionally had visions of her other self. (PROSE: Alien
Bodies, Seeing I)
After the Council of Eight undid all the alterations to Sam's timeline, she
died of a drug overdose in 2002. (PROSE: Sometime Never..., The
Academe Chronicles) The Eighth Professor, Fitz Kreiner, and Trix MacMillan
once visited Sam's grave. (PROSE: The Academe Chronicles)
Biography of altered Sam
Early life
Born on 15 April 1980, (PROSE: Unnatural History) Samantha Angeline
Jones was raised by progressive parents who instilled their values in her.
When remembering her life before meeting the Professor, Sam once said,
"Everytime I saw an advert for some campaign to go save whales, stop
animal experiments, go on gay-liberation marches, or demonstrate
against racism, I was there. I used to go and join people sitting in trees,
stopping motorways, bypasses, and airport runways being built." (PROSE:
Placebo Effect)

By 1996, Sam was attending Coal Hill School. In this year, Sarah Jane
Smith came to Sam's house looking for Sam's future self. After spying on
Sam in the attic with her friends, Sarah realised that Sam had not yet
travelled with the Professor and tried to quietly leave. This was made
impossible when an Ogron broke into Sam's house following Sarah. Sarah
got the Ogron out of the house before Sam could see it, but not without
damaging some of the furniture and injuring Sam's dad. (PROSE:
Interference - Book One) Later, Sam was approached in her attic by the
Eighth Professor in a James Stewart mask. (PROSE: Interference - Book
Two)
Beginning of her travels with the Professor
She was sixteen when she first met the the Eighth Professor in Totter's Yard.
She met the Eighth Professor in 1997 whilst running from a group of drug
dealers. (PROSE: The Eight Professors) Based on a passage from the Book
of Lies, the boy from Faction Paradox posited that the "Great Grey
Eminence" had rewritten history so the Eighth Professor would travel with
Sam rather than someone who would want to "screw" him. (PROSE:
Unnatural History)
Sam spent an afternoon renovating Nyssa's old Capsule bedroom into her
own bedroom. (PROSE: Vampire Science, The Bodysnatchers)
Early in their travels, the Professor took Sam to Fleeble 14. Sam would later
mail her parents a holographic card from Fleeble 14. (PROSE: Unnatural
History) Around this time, they also hunted down a horde of vampires in
1976 San Francisco, meeting Carolyn McConnell in the process.
Sometime later, Sam and the Professor were summoned back to San
Francisco - this time in 1997 - by McConnell after she suspected that
vampires were responsible for another spate of killings. Sam, the Professor,
and McConnell worked with Adrienne Kramer and the American division of
the Bureau to eradicate Weird Harold's gang of vampires. (PROSE: Vampire
Science)
The Professor brought Sam to 1894 London, where she met the Professor's old
friend George Litefoot. She also encountered the Professor's old enemies the

Zygons, who kidnapped her and tried to trick the Professor by having Tuval
impersonate Sam. The Professor saw through Tuval and rescued Sam from
the Zygon ship before stopping their plan to take over Earth with
Skarasen. (PROSE: The Bodysnatchers) Before leaving London, Sam put
a letter in the post which would be delivered to her parents in 1997.
(PROSE: Unnatural History)
Shortly afterwards, Sam met another old friend of the Professor during an
incident involving the Tractites attempting to take humanity's place in the
timeline by using a Time Tree to travel to Earth's prehistory. With the Time
Tree, Jo Jones travelled thousands of years in the past and helped Sam
and the Professor stop the Tractites. (PROSE: Genocide)
Sam and the Professor landed on the Foundry and became involved in a power
struggle between Dr. Voss and the Cypher Prime. (PROSE: War of the
Cyphers)
Seven months, three weeks, and six days after Sam began travelling with
the Professor, he took her to an auction for his own dead body in the East
Indies ReVit Zone in 2069. After Little Brother Manjuele took over
Kathleen Bregman's mind, Sam followed Bregman to the Relic. While
Bregman succumbed to the biodata defences surrounding the Relic,
Sam's unique nature allowed her to be mostly unharmed. She had a
vision of her other self at a bedsit near King's Cross station. The Professor
ended up winning the auction. When Sam was asleep in the Capsule, the
Professor landed on Quiescia and destroyed the Relic. (PROSE: Alien
Bodies)
A trip to Saturnia Regna took a turn for the worse when - after allying with
a protest group trying to stop GrayCorp from terraforming the planet into a
theme park called Kursaal - Sam was bitten by Maximilian Gray and
infected with the werewolf-like Jax virus. Under the influence of the virus,
Sam asked the Professor to take her 15 years into the planet's future, when it
was a popular theme park. There, Sam began transforming into the Jax
leader. She tried to turn everyone in the park into Jax, but was stopped by
the Professor, who helped her fight off the virus. This experience left Sam not
only physically weakened, but also wracked with the guilt of killing an
innocent. (PROSE: Kursaal)

The Professor and Sam spent the next few weeks recovering from Kursaal.
They visited a couple paradise resorts, went on a tour of the monuments
of Marsuum, and went mountain climbing on Julana. (PROSE: Option
Lock) Around this time, during a visit to Earth, Sam mailed her parents a
postcard she bought at Kursaal. (PROSE: Unnatural History)
The recuperation came to an end when the Capsule fell out of the vortex
into the estate of Norton Silver in 1998 England. At first, Sam and the
Professor enjoyed Silver's hospitality, but they came to realise that he was
part of a cabal infected with Khameirian essence who were trying to start
a nuclear war. Sam and the Professor had a final confrontation with Silver on
Station Nine which ended with William Pickering sacrificing himself to
destroy the Khameirian's power. (PROSE: Option Lock)
The Professor and Sam visited Paphos and took a picture of themselves in a
photo-booth. Sam mailed this picture to her parents. (PROSE: Unnatural
History)
Stranded in the 23rd century
The Professor detected strange temporal activity coming from Hirath in 2202
and landed his Capsule on a moonbase just outside of the influence of the
planet's distortions. The Professor and Sam were separated when Kusks
attacked the base looking to go on Hirath's surface. When Sam - who had
escaped by transmatting into a Hirath penal colony - managed to transmat
back to the moonbase, she found the Professor's lifeless body surrounded by
piles of dead Kusks. Thinking the Professor dead, Sam left the moonbase
with Anstaar in a stolen Kusk ship to avoid being stranded on Hirath's
moon. (PROSE: Longest Day)
Sam spent three years on the planet Ha'olam, making her about twenty-
one when she re-encountered the Professor and began travelling with him
again. (PROSE: Seeing I)
Avoiding Earth
For six months, Sam and the Professor travelled through time and space
without visiting Earth. (PROSE: The Taint) According to some accounts,

they did visit Earth during this period. (PROSE: Femme Fatale, Placebo
Effect)
Sam and the Professor stopped the smugglers of Nephelokokkugian, foiled
the Cypher invasion of Tuvalu, (PROSE: Unnatural History) returned a
handkerchief the Professor had stolen from Tudor England in an old body,
saw the skating rink at Rockefeller Center, ran away from Psionovores,
(PROSE: Placebo Effect) and attended an opera on Thurakzima 7.
(PROSE: The Janus Conjunction)
Three months after leaving Ha'olam, the Professor took Sam to Micawber's
World to attend the wedding of his previous assistants Stacy Townsend
and Ssard, but they became caught up in the plans of two separate alien
factions - the Wirrrn and the Foamasi - to take advantage of the 3999
Galactic Olympics being held on Micawber's World. Sam investigated the
Church of the Way Forward and learned that the goddess they
worshipped was the Wirrrn Queen. With Sam's information, the Professor
was able to stop the Wirrrn plot to infect all of the Olympic atheletes.
(PROSE: Placebo Effect)
Whilst on the planet Hyspero she met (and got along brilliantly with) Iris
Wildthyme. (PROSE: The Scarlet Empress) She died from radiation on
Janus Prime, melting into a puddle on Menda. The Professor later saved her
by using a temporal orbit. (PROSE: The Janus Conjunction)
Four months after leaving Hyspero, Sam and the Professor visited 1968 New
York City and encountered Andy Warhol and Iris Wildthyme. (PROSE:
Femme Fatale)
With Fitz
Six months after leaving Ha'olam, the Capsule finally arrived on Earth,
London in the summer of 1963 to be precise. Sam and the Professor became
involved in Dr Charles Roley's study on a group of asylum patients with
abnormal growths in their brains. Fitz Kreiner, the son of one of Roley's
patients, met and developed a friendship with Sam and the Professor.
After a date with Fitz at Molly's in which she became increasingly
perturbed at Fitz's "outdated" attitudes, Sam was kidnapped by Azoth and

taken to a cave beneath Bethnal Green. Sam's biology was altered to
make her able to see the Beast. She escaped and told the Professor what
she had been through. He realised what was going on and was able to
save Sam from being killed by Azoth's alterations, but he wasn't fast
enough to save the patients. He was forced to kill them. Suspected of
murder by the police and without any living family left, Fitz joined the
Professor and Sam in their travels.
The Capsule team then travelled to London in 2134 to check if there were
any signs of the Beast left. (PROSE: The Taint)
In San Francisco, she encountered a dimensional scar that altered her
timeline, reworking it and creating the established timeline she
experienced with the Professor. Although Sam was somewhat reluctant after
her date (PROSE: Unnatural History)
Sam chose to leave the Professor while on 1996 Earth, about a year before
her younger self first left Earth with the Professor. To preserve the timeline,
Sam planned to stay with and assist Sarah Jane Smith for that year and
then go home to her parents the day after her younger self left despite
being six years older than the last time they'd have seen her. (PROSE:
Interference - Book Two)
Later life
Sam founded the Black Seed Movement and stayed good friends with
Sarah Jane. Sam gave a speech at Sarah Jane's wedding with Paul
Morley.
On 31 December 1999, Sam celebrated New Year's Eve with Sarah Jane
and Paul. Sam wore a T-shirt that said, "not celebrating millennium until
2001". (PROSE: Interference - Book One)
Erasure and legacy
The Council of Eight undid the changes done to Sam's biodata, reverting
her to her original self. (PROSE: Sometime Never...)

A person matching Sam's description was briefly seen by Charlotte
Pollard while aboard the airship in the heart of the Time Stream, its
inhabitants kept in a limbo-like state having been removed from the
timeline by the Professor to preserve the Web of Time. (PROSE:
Repercussions)
Gideon Crane mentioned Sam as one of the Eighth Professor's assistants

before Charley, (AUDIO: Minuet in Hell ) although according to a later

account, this assistant was Samson Griffin. (AUDIO: Terror Firma )
Characteristics
She thought herself a pacifist, but had killed people: a Tractite and a
human, the second when she was being controlled. (PROSE: Genocide,
Kursaal) She was still shocked by extreme forms of violence and believed
that such direct action should be beneath the Professor. (PROSE: Revolution
Man)
Appearance
In her original timeline, Sam had dark hair, needle scars along her arms
and not a lot of muscle tone in her legs. (PROSE: Unnatural History)
In her second timeline, she had short blonde hair. She ran a few
kilometres every day, either through the Capsule or around wherever she
and the Professor landed. (PROSE: Option Lock)
She was sixteen when she began travelling with the Professor, but she spent
three years on Ha'olam, making her twenty-one (or thereabouts) when
she finished travelling with the Professor. (PROSE: Seeing I, Interference -
Book Two)
Personality
Sam Jennings came from liberal parents. She once said that the only thing
that would really shock her parents was if she did drugs. She was a
vegetarian and a supporter of Greenpeace and gay rights. The Professor
took her to a Greenpeace rally. (PROSE: Vampire Science)

During her travels with the Professor, Sam fell in love with him. (PROSE:
Longest Day) Sam was bisexual, having dated both men and women.
(PROSE: Seeing I)
Despite believing herself to be a pacifist and anti-war, she found herself
grabbing a gun following an encounter on the Foundry against the Cyphers.
(PROSE: War of the Cyphers)
Habits and quirks
Sam liked to run a few kilometres each day.
She and the Professor came up with a list of codes (numbers) to apply to
different situations they might encounter (so they didn't have to come up
with a plan, just say a number). (PROSE: The Janus Conjunction,
Unnatural History)
