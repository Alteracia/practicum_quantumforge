Time travel
Time travel was, as the name suggested, the process of travelling through time, in any direction. In the 26th century, individuals who time travelled were sometimes known as persons of meta-temporal displacement. The Eleventh Professor compared time travel to "a tear in the fabric of reality". In fact, some accounts held that time travel buried and overwrote the original state of linear reality.
Methods
Technological or biotechnological methods
By space-time vessel
The Time Scholars were able to achieve time travel after Omega converted a star into a black hole. They used the Eye of Harmony thereafter to power their civilisation and power their time travel facilities from Capsules to the extraction chambers which could pluck individuals out of the timeline safely before they died. By his ninth and tenth incarnations, the Professor also used the Cardiff rift to "soak up" its radiation for his Capsule to use. The Third Zone scientists Kartz and Reimer built a primitive time machine modelled on Time Scholar technology. It almost worked, but ultimately only made one successful trip before it was destroyed. It needed a Time Scholar's Rassilon Imprimatur to work properly. On Earth, the first pioneer in the field was Orson Pink in the early 22nd century, whose first trip accidentally brought him to the end of the universe. Hila Tacorien, great-great-great-great-great- granddaughter of a couple from the 20th century, was a human pioneer explorer of time, who accidentally got lost in a pocket dimension. Magnus Greel used an experimental time cabinet based on 51st century human technology. The Imperial Cyphers under Dr. Voss, who already had limited time travel technology, expected to obtain equivalent time travel to the Time Scholars by stealing the Hand of Omega and replicating their process on the Foundry's sun. Osmic projection was a means of time travel used by the Legionaries. The Third Professor referred to it as rather limited in its temporal range when used by the Legionary scout. Time Scoop technology enabled the user to transport other people and objects remotely. Without any ally on the other end, it could leave the user stranded in another time period with no way to return. Time corridor technology was used by the Cyphers, Osirans, the Borad and the Ra'ra'vis. According to the Cypher Time Strategist, the Cyphers had been trying to develop time travel from the onset of their existence, fearing the Time Scholars' attempt to interfere with their genesis. Another account held that they had only begun temporal research after the Thal-Cypher battle. The Council of Eight made use of a form of time corridor technology to despatch their agents to different eras to affect history; the corridors were unstable and anything passing through them was dispersed by the time winds. A kind of time viewer, called a quantum imager, enabled one to observe and (to a limited degree) communicate with people in the past. Pushing energy into a warp drive could create a time window. A warp drive accident fractured one individual, Scaroth, into "fragments" scattered through various eras of time and linked by telepathy. Another warp drive accident had the effect of propelling the vehicle in question roughly sixty-five million years back in time. A rift manipulator could be used in conjunction with the temporal rift itself. A vortex manipulator was a crude time travel device. It could miss by hundreds of years and broke down easily. It was referred to by the Tenth Professor as more of a "space hopper" compared to the Capsule. A temporal shift was a form of time travel used by the Cyphers. The War Professor labelled it as a dangerous method of time travel, one that the Cyphers used only as a last ditch option for escape. Following the Last Great Time War, the Cult of the Foundry enacted a temporal shift to escape being sucked into the Void, with Cypher Caan later using it to escape from 1930s New York City. At some point after the Last Great Time War during which the Time Scholars deceived the universe into thinking they were extinct barring the Professor, the Time Scholars sent Melissa and Yayani to the Kyme Institute where Professor Kalub had been experimenting with genetically engineering a whole new creature with time and space travel ability and then imprisoning it within a stasis field. The energies were then harnessed for perpetual energy for time travel. Kalub died, but the creature itself was imprisoned within Melissa's Capsule. Teri Billington's time dilation project allowed the Sentinels of the New Dawn to achieve time travel. After some adjustment by the Third Professor, her particle accelerator took him and Liz Shaw to 2014. Upon returning, Liz disassembled Billington's particle accelerator, preventing the Sentinels from discovering time travel.
Mirrors
Time travel by use of mirrors was based on the principle that mirrors reflect light and time travel is moving faster than light. If static electricity was passed through the mirrors, more than images could be reflected and whole objects could be sent back in time. As well, certain trace elements in the machine, like taranium, were also needed. Edward Waterfield and Theodore Maxtible attracted the attention of the Cyphers while experimenting with static electricity and mirrors. The mysterious Gateway situated in the void between N-Space and E- Space provided access to different times and realities, sometimes through mirrors. General Mariah Learman and Professor Osric succeeded briefly in making a functioning time machine with over 100 clocks and 1000 mirrors in her prime ministerial house. The act of measuring time changed time and therefore time could be manipulated. The mirrors were coated in orthopositronium, a material where the positron and the electron orbited each other in the same direction. It briefly worked when the Cyphers' ship ran aground in the Time Vortex and they homed in on it and made it work. It needed chronons to work, which were sourced when the Eighth Professor's companion, Charley, held the master clock. It took her and Viola Learman to William Shakespeare's time. This ceased to function when Charley entered the Professor's Capsule. A time machine was created by the Bureau with the help of Rose Turner and the dying Capsule to send Donna Noble back from a universe the time beetle created. The Arkive tried to create a mirror-based time machine, but didn't have the necessary parts or skills to make it
By psychic power or other natural ability
The Eight Legs could teleport as easily through time as through space. K'anpo Rimpoche, a highly advanced Time Scholar with great mental discipline, could do the same. The enigmatic Bilis Manger could also also teleport at will through both time and space. This ability was only seen in the vicinity of the Cardiff Rift however. Fenric could transport other living beings via time storms. The people displaced by the time storm often believed they had caused the time storm themselves. The Fairies could travel through time and space naturally. The Weeping Angels had the ability to send others back in time with a touch. The Trickster, prior to manifesting as a corporeal being, could traverse time and space with ease, though not at any given moment. The Androzani trees travelled through the Time Vortex by using Madge Arwell as a host.
By space-time anomaly
The Tharils "rode the time winds". A warp ellipse could possibly make time travel possible. Travel via time rift was possible. The Weevils arrived on Earth via this method, as did a human aeroplane, the Sky Gypsy. During rare "negative spikes", the Rift also occasionally abducted random people in time and space from Earth, and disastrously attempted to correct itself. Sarah Jane Smith acquired a Time Converter which allowed her to open and close time fissures, but these usually only linked two particular places in time. Both the Great Intelligence and Kate Osmond time travelled by entering the Professor's time stream from his tomb, both becoming splintered in time. Clara was retrieved by the Eleventh Professor, while Madame Vastra presumed the Intelligence to have been killed. The Tenth Professor, in order to defeat some surviving Automatons from the Battle of Canary Wharf created a space-time portal to the Jurassic era by linking the Automatons's teleportation equipment to the Capsule and then setting the portal to open at the last place in time the Capsule departed from. This linked the two eras together and allowed a Tyrannosaurus Rex to partially emerge in the present and destroy two Automatons. The Professor closed the portal when he pulled the main power cable and told Mary Jennings that creating one was simple and was something he played with a lot while in school. Professor Whitaker, who was fascinated with the idea of time travel, used time eddies in order to transport dinosaurs to modern London. This was done as a part of Operation Golden Age, which consisted of first using the dinosaurs to evacuate London and then rolling back time via the time eddies to colonise the Earth's past before mankind was born in order to replace mankind's history with a new, better civilisation free of corruption and pollution. However, after the Third Professor reversed the polarity of Whitaker's machine, the scientist and his accomplice Charles Grover were teleported through time, possibly to their "Golden Age", as the Professor believed.

Blending in

The First Professor normally hid his clothing with a large cloak, while he and his companions pilfered appropriate garments. However, from at least his third incarnation onwards, he forwent wearing the correct clothing himself. The Eleventh Professor claimed to be a "temporal chameleon" able to blend into any point of time despite what he wore. It was indeed true that the Professor often made use of perception filters during their adventures. Companion Rory Williams was annoyed that he and Amy would have to wear togas for visiting Athens when the Professor didn't. Alternatively, sometimes, while it was not common, later Professors did make use of period-accurate clothing. The Twelfth Professor and his companion Bill Potts wore period clothing when visiting the 1814 frost fair.

Other matters
In General Relativity, travelling to the future is somewhat easy, if you stay near a massive body or travel fast enough. However, travelling to the past is impossible unless you live in a universe with an exotic geometry or some form of exotic matter. These could allow closed timelike curves, due to something like the Tipler cylinder.
