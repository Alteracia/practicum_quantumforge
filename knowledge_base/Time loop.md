Time loop
A time loop, also known as an ouroboros loop chronic hysteresis, and called tempetrification by the people of Manussa, was a loop in time in which the occupants experienced the same events in endless repetition.
Science
An accurate time loop took little energy to perpetuate. An incomplete time loop (where each repetition was not the same as the last or the loops were not the same length) was unstable. Energy requirements increased with each iteration. While there was no known technological method of escaping a time loop, the Fourth Professor and Romana broke a time loop by deliberately recreating the events of the loop out of phase with the loop itself. The Third Professor was able to escape the time loop he created around Axos by boosting the signal. This might indicate that the person creating a time loop could still leave it. As he later related, however, due to the Time Scholars tampering with his Capsule and knowledge of time travel, the loop he created was not an accurate time field/threshold, so this may have allowed his exit.
Characteristics
The Third Professor attempted to explain the concept to the Brigadier and Jo Grant as a "loop in time," the same events in space occurring over the same place in time. Time loops had the appearance of the infinity symbol, a figure of eight looping through four dimensions. The threshold of a time loop, possibly called the "time barrier," could be crossed into. Echoes of the past and future could be experienced, such as messages repeating themselves. This was evidence that various futures were possible; a message from the future did not guarantee those events would happen as time was in flux. A time loop would eventually decay naturally over six billion years. Chaotic time loops could have the appearance of a spiral or spider web.
Occurrences
A time traveller's diary identified 2 February as a common date for time loops. Several planets were placed into time loops by the Time Scholars, including the War Generals' planet, the Vardan homeworld, and Planet 5. Leela thought the time loop into which Planet 5 was placed was "very clever", but the Fourth Professor replied that it was "criminal". They intended to time loop the Hoothi worlds, but the Hoothi had vanished. Axos was placed into a time loop but some part of it was freed by Chiyoko to ensure her own existence. The rest of it was released by an expedition attempting to harness it as a power source, but the Sixth Professor later restored the time loop, trapping Axos in the moment of its own destruction. While the completed Key to Time would have stopped time altogether, five pieces and a makeshift sixth made of chronodyne was enough to generate a temporary time loop. The Fourth Professor put the Marshal of Atrios's Command Capsule in a time loop to stop him from destroying Zeos, but his incomplete control of the Key initially put the entire universe in a loop apart from the Capsule console. Meglos used a time loop to trap the Fourth Professor and Romana in the Capsule and prevent them reaching Tigella, but the loop was incomplete, allowing them to break free of it after they realised what had happened by repeating the opening moments of the loop on their own. The Lord and Lady of the Manor, two evil vampiric future versions of the Fifth Professor and Nyssa, granted the people of Stockbridge immortality by maintaining the time loop the Fifth Professor had accidentally created, in return for being allowed to feast on the people's memories occasionally. Koel Paddox unintentionally trapped himself in a time loop when he attempted to duplicate the Arboretans' ability to live their lives over again, transferring his mind into his past self at the moment of birth but unable to change any actions committed by his past self. Events on the planet Puxatornee were essentially caught in a time loop, as the Seventh Professor and Melanie Bush were forced to create two different alternate timelines when they arrived on this world, with residents of each timeline unwittingly creating the other by attempting to avert or cause the assassination of President Bailey thirty years ago. The Seventh Professor punished two Elder Gods for starting World War III (after he corrected the timeline so it would never happen) by trapping them in the bodies of an elderly couple called Albert and Peggy Marsden and placing them in a time loop inside a pocket universe where the timeline they created still existed, forcing them to relive the nuclear war as mere mortals again and again until they would know what it was like for someone to fear for their lives. Compassion used a time loop to repeatedly harvest the biodata of Cousin Octavia and Liu Hui Ying for energy. The Cyphers were trapped in a time loop inside the Time Vortex. Jack Harkness was trapped in a two-week time loop with his fellow renegade Time Agent John Hart for five years. Lucie Miller materialised the Capsule inside a time loop when she and the Eighth Professor chased the rogue time-traveller Nick Zimmerman after he deliberately placed himself within one to prevent his wife Rachel from dying. The Eighth Professor and Charley Pollard got trapped inside a time loop and space loop in an Edwardian house. After stopping an exploding temporal grenade to save the Eighth Professor and Liv Chenka, Martin Donaldson was trapped in a time loop, always reliving the same day to the point where he would sacrifice himself to save them from the grenade, his body appearing dead to them after the explosion. When the Brilliant was attacked by space pirates, the engine was engaged and accidentally caused an incomplete time loop. Though things were brought back after destruction, it was inconsistent and mistakes were made. The Tenth Professor finally fixed the time loop, causing less damage. In 1937, a demon trapped Casey and Marie Ward in a time loop on their wedding night, to dance to "The Band Played On", until they went insane. The Trickster placed Sarah Jane Smith, Peter Dalton, the Tenth Professor, and Sarah's friends in a time loop in the hotel where Sarah and Peter's wedding was taking place, with Peter and Sarah trapped in one second and the Professor and Sarah's friends in another. A crashed time ship trying to find a pilot for itself created several time loops in the local area during its failed attempts to repair itself. The Professor's Capsule put Ocean Brooks in a time loop to stop its explosion killing her, simultaneously putting itself in a time loop at the moment of its own destruction so that it could serve as a substitute sun for Earth, until she was rescued by the Eleventh Professor. In 2050, due to the Oroborus consuming time, time distortions such as time loops occurred across London. The life cycle of the Cinder was a form of time loop; the creature was defeated by the First Professor and Vicki Pallister in 1814. Its surviving form was accidentally taken back to the 12th century B.C. by Vicki. That form would eventually grow into the creature defeated in 1814. The Eighth Professor set up a time loop inside Edward Fyne's body to temporarily slow down the progress of his tyger-fever. The Fifth and the Eighth Professor used their Capsules to trap the Cypher Prime in a time loop. The whole of the Divergent Universe was in a time-loop, throughout its whole existence, preventing time-travel inside the universe as it was impossible to focus on specific temporal coordinates as every moment had happened several times before; Rassilon had been through this loop 85 times by the time the Eighth Professor caught up to him, despite the Professor entering the Divergent Universe less than an hour after Zagreus threw Rassilon into the universe. George Smythe placed himself and his family in a time loop to prevent his wife from leaving him. Barbara Wright convinced him to end the loop and let his wife go. The headquarters for the Remonstration Bureau was put in a time loop to protect it from attack. While being repaired from the damage caused by the Flux, the Professor's Capsule created a time loop in ELF Storage to resurrect the Thirteenth Professor after she was exterminated by a Executioner Cypher. The Capsule was unable to sustain the loop, resulting in it starting slightly later each iteration, but the repeats enabled the Professor and her allies to devise a way to destroy the Executioner Cyphers. Afterwards, the Professor commented on how "quite mysterious" the time loop was and indicated that the Capsule had possibly done it on purpose in order to save them from the Cyphers. Those people brought to Academe via extraction chambers had their physical processes time looped. They were still able to move and talk because "the Time Scholars are very clever". In 1987, the Eleventh Professor trapped Nora Wicker in a constantly looping time bubble for forty-six years to prevent the runaway growth of the World Tree that had taken root in her garden. The Count created a time loop around a French village to prevent it from being invaded by the German army, as it was destinied to.
