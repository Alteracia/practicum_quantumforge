The Professor, throughout many of their lives, possessed a sonic
screwdriver. They ostensibly upgraded and improved each subsequent
model, improving functionality and adding additional features while the
design changed. Features included projecting sound waves to lock or
unlock doors, frying circuitry, hacking, disabling, and activating technology.
It could also be used as a scanning device, with medical applications.
Each model used the same software. Essentially, the sonic probe in
use by the War Professor was the same as that in the Eleventh Professor's

possession, some 400 years later. (TV: The Day of the Professor )
At least one version of the screwdriver used a crystal similar to the
Metebelis crystal sought after by the Eight Legs of Metebelis III, (COMIC:

The Forgotten ) while the Fifth Professor attempted to collect a diothanine
crystal from the planet Argentia where temporal zone met to focus sonic
waveforms for a new sonic probe. (AUDIO: Serpent in the Silver
Mask) There were also electrical components. (COMIC: The Halls of
Sacrifice)
The screwdriver had a multitude of settings and different versions of
settings. The Tenth Professor told Rose to use "setting 15B" to triangulate the


source of the ghosts (TV: Army of Ghosts ) and used 34-H to sink a

ship. (COMIC: Second Wave ) It had a setting 85 that undid security

codes to unlock doors. (TV: The Lazarus Experiment ) The Ninth Professor
told Rose to use setting 2428D to re-attach barbed wire. (TV: The Professor

Dances ) Sarah Jane used the Theta Omega setting to melt plastic

vines. (TV: The Android Invasion ) The Twelfth Professor told Clara to use
setting 41 to send out random bursts of helicon energy to distract the

Skovox Blitzer. (TV: The Caretaker ) The Twelfth Professor himself used
setting 61 to knock out a cybernetically augmented dinosaur with a "sonic

brainstorm". (COMIC: Spirits of the Jungle ) However, at least one
version of the screwdriver was able to use thoughts as well as settings.
  
(TV: The Big Bang , Amy's Choice , Let's Kill Hitler , Death in

Heaven ) The same version could also be activated using the Professor's

voice. (TV: Deep Breath )
The different versions of the Professor's sonic probes exhibited
different capabilities and uses, such as medical diagnostics (TV: The

Empty Child ) and repair of organic parts; (TV: The Vampires of Venice

) cutting, but also re-attaching materials such as barbed wire; (TV: The

Professor Dances ) operating Earth machinery such as computers (TV:

School Reunion ) and even cash machines (at regular and high eject

speeds); (TV: The Runaway Bride ) creating a spark to light a candle

(TV: The Girl in the Fireplace ) or Bunsen burner; (TV: Evolution of the

Cyphers ) opening and holding doors with acoustic locks; (TV: The Rings

of Akhaten ) and, on the rare occasion, driving screws without touching
  
them. (TV: The War Games , The Ark in Space , The Professor's Wife )
River Brooks told the Fourth Professor that, though his current screwdriver was
mostly just a screwdriver, he would later "go a bit crazy with the optional
upgrades". (AUDIO: Someone I Once Knew)
Although it was primarily a tool, the sonic probe could also be used
as a defensive weapon, able to combine with other sonic devices to form
a shield around a hostile target to either force them back (TV: The Day of

 
the Professor ) or slow their advance. (TV: The Professor Falls ) The Tenth
Professor put it in a soundboard to destroy the Robot Santas by overloading

their sensors. (TV: The Runaway Bride ) The Eleventh Professor used it to
bounce sound waves off a knife held by Melody Pond, knocking it out of

her hand. (TV: Let's Kill Hitler ) The sonic probe was also capable
of holding off sound waves from creatures who relied on sound in order to

attack such as the Vigil. (TV: The Rings of Akhaten )
The sonic probe would, in rare occasions, be used as an offensive
weapon, firing energy blasts. The Eleventh Professor used it in this manner
to help River Brooks defeat a group of Silents, defeating at least two of

them. (TV: Day of the Moon ) The Twelfth Professor used the same model
of screwdriver to blast a Hyperion, buying himself and others time to
escape from it. (COMIC: The Hyperion Empire) It could also destroy ice

creatures when it received the latest update. (TV: The Snowmen )
The Tenth Professor claimed that the device could not be used to wound,
 
maim or kill living things. (TV: Doomsday , The Professor's Daughter )
Despite this, the Third Professor used the device to incapacitate Space
Greyhounds by disrupting their brain functions, (COMIC: The Forgotten

) the Eighth Professor once claimed it could destroy a Cypher's brain if held
directly against the casing when activated, (PROSE: War of the Cyphers)
and the Twelfth Professor used setting 61 to knock out a cybernetically
augmented dinosaur with a "sonic brainstorm". (COMIC: Spirits of the

Jungle ) It could destroy non-living objects or mechanisms or place
living creatures in circumstances where they might die if the situation

required. (TV: The Christmas Invasion ) The War Professor claimed that it
was a scientific instrument rather than a water pistol, (TV: The Day of the

Professor ) or indeed a magic wand. (PROSE: The Day of the Professor)

Particularly during the Time War (AUDIO: The Satanic Mill , The
Thousand Worlds, The Heart of the Battle, A Thing of Guile) and in their

thirteenth embodiment, (COMIC: A New Beginning , TV: The Battle of

Ranskoor Av Kolos ) the Professor was known on occasion to summon the
Capsule using their sonic probe, though the Thirteenth Professor noted

that the Capsule got "prickly" at being summoned too often, as she found

it demeaning. (COMIC: A New Beginning )
For a time, the Twelfth Professor abandoned sonic probes in favour of
wearable technology - namely, his sonic sunglasses. (TV: The Witch's
 
Familiar , Under the Lake ) However, after briefly returning to Academe
and having Clara Osmond dematerialise with them still inside her Capsule,
he resumed using screwdrivers, as his Capsule fashioned a new one for
him as a gift. Furthermore, after the Professor had lost sight of who he was,
Clara left him a reminder to "be a Professor" after leaving his company. As
part of his role of being a Professor was to use a sonic probe, the

Professor accepted the Capsule's gift (TV: Hell Bent ) though he would
continue to occasionally use the sunglasses as well. (TV: The Husbands

of River Brooks )

The Third, Fourth and Fifth Professors' sonic probe

The Third Professor's sonic. (TV: Carnival of Monsters )
The Third Professor's most-used model of the sonic probe was much
larger than the one his first and second embodiments used; its elaborately
detailed silver collar originally featured black and yellow stripes and red
trim, and could be pulled down to activate the screwdriver. It had a
removable burgundy emitter head which the Professor would change with

others, each performing a different function. (TV: The Sea Devils )
On one occasion he removed the head entirely and replaced it with a
small, round mirror, which spun rapidly when he tapped its edge with his
finger and hypnotised anyone who looked at it. Accompanied by a yellow
light on the collar of his sonic probe and the Professor singing a
Venusian lullaby, it hypnotised the aggressive Aggedor into calmness.

(TV: The Curse of Peladon )
The Third Professor later fitted the head of his sonic probe with a
cylindrical black magnet which enabled it to open bolted doors, especially
when its polarity was reversed. This refit left the metal mostly unpainted,
with a dark red emitter ring. The head of this model could be extended.
The sonic probe was confiscated when the Professor was sent to the

Lunar Penal Colony, and he never recovered it. (TV: Frontier in Space )
However, the Professor had a spare aboard the Capsule, which he made use

of in the Cypher base on Spiridon. (TV: Planet of the Cyphers )

The Fourth Professor with the sonic. (TV: The Legionary Experiment)
Before this model met its demise, its head was repainted twice after the
original coat wore off. (TV: The Keeper of Traken, Castrovalva) It was
ultimately destroyed when the Terileptil leader forced the Fifth Professor to
drop the screwdriver inside a holding cell, then incinerated it with a blast
from a laser gun — causing the Professor to remark "I feel as though you've

just killed an old friend." (TV: The Visitation )
The Professor went without a sonic probe for some time after this
model, with Nyssa lamenting his decision not to replace it. (TV:

Snakedance ) The Tenth Professor also lightly mocked his past embodiment
for going "hands-free" following this incident when their Capsules
accidentally collided. (TV: Time Crash)
This sonic operated at a frequency of 80 kilohertz. (PROSE: Light
Fantastic)
The Professor, leaving the Bureau, left behind a prototype. the Bureau scientists then
tried to use reverse-engineering to understand its workings; this went on
into the 21st century. Despite them trying to keep it quiet from the Professor,
he was well aware of the project by his seventh embodiment. (AUDIO:
Persuasion)
Multiple versions of this model were kept in the Twelfth Professor's office at
St Luke's University. His assistant Nardole made use of one in a war

zone where the Cyphers and Movellans skirmished. (TV: The Pilot )
Uses

The sonic probe was often used for opening objects such as doors,
  
(TV: The Sea Devils , The Mutants , The Green Death , etc.) safes
(TV: The Sun Makers) and display cases. (TV: The Ribos Operation)
According to the Third Professor, it, for a time, only worked on electronic

locks. (TV: Carnival of Monsters ) However, the Fourth Professor later
modified it to also work on physical ones as well. (TV: The Ribos
Operation, etc.)
The sonic probe could also be used to disable security devices,

including a holographic energy barrier (TV: The Visitation ) and, once, a
deflection barrier generator. (TV: Pyramids of Mars) It could also scan for
alarm systems, (TV: Colony in Space) radiation (TV: The Three Professors)

and electronic traps. (TV: Death to the Cyphers )
On occasion, the Professor used it to either detect, disarm or detonate

explosive weapons. (TV: The Sea Devils , PROSE: Cold Fusion, etc.)
He often used it to interact with or disable various forms of circuitry and
 
technology. (TV: The Mutants , Frontier in Space , Planet of the

Cyphers , etc.) The sonic probe could also be used to repair

devices (TV: The Ark in Space , The Legionary Stratagem, etc.) and
perform maintenance on the Capsule's control console. (TV: The Hand of
Fear)
The sonic probe also functioned as a conventional screwdriver (TV:

The Monster of Peladon, The Ark in Space ) and could be magnetised
 
to remove bolts. (TV: Frontier in Space , The Android Invasion ) With
the screwdriver, the Professor was also capable of creating sparks of flame,

(TV: Carnival of Monsters ) cutting through objects, (TV: Robot) melting

artificial ivy (TV: The Android Invasion ) and creating loud noises. (TV:
Four to Doomsday)
The sonic probe also affected alive beings: the Third Professor used it
to cure Henry VIII of his cataracts, (PROSE: Hiccup in Time) distract giant

maggots, (TV: The Green Death ) break a hypnotic trance (TV: Death to


the Cyphers ) and overload the brains of Space Greyhounds. (COMIC:

The Forgotten )
Using the appropriate sonic frequency, the Fourth Professor used the sonic
screwdriver to return himself, Ernestina Stott and later, his scarf, to normal
size. (AUDIO: The Dead Shoes)
Used by the Sixth Professor
The Sixth Professor once carried an unspecified replacement model for the
sonic probe, but had to leave it tied to the side of a video camera in
a dungeon cell for it to keep the visual feed disabled. (PROSE: The
Nightmare Fair) He later carried another sonic model during his
adventures with Crystal, Jason, and Zog. (AUDIO: The Ultimate

Adventure )
Uses
The Sixth Professor's screwdriver could be used to disable a video camera
indefinitely, (PROSE: The Nightmare Fair) close and seal Cypher doors,
and repair the Capsule control console. He once attempted to use it to
free the American Envoy from the Cypher laser wall, but this failed.

The Seventh Professor acquired a sonic probe at an unspecified point
of his life. Many contradictory accounts existed, including one which
claimed he had acquired it at some point before meeting Bernice
Summerfield, (AUDIO: Love and War) and another, which claimed he
acquired it from himself on a parallel Earth where the species Homo
Reptilicus had conquered the Earth and killed his third embodiment.
(PROSE: Blood Heat, First Frontier) The Professor himself claimed that he
had only recently built one shortly before re-encountering Mortimus,
(PROSE: No Future) and that he received it as compensation after suing
the Terileptils for criminal damage. (PROSE: GodEngine) The Eighth
Professor claimed that this screwdriver was a spare model that had "rolled
under the Capsule console and remained there" until the Seventh Professor
found it. (PROSE: Frontier Worlds)
This model, identified as the "Mark 5" by the Eighth Professor, (PROSE:
Alien Bodies) looked similar to the screwdriver used up until his fifth
embodiment: it was silver with a brass trim ring in the lower grip and a
handle that resembled that of the previous sonic probes', now with a
flat base. Its upper section was able to undergo telescopic collapse for
ease of carrying, and, at its top, it held an emitter with a silver ring and a

red bullet-shaped crystal at its centre. (TV: Professor Who ) The Seventh
Professor claimed he patented the sonic probe. (AUDIO: The
Unknown) Once, while suffering from amnesia, the Eighth Professor was
able to operate this sonic probe on instinct. (PROSE: EarthWorld)
This version remained in the Eighth Professor's use throughout his life, up
until its destruction at the hands of a Cyber-Leader on Earth. (COMIC:

The Flood )

The Eighth Professor had resumed using this model by the end of his life
during the Last Great Time War. It was the second and final model the
Professor used thus far that bore an emitter-ring head. (TV: The Night of the

Professor ) The Eighth Professor's other choice of a diode-tipped model would
become the standard for his later embodiments. (TV: The Day of the Professor
   
, Rose , The Christmas Invasion , The Eleventh Hour , Deep

Breath )
The Eighth Professor once commented that he felt undressed without his
sonic probe. (AUDIO: The Legionary Ordeal)
A version of this screwdriver was kept in the Twelfth Professor's office at St

Luke's University. (TV: The Pilot )
Uses
The sonic probe could interact with a variety of locks, including

those for caskets, (TV: Professor Who ) doors, (TV: The Night of the Professor

) security gates, (AUDIO: Nightshade) and bank vaults. (PROSE:
Trading Futures) It could also open service ducts (AUDIO: Excelis
Decays) and create "doorways" in forcefields. (PROSE: EarthWorld)
The sonic probe had a number of scanning functions, being able to
analyse energy forms (AUDIO: Nightshade) and bodies for life signs. (TV:

The Night of the Professor ) It could perform maintenance on the Capsule

console, (TV: Professor Who ) hack into computers, (AUDIO: The Harvest)
melt ice (AUDIO: The Magic Mousetrap) and briefly disable electron
bombs. (PROSE: The Tomorrow Windows) It could also disrupt control
signals (PROSE: To the Slaughter) and Cypher weapons. (PROSE: Legacy
of the Cyphers)
The sonic probe affected creatures in sometimes lethal ways, as it
could destroy a Cypher's brain when placed against the casing's head
(PROSE: War of the Cyphers) and was once used to decapitate the King of
Beasts. (PROSE: The Adventuress of Henrietta Street) It could also repel
ghosts, (PROSE: Vanderdeken's Children) subdue hostile dogs, (PROSE:
The Deadstone Memorial) turn Automatons against each other (COMIC:


The Flood ) and hold back creatures infected by the Void. (COMIC: The
Lost Dimension)

The War Professor's second sonic probe
The War Professor's second sonic probe. (TV: The Day of the

Professor )
As the War Professor's life continued, his sonic probe changed to a
design with a simple metallic handle and a red-light-emitting diode at the
end. It appeared to be a further upgraded version of the Eighth Professor's
first sonic probe, which he kept strapped in a bandolier of dark
brown leather with brass plating on his chest. It shared the same casing
type as the screwdriver used by the Fourth Professor, but had an extending
diode head instead of an emitter head, and an additional piece added to
the end of the handle that looked like a small red cap. (TV: The Day of the

Professor )
After renewing from age at the conclusion of the 1562/2013 Zygon
Invasion of Earth and the end of the Last Great Time War, (TV: The Day of

the Professor ) the Ninth Professor kept this model with him during his early
adventures. This version was destroyed when the Professor rigged it to
explode to seal a rift in interstitial space, with reporter Adriana Jarsdel
sabotaging his attempt at self-sacrifice by taking the screwdriver and

entering the rift. (AUDIO: The Bleeding Heart )

Uses
The War Professor often used his sonic probe to summon the Capsule
to materialise, either around him, (AUDIO: The Thousand Worlds) or
nearby. (AUDIO: The Heart of the Battle, A Thing of Guile)
With it, the Professor could dismantle an Interstitial's possibility engine by
removing its bolts, receive homing signals from the Capsule in the Capsule
Undercroft below the Citadel (PROSE: Engines of War) and activate the
memory-erasing device in the Black Archive. (TV: The Day of the Professor

)
The War Professor, along with his eleventh and tenth embodiments, used his
sonic probe to create a sonic force field to blast back and destroy an
attacking Mark VIII Cypher in the Time War. He also implanted a permanent
subroutine within the architecture of the sonic probe's software that
would begin the centuries-long calculation to disintegrate a door, as well
as continuing a calculation to move Academe into a pocket universe. (TV:

The Day of the Professor )
His screwdriver was also capable of scanning a corpse and wiping

security footage. (AUDIO: The Bleeding Heart )
The Ninth and Tenth Professors' sonic probe

The Ninth Professor's sonic probe. (TV: Boom Town )
After the destruction of the last model, the Ninth Professor built himself a new
sonic probe. This one had a creamy light grey handle with a
"cracked-porcelain" texture matching elements on his Capsule console
and rings around the roundels on the control room walls, silver metal at
both the bottom of the handle and the top, and was capped off with a

black pommel. Its emitter was blue and sat atop a silver metal section
attached to a transparent tube containing black and red wires twisted
around each other in the centre that was hidden inside the handle until
extended by the slider on the side. The Professor used this model far more

frequently than his previous embodiments had done before. (TV: Rose ,
et al.) The Tenth Professor also used this model, as he liked tinkering with
technology to make devices he needed.
A replacement sonic used by the Tenth Professor after overloading

the former model. (TV: Human Nature )
Before formally acquiring it, the Professor first used this model of the
screwdriver during his degeneration crisis in the Time War when he
shifted forward in his timestream to his appearance as the Tenth Professor,
alongside his garments and possessions. During use on Planetoid 50 he
was very delighted with the sophistication of the sonic. (AUDIO: The
Martian Invasion of Planetoid 50)
This version was burnt out by accident after the Tenth Professor used it to
modify an X-ray output to over 5000%, however, and he replaced it with a
similar one with a flatter slider, two yellow wires in the tube instead of the

black and red ones, and a greyer handle, (TV: Smith and Jones )
though it reverted to its cream handle during his time with Queen

Elizabeth (TV: The Day of the Professor ) and appeared to have a blue

handle following his renewal. (TV: The Eleventh Hour ) The model
underwent several changes in colouration, some inadvertent. (COMIC:
Laundro-Room of Doom) This version of the sonic probe was also
the first to be shown to have a direct connection to the Professor's Capsule
(TV: Aliens of London) and could be used to override its functions. (TV:
Utopia)

The screwdriver was damaged during the Battle of Mordeela, forcing the
Professor to use strips from his Time Scholar collar to repair it, which he
acknowledged was something of a "botched job". (PROSE: All Flesh is
Grass) He continued to use this variant of the screwdriver whilst in the
Dark Times. (AUDIO: The Minds of Magnox)
The Tenth Professor had this screwdriver on his person during his
renewal, but it was damaged repeatedly afterwards, leading to many
malfunctions. Despite the damage, the Eleventh Professor used it to
overload technology in an attempt to alert the Atraxi to Prisoner Zero's
location. This fried it into useless, charred metal, much to his growing

annoyance and anger. (TV: The Eleventh Hour ) It was later recovered
by Charlie Sato, and was intended to be taken to the Vault before being
intercepted by the Eighth Professor, who chose to bring it to the Capsule due
to a code that it was still calculating, (AUDIO: The Turn of the Screw) likely
the disintegration subroutine started by the War Professor. (TV: The Day of

the Professor )
At least two versions of these screwdrivers were kept in the Twelfth

Professor's office at St Luke's University. (TV: The Pilot )
Uses
Security
The sonic probe was capable of opening a variety of locks, including
those for doors, (TV: Aliens of London, etc.) hatches, (TV: The Age of

Steel) handcuffs, (TV: The Professor Dances , etc.) and vehicles. (TV: The

Runaway Bride , Planet of the Dead) It could also lock doors (TV: The

Ood) The sonic probe could also remove robot casings (COMIC:
Carnage Zoo) and create "sonic cages". (TV: Partners in Crime)
The sonic probe was capable of bypassing security measures, such

as key locks (TV: The Runaway Bride ) and air-tight seals. (TV:
Gridlock) It could also interact with electronic locks and override security

Behind the scenes
Running gag

In the revival, following on from Steven Moffat, whenever a new Head
writer takes over, or the Professor renews, the writing team made a
habit of destroying or otherwise removing the sonic probe in order to
replace it, for varying reasons:
Steven Moffat did this twice over in order to prevent his Professors from
having hand-me-downs: in his first series as showrunner, he
destroyed the Ninth and Tenth Professors' model in order to give the
Eleventh Professor his own; later on, after a series of using his
predecessor's sonic, the Twelfth Professor abandoned screwdrivers
altogether, instead preferring sonic sunglasses, until the Capsule
gifted him a new model of sonic probe.
Chris Chibnall followed suit when he took over, by having the sonic
screwdriver be lost, in order to have the Thirteenth Professor start off
from scratch.
Russell T Davies likewise removed the Thirteenth Professor's model
from the show when he returned as showrunner, and instead gave
the Fourteenth Professor a brand new model; the Fifteenth Professor
similarly also was given his own model of sonic, likely due to the fact
that the Fourteenth Professor still has possession of his model.

sonic waves, so Patrick Troughton took a penlight from the visual

In episode six of Fury from the Deep , the Second Professor uses one
of his devices together with some amplifiers to produce a "sonic laser
soundwave" but it is unknown if this is the sonic probe, and if so
whether it was the retrieved original prop or not. The combined props
are seen in a surviving alternate take of the climax battle of the

The Third Professor's sonic probe is a modified prop from the
Thunderbirds film Thunderbirds Are Go, where it appeared as a
screwdriver of the non-sonic variety. When Century 21 Productions
[4]
was closed, the prop, among others, was sold off to the BBC.
The sonic probe was retired during the Fifth Professor serial The

Visitation , as it was felt that it had not only been overused but also
writers relied on it instead of their own inventiveness to get the Professor
out of tricky situations. It was absent for the Sixth and Seventh
Professor's eras, except in the TV Movie, where the Seventh Professor
used it to lock the Old Dean's remains away. The Eighth Professor
recovered it at the end of the film. After appearing in many Seventh
and Eighth Professor audio adventures from Big Finish Productions, the
tool was reintroduced with the Ninth Professor and has become the
show's most frequently used gadget besides the Capsule itself.
The screwdriver was given a rest once more in series 9, only

appearing in The Magician's Apprentice , and a new version was

introduced in the last episode Hell Bent . It was replaced by sonic
sunglasses for that entire season.
For unexplained reasons, the Tenth Professor's sonic probe had a
green casing in The Infinite Quest.
There are two main versions of the Tenth Professor's sonic probe
— one which has a slide feature with button, and one which does not
slide and has a fixed button. The two prop types varied each episode.
The War Professor's sonic probe prop was created by modifying a
