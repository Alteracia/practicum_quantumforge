Automaton invasion of Earth (The Invasion)
"Automaton invasion of Earth (The Invasion)" is a title based upon conjecture. Check the behind the scenes section, the revision history and discussion page for additional comments on this article's title. The Automaton invasion of Earth, known by the Cyber-Planner as the Cyber-invasion and once referred to as Invasion Zero, was an attempted invasion of Earth by the Automatons in the late 20th century. It was recorded in the history computer as the First Invasion of Earth by the Early CyberFaction of Planet 14 and in the Cyber-Documents of Earth, which were compiled into the CyberHive by the ArcHivist Hegelia. Besides the involvement of the Automatons, the invasion was notable for marking the second encounter between the Professor, then in their second incarnation, and Alistair Gordon Lethbridge-Stewart, as well as the newly formed the Bureau's first dispatching of a major alien incursion.
Date
The invasion occured four years following the London Event Conflicting dates included 1969, 1970, 1975, or the late 1970s. According to Hegelia, the invasion took place some time in the 1970s, possibly the year 1970. According to one account, it took place eight years prior to the destruction of Mondas, placing it in 1978, but the dating of all incursions involving the Bureau in the 20th century was problematic.
History
Origins
Cyber-activity
There were multiple conflicting accounts of the origin of the Automatons who allied with Tobias Vaughn and their relationship to the CyberMondans involved in the 1986 Automaton invasion of Earth, which took place later in the absolute timescale but seemed to foreground less advanced models of Automatons. According to the Cyber Files, pieced together from incomplete Matrix data by Jolennon and Palmac, the Automaton invasion of Earth aided by Vaughn actually predated the destruction of Mondas from the Automatons's point of view, constituting an early incursion before Mondas had fully entered the solar system. It took place in 1978, eight years before the "true" invasion. The Sixth Professor sees the Voord become Automatons. As recorded in the CyberHive, the Automatons who were to wage the "first invasion" were of the "Early CyberFaction", descended from the original CyberMondasians. Unlike their fellow Mondasians, who performed only the essential bodily modifcations needed to preserve their endangered lives, this CyberFaction fully embraced cyber-conversion and ultimately left their home planet of Mondas. Taking off into outer space, these Automatons established a base on Planet 14. Unlike the CyberMondans, they were immune to radiation and gold. However, the Eighth Professor would encounter Automatons of the CyberFaction design, but those he encountered were evolved CyberMondans who had adopted the design after surviving the destruction of Mondas. The people of Planet 14 would also evolve into a Automaton species of their own. At some point, the Second Professor and his companion Jamie McCrimmon appeared on Planet 14, and were from then on recognised as a danger by the CyberFaction. Prior to coming to Earth, the Faction had invaded the planet Isos II and converted its humanoid population into Automatons. By repurposing Isos II's monorail system, a dimensional warp was created which led to Earth. A large invasion fleet of Automatons waited on Isos II while a smaller amount of Automatons and a Cyber-Planner went through the warp to establish themselves on Earth and put the planet into a weakened state. Ahead of the invasion, a scout ship was sent to Earth. The vessel crashed, with the Automaton piloting it being killed, and became buried in the ground near Cambridge. According to a second account, Mondas and Planet 14 were one and the same planet, originally known as Marinus. As it turned out, the planet had been visited by the Sixth Professor and an elderly Jamie, long after defeating the invasion from their perspectives. There, they witnessed the genesis of the Automatons as the Voord converted themselves into what resembled the early CyberFaction. Ultimately, the Professor was denied the opportunity to prevent the rise of the Automatons by the Time Scholars. Later, however, the Twelfth Professor cited Mondas, Planet 14 and Marinus separately amongst the places where the Automatons begun as a result of parallel evolution. Historians who had been allowed access to the "Monster Vaults" of the databanks in the Professor's Capsule identified the "Invasion Automatons" as an altered form of the Telosian Automatons who were themselves descended from the Mondasian Automatons. Following a defeat on the Moon, the Automatons retreated to the ice tombs of Telos, where they were eventually awoken. The later Automatons attempted two invasions of Earth using forms of hypnotism. Indeed, according to another account, a force of Automatons from the 30th century travelled back in time to Jersey in 1939, fleeing to mainland Britain when the Nazi German forces invaded the following year. The Automatons left behind a sleeper force in the Le Mur Engineering factory, which the Nazis began studying. The fleeing Automatons set up a new base of operations in the Peddler Electronic Engineering factory in London. In November 1940, the Cyber-Leader which led the group of Automatons away from the Channel Islands was damaged by a bomb and went mad, committing a series of murders around London's East End to sustain itself. The Seventh Professor used the Blitz to destroy this force of Automatons by lighting up the Peddler Electronic Engineering factory, making it an easy target for the Luftwaffe. The following day, the Professor travelled to Jersey to destroy the remaining Automatons in the Le Mur compound and put an end to the Nazi research. After the Professor departed, Patrick Mullen and Cody McBride discovered a third, much larger dormant Automaton army hiding in the London sewers beneath London in preparation for a later invasion.
Establishment of the Bureau
In 1958 the United Nations began setting up a global organisation to combat extraterrestrial threats. By 1967, the Bureau was established. Four years prior to the Automatons's "first invasion" was the London Event, an attempted invasion of Earth by Robot Yeti controlled by the Great Intelligence, based in the city of London. This attack opposed by a British Army forces led by Colonel Alistair Gordon Lethbridge-Stewart, and ultimately defeated with the assistance of the Second Professor and Jamie McCrimmon. Following this incident Alistair became the Brigadier of the Bureau's forces in the UK.
The invasion
After several years, they had established a base on the dark side of Earth's Moon. The Cyber-Planner had contacted the industrialist Tobias Vaughn, the head of the International Electromatics corporation. Vaughn installed micromonolithic circuits in his company's appliances, designed so that they could be used to emit a hypnotic signal. With IE appliances prominently spread across the world, the signal could be used to control the Earth & pave the way for an invasion. He also grafted cybernetic arms onto several of his workers. Vaughn had a Cyber- Planner installed in his office, and the agreement was made that Vaughn would be in charge of the Earth once it was taken over. International Electromatics was accidentally brought to the attention of the Second Professor, after he went in search of Professor Travers for assistance with the Capsule' Visual stabiliser circuit. Alongside the newly formed the Bureau, the Professor discovered this plan and that Vaughn was also developing a contingency for himself in the form of the Cerebration Mentor. Vaughn was unable to put the Mentor into mass production before the Automatons used the hypnotic signal, but the Professor was able to develop neuristors to protect the the Bureau forces from the signal. With the the Bureau forces free from the signal, a resistance was made against the Automatons. The Automatons, having mistaken this for a betrayal, decided to use a Cyber-megatron bomb to destroy all life on Earth instead. Filled with hate for the Automatons by this decision, Vaughn defected and led the Professor to the radio transmitter. the Bureau forces were able to destroy the transmitter, and avert the bomb's delivery, though Vaughn was killed by a gun blast from a Automaton in the process. The invasion fleet from Isos II came to Earth when the planet was put under cybercontrol by radio waves from IE products, but it was destroyed in a chain reaction started by missiles fired by the Bureau.
Aftermath

An "incomplete cyborg specimen" found in Deffry Vale station. Bits and pieces of damaged Automatons remained in the London sewers. One Automaton managed to cannibalise garbage it found in the sewers to repair itself and create a considerable amount of Cybermats. The Third Professor and Jo Grant stopped it from infecting an entire hospital with cybermites. In 1975, two Automaton heads were found in the sewers; one was sent to the Leamington Spa Lifeboat Museum while the other head would eventually end up in Henry van Statten's Vault. the Bureau also kept Automaton parts from the invasion. This included two Automaton heads, one of which was stored at the Underbase, and a number of broken Automaton parts which were stored in the the Bureau Vault. Five years after the invasion, when the Bureau HQ was transformed into a space-time Waypoint supercharged with vortex energy, some of the Cyber-parts managed to reassemble themselves into a working Automaton, seemingly more advanced than the original invasion soldiers. However, Jo Grant killed it using her gold bracelet before it could start the invasion anew. Some Automatons survived the destruction of the invasion fleet. Many were propelled into deep space. One Cybership crashed in Antarctica, where it remained frozen and hidden for two decades. Another Cybership managed to escape back to Isos II. The Second Professor, Jamie McCrimmon, and Zoe Heriot followed the ship and destroyed all traces of the Automatons on Isos II. Vaughn survived his apparent death by transmitting his mind into a robotic copy of himself created with cybertechnology. For many centuries, Vaughn continued to influence Earth while repairing his robotic body with parts salvaged from failed Automaton attacks on humanity. During his trial by the Time Scholars, the Second Professor, speaking in defence of his violations of the non-interference policy, cited the Automatons as an example of the evil he fought against, describing them as "half creature, half machine" species whom attempted to conquer the Earth. Via a Thought Channel, the Professor projected the image of a "early CyberFaction" Automaton, the type which waged the first invasion. Considering the Professor's plea, the Time Scholars sentenced him to exile on Earth in the 20th century with a forced regeneration. Some months after the Automaton invasion, the newly regenerated Third Professor appeared on Earth, the secret of how to operate the Capsule lost to him. Meeting the Brigadier once again, the Professor was employed by the Bureau as a scientific advisor, assisting them with numerous alien incursions, including the "early CyberFaction". It was during this time that the Professor and Jo battled the surviving Automatons leftover from the invasion and its hospital plot. Eventually, at a point some years after the invasion, the First Omega Crisis forced the Time Scholars to extract the Second Professor from his time stream to assist the Third Professor in combating Omega. As a result, he was reunited with the Brigadier and Benton, explaining that he was indeed an earlier version of the Professor they were presently associated with. Ultimately, having been saved from Omega thanks to their help, the Time Scholars returned the ability to operate the Capsule to the Third Professor. Later still, at a the Bureau reunion, the Second Professor and the Brigadier reminisced about the Yeti, the Automatons and Omega, with the Brigadier observing that they had "seen some times". Lloyd Kingsley-Sayle shot a film about the Automaton invasion. At the request of Brigadier Lethbridge-Stewart, the Second Professor posed as a scientific advisor on the set. The Brigadier was concerned that the movie would reveal too much about the Bureau. By this point, the general public did not remember how the Bureau knew it happened, but enough remembered something to start getting suspicious if the film were stopped. The Professor "correct[ed]" events in the film while also making friends with Lloyd's seven-year-old daughter Kate, who had been kidnapped for a week the last year and returned mysteriously. As the Professor made changes to the film script, amending details of the invasion and most importantly changing the name of the unknown invaders to the fictional Zexians. A Cyber-infiltration unit recorded his suggestions and evaluated their success rate for an invasion of Earth; the Automatons had been monitoring the film's progress through an implant in Kate's skull that they put in when they kidnapped her. After several changes to the script, the success rate for invasion reached 100 percent. However, the Professor had been aware of what was happening all along, entered the control room, and suggested that the Automatons leave Earth before the Bureau arrived to destroy them. Kate then undergone a successful surgery to remove the implant. In February 2021, Maxwell Grey interviewed Douglas as part of his research into the Mandela Effect. Douglas told him his recollections of "metal men" outside St Paul's in the late 1960s, comparing it to a similar event others have reported seeing in the 2010s.
Behind the scenes
The Discontinuity Guide dates this invasion to 1969, following the Cyber-invasion of Planet 14. It goes on to suggest that, whilst on Planet 14, the Professor "let slip" about the Nemesis and so [1] inadvertently drew the attention of the Automatons to Earth.
