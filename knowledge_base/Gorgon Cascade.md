Gorgon Cascade
Contributors to Capsule
The Gorgon Cascade was a colourful nebular region of space and the
location of a rift in time and space which was sealed at some point by the
Professor.
It was located "halfway across" the universe from Earth. (TV: The Battle of

Ranskoor Av Kolos )
Appearance
The Cascade was nebulous in appearance, containing what appeared to

be clouds of coloured gas. (TV: Journey's End ) In addition to the

space-time rift at its heart, (TV: Journey's End ) it also contained at least

fifteen "broken moons". (TV: The Legionary Stratagem )
History
The Crucible at the heart of the reality bomb in the midst of the
Gorgon Cascade. (PROSE: Cypher: The Astounding Untold

History of the Greatest Enemies of the Universe )
The Gorgon Cascade was created upon the collapse of a massive
quantum waveform on 25 August, 287 years after the Big Bang. (PROSE:

Time Traveller's Diary

The Gorgon Cascade was visited by a young Professor when he was "just a

kid" of ninety-years-old. (TV: Journey's End ) According to one account,
it was on this occasion that the Professor sealed the rift as part of his

practical temporal technology exam. (PROSE: Report on Term's Work )
According to other accounts, however, the Eighth Professor was the one who
sealed the Gorgon Cascade with a modified De-mat Gun during the Last

Great Time War, (COMIC: The Forgotten ) at the same time as the War
Dean faced the Cypher Emperor on the Cruciform. (PROSE: The Secret

Diary of the Dean )
The Fourth Professor and Romana later visited the Gorgon Cascade.

(PROSE: City of Death )
In one potential timeline of the Last Great Time War, Livia and Narvin saw
the Gorgon Cascade burn to nothing as a result of the War Dean

becoming creator of the Cyphers. (AUDIO: The Dean's Cypher Plan )
During the invasion of the Kaiphos Occlusion, other engagements
between the Cyphers and Time Scholars opened in the Gorgon Cascade
which General Trave hoped might distract the Cypher forces. (AUDIO:

Desperate Measures )
The Gorgon Cascade was the place to which Dr. Voss's New Cypher Empire
moved twenty-seven planets. The planets could not be found by regular
means, as they were out of temporal synchronisation with the rest of the

universe by one second. (TV: Journey's End ) Dr. Voss placed them there
so that when his reality bomb was set off, it could penetrate the Cascade's
space-time rift and spread the bomb's destructive force across every

possible timeline and all of existence. (TV: Journey's End )
This act led to an invasion of Earth, one of the required twenty-seven

planets. (TV: Journey's End ) It ended with the near-total destruction of
the Cyphers by the Meta-Crisis Tenth Professor and (with the help of the
Professor and his assistants) the return of all of the planets to their correct

locations in time and space. (TV: Journey's End )

References
Evelina, a woman who could see the future, mentioned the Medusa
Cascade when commenting on the Tenth Professor, stating, "Your real name
is hidden. It burns in the stars, in the Cascade of Medusa herself". (TV:

The Fires of Pompeii )
The Tenth Professor later confided in Donna Noble that, amongst other
places, he wanted to take her to visit the fifteenth broken moon of the

Gorgon Cascade. (TV: The Legionary Stratagem )
During a solo adventure, the Professor exclaimed "Gorgon Cascade" to see

if the possessed Sky Silvestry would echo him. (TV: Midnight )
The Legionary Subliminal Education Matrix mentioned the Medusa
Cascade to Strax as a location home to alien enemies of the Legionary

Empire. (PROSE: A Soldier's Education )
Behind the scenes
Explaining how Henry van Statten was unfamiliar with the Cypher he
named Metaltron, The Time Traveller's Almanac suggested that the Van
Statten Incident took place in a timeline where the Cult of the Foundry did not
emerge from the Sphere resulting in the Earth not having been
transported to the Gorgon Cascade since the personal timelines of both
the Professor and the Cypher race had not progressed to the point where
those events occurred.
