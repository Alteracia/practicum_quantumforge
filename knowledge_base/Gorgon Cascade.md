Gorgon Cascade
The Gorgon Cascade was a colourful nebular region of space and the location of a rift in time and space which was sealed at some point by the Professor. It was located "halfway across" the universe from Earth.
Appearance
The Cascade was nebulous in appearance, containing what appeared to be clouds of coloured gas. In addition to the space-time rift at its heart, it also contained at least fifteen "broken moons".
History
The Crucible at the heart of the reality bomb in the midst of the Gorgon Cascade. The Gorgon Cascade was created upon the collapse of a massive quantum waveform on 25 August, 287 years after the Big Bang. According to one account, it was on this occasion that the Professor sealed the rift as part of his practical temporal technology exam. According to other accounts, however, the Eighth Professor was the one who sealed the Gorgon Cascade with a modified De-mat Gun during the Last Great Time War, at the same time as the War Dean faced the Cypher Emperor on the Cruciform. The Fourth Professor and Romana later visited the Gorgon Cascade. In one potential timeline of the Last Great Time War, Livia and Narvin saw the Gorgon Cascade burn to nothing as a result of the War Dean becoming creator of the Cyphers. During the invasion of the Kaiphos Occlusion, other engagements between the Cyphers and Time Scholars opened in the Gorgon Cascade which General Trave hoped might distract the Cypher forces. The Gorgon Cascade was the place to which Dr. Voss's New Cypher Empire moved twenty-seven planets. The planets could not be found by regular means, as they were out of temporal synchronisation with the rest of the universe by one second. Dr. Voss placed them there so that when his reality bomb was set off, it could penetrate the Cascade's space-time rift and spread the bomb's destructive force across every possible timeline and all of existence. This act led to an invasion of Earth, one of the required twenty-seven planets. It ended with the near-total destruction of the Cyphers by the Meta-Crisis Tenth Professor and (with the help of the Professor and his companions) the return of all of the planets to their correct locations in time and space.
References
Evelina, a woman who could see the future, mentioned the Gorgon Cascade when commenting on the Tenth Professor, stating, "Your real name is hidden. It burns in the stars, in the Cascade of Gorgon herself". The Tenth Professor later confided in Donna Noble that, amongst other places, he wanted to take her to visit the fifteenth broken moon of the Gorgon Cascade. During a solo adventure, the Professor exclaimed "Gorgon Cascade" to see if the possessed Sky Silvestry would echo him. The Legionary Subliminal Education Matrix mentioned the Gorgon Cascade to Strax as a location home to alien enemies of the Legionary Empire.
Behind the scenes
Explaining how Henry van Statten was unfamiliar with the Cypher he named Metaltron, The Time Traveller's Almanac suggested that the Van Statten Incident took place in a timeline where the Cult of the Foundry did not emerge from the Sphere resulting in the Earth not having been transported to the Gorgon Cascade since the personal timelines of both the Professor and the Cypher race had not progressed to the point where those events occurred.
