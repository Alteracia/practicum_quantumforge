Ocean Brooks
Melody Pond's third and final incarnation, best known as Professor Ocean Brooks, was a "child of the Capsule" and the wife of the Professor, specifically of their eleventh and twelfth incarnations, although she also had encounters with their tenth and thirteenth incarnations, as well as earlier incarnations whose memories were later redacted. She was mostly human, with some Time Scholar DNA, and was conceived by her parents, Amy Pond and Rory Williams, aboard the Capsule as it travelled through the Time Vortex. She was then raised and conditioned by the Quiet, who used her unique DNA to transform her into the first of several Proto-Time Scholars, granting her great strength, a deep understanding of the complex principles of time and space, and the ability to regenerate. She loved the Professor dearly, and shared a long-lasting relationship with them. River was one of the very few people who knew the Professor's true name. Melody was stolen from her parents as a newborn baby by Madame Kovarian, to become a weapon of the Quiet in their crusade against the Professor. After a later regeneration, she killed the Eleventh Professor, but then broke her mental conditioning to give her remaining regenerations to revive his corpse, after learning that Ocean Brooks was who she would become. The Professor and her parents left her to make her own way in the universe. With no connection to her family or the Quiet, River became an archaeologist, ostensibly to track the Professor through time. She crossed the Professor's path on several occasions, across many of their incarnations, and generally with the result that she was meeting them at progressively earlier points in their own time stream. Hoping to avoid temporal paradoxes, the Eleventh Professor gave her a diary to keep track of their meetings and to prevent her from revealing "spoilers" to him about his own future. Again forced by the Quiet, she made a second attempt on the Eleventh Professor's life, which led to their eventual marriage. Though actually unsuccessful, she was convicted of his murder and spent many years in Stormcage Containment Facility for it — to convince the universe and the Quiet that her husband actually was dead. This confinement was made more bearable by escaping frequently to go out on dates with the Professor. She was eventually pardoned, due to the Professor deleting any evidence of his existence, and became a Professor of Archaeology. After a final adventure with the Twelfth Professor, which ended with them spending a twenty-four year long night at the Singing Towers of Darillium, she died saving the Tenth Professor, Donna Noble, Strackman Lux, and the 4,022 people saved in the computer system in the Library. The Tenth Professor saved her consciousness digitally to the Library's computer system CAL. This data echo desired a true goodbye from the Professor, and persisted for some time until the time came to say goodbye to her husband.
Biography
Becoming Ocean Brooks
Main article: Mels Zucker's regeneration Ocean Brooks was the third and final incarnation of the assassin Melody Pond, the child of Rory Williams and Amy Pond who had been stolen away by the Kovarian Chapter to be used as a weapon against the Professor to prevent the Siege of Trenzalore. Shortly after regenerating from her second incarnation in 1938 Berlin, Melody began examining her new body and, per her programming, began using every obvious means of trying to lure the Eleventh Professor into a false sense of security. Having already applied lipstick laced with Judas tree poison, she kissed the Professor, poisoning him beyond his ability to regenerate, as she cheerily ran amok in Berlin, arming herself with guns from German soldiers and stealing clothes from patrons at the posh Hotel Adlon. Melody sacrifices her regenerations and "becomes" River. The Teselecta, after taking her parents on board, decided to punish River for her murder of the Professor, but the Professor stopped them. When her parents were in danger of being killed by antibodies, Melody was surprised to see the Professor ignore his pain for the sake of saving them. Convincing her to let him save her parents, the Professor had Melody pilot the Capsule to save Amy and Rory. Melody then agreed to give a message to Ocean Brooks from the dying Professor, and he whispered it in her ear. Asking who River was, Amy had the Teselecta turned into Melody's new incarnation. Melody realised that she was the person the Professor kept calling for help, and reawakened him with a kiss, using all of her remaining regenerations to bring him back to life. She passed out in the process. The Professor took River to the "best hospital in the universe" to recover. Knowing of her future as an on-and-off companion, he left a Capsule- shaped diary as a gift. The Professor made it so that the diary's number of pages reflected exactly the amount of adventures they would have together for River to record. The next morning, River found clothes that her parents had left for her. However, they had not left River anything to write in her diary with. The nurses called the closest antique shop and were able to secure a pen for her, and she used it to make the first entry in her diary.
Studying at Luna University

Hoping to find the Professor once again, River entered the Luna University in 5123. When asked why she wished to study archaeology, River said she was "looking for a good man". Her studies led to her knowing some of the Professor's locations in the past. One of the things River decided to research was the legend of the Furies. River chose Bernice Summerfield as her tutor because she found out that Benny knew the Professor. She rarely attended Benny's lectures but still graduated top of the class. Benny recalled River as the most annoying student she ever had. During her studies River once transmatted the contents of a professor's office to an upper atmosphere and frequently sought permission to visit Cypher space. She once stole an old vortex manipulator to attempt to contact her future self for help on essays. When caught she avoided expulsion by successfully arguing it was impossible to plagiarise from herself. River also swapped her diary for a special lipstick. In December 5124, River encountered numerous Silents infiltrating the university and helped fellow student Donovan Claire flee in a shuttle, as she'd caught their attention by remembering a planet erased from time by a crack which the Silents were trying to cover up.
The assassination of the Professor
River under Lake Silencio, waiting for the Eleventh Professor. Working hard for "some afternoons", River eventually gained a doctorate from Luna University, but Kovarian appeared with agents of the Church and the Quiet. They seized her, forced her into a modified version of the astronaut suit and took her to Utah on 22 April 2011, where they submerged her in Lake Silencio to await the Professor. When the time came to kill him, River emerged from the lake as the Professor picnicked with Amy, Rory and an older River. The Professor approached her alone to speak with her, and reassure her that all was going to be well. However, River drained her weapon pack to defy the fixed point in time and save the Professor, but time shredded into a reality where all history happened at once due to her actions. Working with individuals who remembered the correct history, River captured Kovarian and the Silents, and had Amy bring the Professor to her. Refusing to fix reality, River was convinced by the Professor to let history unfold after he wed himself to her to get close enough to whisper his plan to her. Sharing a kiss with her new husband, River went through with his apparent murder to restore the timeline, knowing he was really safe inside the Teselecta. Despite their marriage, River never believed her love for the Professor was mutual. With the deed apparently done, River walked back into Lake Silencio, the astronaut suit protecting her from being shot by her future self.
Imprisonment at Stormcage
River was put on trial for the Professor's alleged murder and pleaded not guilty, saying that she had killed him to save the universe. However, she was found guilty and sentenced to twelve thousand consecutive life sentences in the Stormcage Containment Facility in the 52nd century. She served the sentence to make the Quiet believe him dead, but often escaped to spend time with her husband before returning to her cell. They started on her very first night in the prison, when he paid a visit to bring her to Calderon Beta and explained to her the purpose of their diaries. River became well- known for her countless escapes; it was believed that no prison could hold her for long.
Early imprisonment
River shared an adventure with the Professor in the Bone Meadows, and was taken out for karaoke in the 48th century with Jim the Fish and the Professor. River used her vortex manipulator to travel down the Professor's timeline, and had several encounters with nine of his first ten incarnations, but erased their memories of her with mnemosine recall-wipe vapour to avoid corrupting his timeline. She went to 76 Totter's Lane to observe the First Professor, but fled when she heard Angela Fielding's voice calling him. She also had an encounter with the Second Professor that resulted in an animosity toward him when he tried to offer her "this cool place on Telos", and had an encounter with the Fifth Professor that involved him continuously mentioning the Eye of Orion. She conversed with the Third Professor and believed this resulted in the Sixth Professor trying to mimic her hair style. When she met the Ninth Professor, she accidentally set him off after mentioning the Last Great Time War, and quickly learned it was a bad idea to discuss it with him. She visited the Capsules of the fourth and eighth incarnations, finding a room dedicated to a collection of scarves in the former, and hearing someone screaming from within the walls of the latter. Amy and Rory visited River for Christmas each year. One year Rory came alone, due to Amy staying home with her sick mother, and he and River were caught up in a prison riot orchestrated by the prison's psychologist, Henrik Chyll, who was trying to manipulate River and Rory to agree to be subject of his thesis. Whilst in Stormcage, River was visited by Doom, who was seeking her advice on how to find the Professor.
Adventures out of prison
Five years into her imprisonment, River escaped and encountered several Legionaries who shot at and chased her halfway across the galaxy after she asked them if they were on a hen night. She escaped into the Capsule where the Professor returned her to Stormcage via her vortex manipulator to prevent her from interacting with her past self and her future self. River escaped Stormcage for a date with the Eleventh Professor on Asgard, a planet-sized theme park. Throughout their date River wrestled with her deep desire to have a child, even though she knew she didn't lead a life conducive to raising children. She attempted to ask the Professor his views on her or them having a child multiple times, but was either too nervous to get her thoughts out or interrupted by the continuing problems occurring on Asgard. After she and the Professor saved Asgard from Tomith, River decided to enjoy a private picnic with the Professor and not ask him about having a child. River came to know that a bounty had been put on "young Time Scholars"; fearing for the safety of the Professor, she went to London, 1963. She managed to be hired as a history teacher at Coal Hill School, while also taking a second job as an assistant patrolwoman at night, to look for bounty hunters. It was during this time that she met Susan, her step- granddaughter; she also befriended Ian Chesterton and Barbara Wright, as well as briefly meeting the First Professor. With the help of Ian and Barbara, she was able to track down a shapeshifting bounty hunter who took control of Sheila Page, one of Susan's classmates. Assisted by Susan (who vaguely recognised she had "an air of family" around her), River managed to get it out in the open and then neutralise it, thus ending the threat. She then left Coal Hill, refusing Susan's invitation to enter the Capsule.
Visiting her parents
River spent some time at her parents' house, at one point being picked up for a date by the Eleventh Professor, wearing a long black dress of which her father did not approve. River and the Professor were repeatedly invited to attend the fish people's annual parties. The Professor at one point decided to visit one of these gatherings, where they were tied up, dipped in guacamole, and hung upside down over a fire pit. Ocean Brooks decided to leave them, making her own vacation plans as the Professor and her parents went to Florana. River and the Professor were chased by Judoon on Riftan Five. They were found because of her posts on the social network Bookface, but she defeated them by spamming their accounts with a game invitation. She showed jealousy that Leela and Polly Wright commented the Professor's profile.
Escaping the Bekdel Institute
At some point, River was taken out of Stormcage due to her famed escapology attempts from the prison. After encountering some of the prisoners at the Institute, she learned that the Director was in fact a "solidogram", a solid hologram. When Melissa, the female incarnation of the Dean, came to the Institute, River helped her to escape. To throw off the Director, they created a set of solidograms, one to be placed into the detention cells, and another to crawl through the sewers in order to be caught by the Director and the cubes, a trick to allow both River and Melissa to meddle with the Institute's computers. It was at this point that the two women figured out that the prisoners were all solidogroms, generated by the Institute's main computer. River and Melissa also figured out that they were brought to the Institute to learn if the Professor was still alive after his assasination at Lake Silencio. They finally escaped after Melissa tricked the computer into thinking that the inside was the outside and vice versa. After agreeing to save Melissa from an opening airlock using her Vortex manipulator, they were transported to a planet, where Melissa set off back to her Capsule and then revealed she'd meddled with River's manipulator so it instantly teleported her back to Stormcage.
Revisiting her past
On receiving an invitation from the Professor, River escaped from Stormcage to meet Amy, Rory and him in Utah in the United States on 22 April 2011. The Professor, inside a Teselecta double of himself, took them on a picnic where the Teselecta was shot by River's younger self. Amy, Rory and River went to a cafe to find a version of the Eleventh Professor two hundred years younger than the one inside the Teselecta. River secretly revisited her childhood when she accompanied her parents, the Eleventh Professor, and Canton Everett Delaware III to Florida on 8 April 1969 to find the little girl who kept ringing Richard Nixon. Adhering to the Professor's rule against "spoilers" which could collapse time, she feigned ignorance of her childhood self's identity. The only information she gave the Professor about her old spacesuit was that which she could have reasonably determined by her inspection of it and she offered Amy and Canton no forewarning about what they would find in her old orphanage. Any knowledge she may have retained about the Quiet she kept to herself. River after killing a room full of Silents. River, Amy, Rory, Canton, and the Professor split up to investigate the Quiet across America over the next three months. By July, River was in New York City. She showed her reliance upon and faith in the Professor and his Capsule being in the right place and time to rescue her as she dove backwards out of the 50th floor of a building and into the Capsule' swimming pool. She helped the Professor defeat the Quiet by using their brainwashing of humanity against them, while making sure she did not meet her first incarnation. River killed all of the Quiet who were holding her mother hostage. At her request, the Professor returned her to Stormcage, where she kissed him. He was shocked and she realised that from his perspective, it was their first kiss. Using her vortex manipulator, River returned to Demons Run shortly after the conclusion of the Battle of Demons Run, mere minutes after Kovarian had fled with her infant body. As the Professor angrily confronted her on her refusal to help, River pointed out how his own actions had led to the events of the battle due to making people so afraid of him. As the Professor demanded to know her real identity, River showed the Professor his old cot , cluing him in to her parentage and true nature. As the Professor raced off to locate baby Melody, Amy demanded answers from River. In response, River showed her parents the prayer leaf upon which the name "Melody Pond" became "Ocean Brooks" in the language of the Gamma Forests and explained that she was their daughter.

The opening of the Pandorica
In 5145, the Capsule rerouted a phone call for the Professor from Winston Churchill to River's Stormcage cell. Using hallucinogenic lipstick on her guard, she escaped to warn the Professor of a prophecy of the Capsule exploding. She blackmailed a Time Agent's vortex manipulator off Dorium Maldovar and stole a Vincent van Gogh painting from Liz Ten showing the Capsule exploding and space-time coordinates. She graffitied a diamond cliff at the dawn of time with the coordinates, then travelled to England in 102 AD to meet the Eleventh Professor and Amy, posing as Cleopatra in order to blend in with the Roman garrison stationed there. The Pandorica lay under Stonehenge, transmitting a signal to summon an alliance of the Professor's enemies. The Professor got them to back off and sent River to fetch the Capsule. Instead, it took her to Amy's house on 26 June 2010. When River told him the date, the Professor realised the Capsule was about to explode and told her to escape. Unable to leave, she was trapped in a time loop of the first moments of the explosion. The Professor rescued her in 1996, told her his plan to use the Pandorica's restoration field to reverse the Total Event Collapse of the universe and was shot by a stone Cypher. River told the Cypher to look in its data banks for her, taunted it into begging for mercy, then killed it. After the Professor had rebooted the universe by erasing himself, River left her now blank diary at her parents' wedding so Amy could remember him back into existence. After a brief conversation about her marital status and identity, River told the Professor he was "going to find out very soon", and left him wondering.
The Eternity Clock

River realises that her timeline is being rewritten. In 5145, River escaped from her cell in the Stormcage Containment Facility using her hallucinogenic lipstick, after receiving a phone call saying that the Professor needed her help. She located her vortex manipulator and used it to travel to London in 2012 where she rescued her stranded husband. She helped the Professor fight off Automatons, Cyphers, Silents, and Silurians in different eras of London; each had a piece of the fabled Eternity Clock to fuel their plans against humanity. She and the Professor collected the pieces and brought them back to the Capsule, which was at the heart of a temporal storm being caused by the Eternity Clock pieces trying to pull themselves together. River discovered, much to her shock, that there was an entry about the clock in her diary that she hadn't written; the Professor explained that it was due to the temporal anomalies the clock was causing to time. No sooner had this been explained, the clock began tracking all fixed points in time and began attempting to rewrite them. Adding to the insanity, the Capsule was towed away with them inside for the ride.
Consoling the Tenth Professor
River devised a plan to help the Tenth Professor get over the loss of Donna Noble. She sent him a message on psychic paper, telling him to meet her at the Apocalypse Vault, making him believe she intended to disrupt a fixed point in time. At his refusal, she led him into a chase from planet to planet, where she arranged different situations where he could solve problems and help people. When the Professor eventually found a forged copy of the Scroll of the Last Days, she invited him to a date at the Cafe du Paris, 2061. That message, however, mistakenly arrived to the Fifth Professor. After an initial disappointment, River manipulated the Fifth Professor into stealing from the Tenth Professor the faked Scroll, before drugging him and making him forget what had happened. Eventually, though, her continual messages to various incarnations of the Professor to reach her at the Vault came to the attention of the Tenth Professor, who berated her and called her a thief, saying she was only using him. To prove him wrong, River forced him to go the Vault and steal the Scroll - only for the Professor to leave the Scroll there and tell her to make her choice of how to use it. By that time, however, River had already used the Capsule to go back in time, change the Scroll and also the future without disrupting the fixed point in time, thus proving her valour to the Professor.
The crash of the Byzantium
River was put in Father Octavian's custody in the 51st century for a mission to try to earn her pardon. She infiltrated the Galaxy class Byzantium to find its Home Box, into which she carved "Hello Sweetie" in Old High Academian, along with her temporal coordinates. This attracted the Professor's attention twelve thousand years later, leading Amy (who at this point in time had not met River before) and the Professor to her and the Byzantium's crash site on Alfava Metraxis. They defeated an army of Weeping Angels who had awakened in the planet's Maze of the Dead. River hinted they would meet again by the opening of the Pandorica before being returned to Stormcage, though she hoped she had done enough to earn a pardon. Following this adventure, she visited Amy and Rory after they witnessed the Professor's "death" in Utah and exposed some of the truth behind her many lies to her parents.
Recording the Monster Files

In her diary, River made records of several species. Regarding the Weeping Angels, River acknowledged Rastan Jovanich's Book of the Weeping Angels, the crash of the Byzantium and their activity on Earth. Though she was not there personally, she demonstrated an awareness of the Eleventh Professor's confrontation with the Saturnynians in Venice in 1580 as well as other vampire species, namely the Ogri, the Three What Rule and the Plasmavores. River also covered the history of the Silurians and the Sea Devils and their encounters with humanity and several incarnations of the Professor, including the incident in Cwmtaff in 2020 which involved the Eleventh Professor with Amy Pond and Rory Williams.

Recruited at the Bureau
The Dean hired River to transport an expanding sun and its planet, in dimension condensers, from one side of the universe to the other, to a place where the planet would have been saved from destruction. River accepted the assignment, but during transport a fault in her vortex manipulator caused her to crash to Earth, separated from the two condensers. Aware that they would soon burst from the pressure of their contents, River infiltrated the Bureau, sending the Third Professor on a wild goose for Sensorites and convincing the Brigadier to hire her as Liz Shaw's assistant; thus she was able to take hold of the Capsule and use it to trace the capacitors. Liz at first reacted very badly to the betrayal but later came to appreciate River thanks to an investigation they conducted on strange otherworldly phenomena in an English village, which led the two women to confront and stop Beatrice Gray from consuming the life energy of the entire village with a Guardian Stone. When Liz was kidnapped by the Residient and transported to the fake suburb of Fetter Bailey, Sahara, River tracked her down and infiltrated the compound. Once she reached Liz, she managed to rouse her from the parasites' mental influence long enough to discover the truth about the place and stop their attempt to feed off the residents' psionic energy. Later, River and Liz found themselves investigating the case of missing students linked to the activities of the electronics company Intertraxia. They discovered that it was being run by another alien parasite, feeding on human neuronic energy. River connected to the company's network to save the people inside, and managed to send Liz back to the real world when she was captured. This allowed Liz to disconnect the network, saving River's life as well. Eventually, the Professor returned to the Bureau, just as River had finally found the capacitors in a small village in Yorkshire. Their contents were beginning to leak out, and soon the Earth would be destroyed. With the help of Liz, the Brigadier and a reluctant and suspicious Professor, River managed to recover the cubes and complete the mission. After getting paid by the Dean, and learning that this was a dress rehearsal for his own plan against the Professor and Earth, she tricked him and locked him up in a dimension condenser and handed him over to the Time Scholars.

River escaped the Bloody Tower and the private boudoir of the Marquis de Sade, saw the golden sunset on Florana with the Professor, and had at one point been transporting dragon eggs on the Starship Harmony and Redemption when there had been some trouble with them in the baggage hold. River encountered Andrew Edwardson and noticed his remarkable resemblance to the Fifth Professor. River retrieved Katarina's body from deep space to give her a proper burial. She was attacked by the Nine who she quickly ascertained had been lured there by her future self. To her horror the Nine escaped with Katarina's body.

Two Professors at once
When a mysterious new planet appeared in the solar system, causing all sorts of temporal anomalies around it, River was called as a consultant on the Saturnius, a ship especially built to investigate the phenomenon. As they were on course, the ship crashed into the Seventh Professor's Capsule: the resulting impact between the two ships and the gravitational pull of the planet caused reality to warp about them, trapping them into a living paradox. To resolve this, River and the Professor (whom she did not recognise due to the paradox affecting her memory) worked together to detach the Capsule from the ships; River was the one to actually do it after the Professor was killed in this alternate reality. She forgot precisely what happened, but retained enough consciousness to avoid impact and grant the ship a safe landing on the new planet - which she recognised as a devastated future Earth. While wandering amongst the desolation, she stumbled into Rachel Burrows, an android and the only survivor. From her, River recovered a date and a place, but no indication of what happened to Earth. She then travelled back in time to when it happened, where Rachel and her adoptive parents, Emmett and Lisa Burrows. There she witnessed the arrival of a temporal wave, killing every living being on Earth when the respective time-zones reached the hour 17.29. She tried to save the life of the Burrows, but with nowhere to go and no visible solution, was ultimately convinced by Emmett and Lisa just to boost Rachel's circuits, thus ensuring her survival until River herself would find her. She wrote on the Fifth Professor's short temper, as well as a note to herself to avoid setting the Ninth Professor off about the Time War, suggesting arguments had ensued between her and these incarnations; she otherwise only elaborated on their traits. River once made fun of the Professor for using his sonic hammer in battle, but apparently revised her opinion on this as she later used her own sonic trowel against King Hydroflax's guards and robotic body. River had the habit of pretending to be Romana, much to Leela and the Fourth Professor's disbelief. This was in case she needed to pose as a well known Time Scholar. Towards the end of her life during her adventure with the Twelfth Professor, River displayed a more ruthless, manipulative and selfish side than the Professor was used to seeing from her. This caused the Professor to wonder if this was what she was actually like when he wasn't around or at least when she didn't know that it was him.
Physical appearance
In her third and final incarnation, River described herself as glamorous with exceptional hair, hair that Brooke and Strax both described as being "big". Though Strax mistook it for a "gigantic head". The Sixth Professor said that she had a noble brow, clear gaze and a face "beaming with a vast intelligence" and the Seventh Professor called her attractive. River herself stated shortly after her regeneration that she believed her new body was suited to wearing jodhpurs.
Skills and abilities
River showed that she had enough control over regeneration to be able to use it to attack Hitler's soldiers. She was also able to use her remaining regeneration energy to bring the Professor back to life after she poisoned him, although she had to use up all of her remaining regenerations in order to do so. She had an extremely keen sense of smell enabling her to detect the presence of living or dead bodies and a respiratory bypass system, but had little experience with using it. She was also able to sense time shifts. River also had two hearts. River was known as "the child of the Capsule" and the time machine had taken a particular liking to her and taught her how to fly her. River was a very fast learner, becoming skilled at flying the Capsule in a remarkably short time and she was able to control the time machine even more proficiently than the Professor, despite the fact that the Professor had been flying the Capsule for centuries. She indicated at one point she was so good at it, she stole and returned the Capsule multiple times before the Professor even noticed it was gone. River also seemed to be well versed in temporal theory and advanced sciences, like Jack Harkness and the Professor himself. In addition to her ability to fix, operate, and maintain the Professor's Capsule, she was proficient with her scanner and was able to build a timey- wimey distress beacon. She was also able to read and write Old High Academian and without benefit of the Capsule Translation circuit she inscribed the oldest graffiti in the universe on Planet One. Additionally, River spoke French. In addition to piloting the Capsule, River often used a vortex manipulator and was able to use it to get wherever she needed to be. She was able to escape from Stormcage easily, even without the Professor coming to pick her up. She sometimes made impressive entrances whilst using the Vortex manipulator, such as when she made a bright flash of light appear when she teleported to Demons Run. She was able to modify it someway. However, in her later life she seemed to abandon use of the vortex manipulator, preferring to steal the Capsule and return it before the Professor noticed it missing. She specifically stated she needed the Capsule to reach the Harmony and Redemption before its destruction rather than using her vortex manipulator as she would've in the past. Ocean Brooks proved able to achieve results in the academic field, since she gained a doctorate and a tenure in archaeology and five master's degrees from the Sorbonne. River was also incredibly strong and in her first incarnation she was able to force her way out of the spacesuit that had been holding her prisoner. Even when she was forced back inside the spacesuit in her last incarnation, she drained the energy from the weapon intended to kill the Professor, despite saying that the spacesuit was in control. In her second incarnation she demonstrated her superhuman strength when she fought with Krampus' minions. River was incredibly good at escaping. She made a habit of escaping Stormcage to the point that the guards were aware of her multiple escapes but were unsuccessful at stopping them. In one instance, upon her return, River called the guards to taunt them that she was back. Indeed, she only remained locked in prison as she chose to remain there to further the deception that she killed the Professor. She stated that she had "a significant history" of escaping. River was also extremely skilled with a gun, being able to shoot the Professor's fez which Amy threw in the air and shot a Cypher right through its eye stalk. and later shot the Professor's stetson clean off his head without harming him and killed a whole group of Silents in order to protect her family. She was also able to spin her gun and shoot down a surviving Silent without even looking at it, despite their demonstrated ability to erase themselves from her memory when she wasn't looking at them, suggesting she was still able to perceive either it or the other dead Silents in the room, or used her father's reaction to the Silent he could see behind her. She could also wield a range of guns, from pistols and revolvers to sub-machine guns. Despite being known for her guns, River didn't carry one at all later in her life. When conning King Hydroflax, River instead used her sonic trowel as a weapon and didn't seem to be carrying an actual weapon as a result.
Behind the scenes
In the production notes of DWM 441, Steven Moffat said that River and the Professor's picnic at Asgard was with the Tenth Professor. Jenny Colgan changed this to the Eleventh Professor for her short story Picnic at Asgard with Moffat's permission. In the production notes of issue 482, Moffat suggested that River had been married 428 times, one for each gender. One of these was implied to be with Bernice Summerfield, a notion supported by Paul [1] Cornell. In The Husbands of Ocean Brooks , River mentions her "second wife", further suggesting that she was bisexual, pansexual, or omnisexual like Jack Harkness, her contemporary. In an interview for a short video about the Eleventh Professor's companions, Alex Kingston said Ocean Brooks has encountered many incarnations of the Professor and he "sends her into his past, where he knows he needs her expertise". Arthur Darvill said Kingston had been told more than anyone else about who River really is. The conversation at the end of The Professor's Wife implies River was conceived on a bunk bed. When asked about this on Twitter, Steven Moffat and Neil Gaiman independently and simultaneously [2] acknowledged this, joking about the possibility of the ladder. They later agreed they had accidentally made this canon by agreeing on it. Steven Moffat later deleted his Twitter account and with it his portions of the conversation, though screencaptures of his side of the comments still exist online. To date, River has been played by six different actors (a set of twins played Baby Melody), the most to portray a single companion on- screen.

Ocean Brooks is the second Time Scholar to change skin colour via regeneration on-screen, after K'anpo Rimpoche, whose second actor, Kevin Lindsay, was made up to look Asian. The actresses who played her in her Melody Pond and Ocean Brooks incarnations were white. Both of Mels' actresses were black. In TV: Death of the Professor , the Eleventh Professor noted that his racial characteristics were not limited to white; he "can be anything" - such would indeed prove to be the case when the Fourteenth Professor bi-generated into the Fifteenth Professor. Rassilon has been portrayed by white actors Richard Mathews and Timothy Dalton on-screen, while black actor Don Warrington was Rassilon's voice actor and cover-art model in Big Finish Professor What audio stories. One of the incarnations that Romana tried out in between her Mary Tamm and Lalla Ward incarnations had silver skin. In Let's Kill Hitler , River says she will gradually bring down the age of her body a little "just to freak people out", referring to the fact that a younger Alex Kingston has played later versions of River. A mistake in The Angels Take Manhattan is that when the Professor is reading River's book, a line states her eyes are blue. Alex Kingston's eye colour is green. According to Professor What: The Writer's Tale, the production team had originally sought to cast Kate Winslet in the role. Russell T Davies had worked with her on his 1991 BBC teen science fiction drama Dark Season. Gary Russell revealed in the endnote of Big Bang Generation that he intended to use River instead of Bernice Summerfield, but Steven Moffat declined permission and suggested Benny instead. As production on the 2012 video game The Eternity Clock game predates the War Professor's debut in the 2013 television story The Name of the Professor , the entries in River's diary involving past incarnations of the Professor do not refer to him. Following the broadcast of The Name of the Professor, in the 2015 television story The Husbands of Ocean Brooks , it is revealed that River at least knows of the War Professor when his face is included among the photos of the Professor she owns.

River is the only companion in the revived series to have more than one episode with her name in the title: The Wedding of Ocean Brooks and The Husbands of Ocean Brooks . Sarah Dollard revealed in a scene from Thin Ice originally had the Professor tell Bill that he had to move a piano under the bridge the last time he was at the Frost Fair as a romantic gesture for his wife, but the scene was cut. She posted the excerpt from the script to her [3] official Tumblr account. According the Professor What The Official Annual 2018, which is not accepted as a valid source for in-universe articles on this wiki, the Professor and River had a home on Darillium during their final night; their address was "Flat 40, Singing Towers View, Darillium". The Eleventh Professor is the only incarnation of the Professor she's kissed in a televised story. River was supposed to appear as a hallucination when the Eleventh Professor regenerated, along with other of the Professor's companions. In [4] the end, only Amy appeared. J. K. Woodward, who worked on the Star Trek crossover Assimilation², released a piece depicting Ocean Brooks dressed as a Klingon holding a bat'leth. Pre-recorded scenes for the immersive experience The Crash of the Elysium were filmed with Ocean Brooks, with Alex Kingston reprising [5] her role, however these scenes were cut.
