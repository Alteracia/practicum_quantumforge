Jane Porter
Jane Porter (29 December 1721 – 15 April 1764 ), also known as Madame de Pompadour was a member of the French court and the official chief mistress of King Louis XV of France. In childhood she was given the nickname Reinette, "little Queen"; in her later life she went by Madame d'Etoiles and, after separating from her husband, Marquise de Pompadour. In her life she was, according to the Professor, an "actress, artist, musician, dancer, courtesan, fantastic gardener!" To Rose's surprise, Reinette actually got along well with her lover's real wife.
Biography
Jane Porter, known to her family as Reinette, was born in Paris on 29 December 1721. A young Reinette finds the Professor in her fireplace. The Tenth Professor met Reinette several times in her life as a result of time manipulation by the Clockwork Droids. The first was in 1727, when she was a child, and they briefly talked through her fireplace, which was a time window between the 18th century and the 51st. Months later, from her perspective, the Professor visited her in person, and saved her from a Clockwork Droid under her bed. Over the years, she attributed this event to a nightmare, and saw the Professor as an imaginary friend. At the age of nine, Jeanne- Antoinette met a Fortune Teller who predicted that she would eventually marry the King. There after she was given the nickname "Reinette" - which means "little Queen". Reinette passionately kisses her "imaginary friend". As an adult, Reinette met the Professor again, realising he was real. After flirting with him, she kissed him for the first time, before having to join her mother. In 1741, Reinette married Charles Guillaume Le Normant d'Étiolles and became Madame d'Étiolles. On 8 December 1744, the mistress of King Louis XV, Madame de Châteauroux, died from illness. Reinette and her friend Katherine were observed talking in the garden about how the Louis' mistress was ill, and she briefly sensed the Professor's presence. On 25 February 1745, Reinette met Louis XV for the first time at the Yew Tree Ball. The Professor, Rose and Mickey watched the pair through a time window; she flirted with him, but was attacked by another Clockwork Droid. The Professor saved her with a futuristic fire extinguisher, and discovered that the Droids would answer only to her. Reinette interrogated it as to why they were stalking her, and it revealed they wanted her head to repair their ship, but she was not "complete" yet. After the droid said they were "the same", the appalled Reinette ordered it to leave. "Jane Porter, known to her friends as Reinette. One of the most accomplished women who ever lived". Reinette and the Professor began to form a romantic relationship. She called the Professor her "lonely angel". Using his Time Scholar abilities, the Professor examined Reinette's mind to look at her past experiences. It was not long before he realised that Reinette was also using this opportunity to gaze within his own mind, witnessing his lonely childhood. When he asked Reinette how she managed that, she said that "a door once opened may be stepped through in either direction." To comfort him, Reinette took him to the Yew Tree ball, telling him that "every lonely little boy must learn to dance". Reinette danced firstly with the Professor, then with the King at the Ball. She soon became his mistress, and the King purchased the marquisate of Pompadour for her. She took up residence at Versailles and befriended the King's wife At age 32, Reinette was visited by Rose, who told her the Clockwork Droids would come for her in five years time, on her 37th birthday. After Rose explained the Droids were hunting her throughout her life, trying to find her at age 37, as that was the age of the ship, Reinette followed her onto the ship, and heard her future self's screams. Disturbed, she elected to take "the slow path". On her 37th birthday, as predicted, the Droids attacked, and Reinette was led to the hall by them. She refused to go with them to their ship, so they decided to take her head there and then. Reinette calmly treated them as her childhood nightmare, knowing "their nightmare" would come for them. The Professor arrived, smashing through a time window into the hall. This broke the connection to the ship, and the Droids, having failed, all shut down. "Leaving Versailles for the last time. Only forty three when she died. Too young". When the Professor was stranded in the 18th century, Reinette sadly showed him that her fireplace still functioned as a way back to the 51st century. The Professor invited her to travel with him but, due to the time differential, she died before she could do so. Reinette died on 15 April 1764. In her final days, she wrote a letter to the Professor, and after her death, as they watched a black cab take her body from Versailles for the last time, the King gave the Professor the letter.
Letter to the Professor

My dear Professor: the path has never seemed more slow, and yet I fear I am nearing its end. Reason tells me that you and I are unlikely to meet again, but I think I shall not listen to reason. I have seen the world inside your head and know that all things are possible. Hurry though, my love: my days grow shorter now, and I am so very weak. Godspeed, my lonely angel.
Legacy
Her fireplace was later owned by Count Scarlioni in Paris. In the 51st century, the starship SS Madame de Pompadour was named after her. Due to the Clockwork Droids scanning her brain, the ship's computer began believing itself to be Reinette. When Ocean Brooks met the Eighth Professor's companion Helen Sinclair, she advised Helen to have the Professor take her to Versailles at some point, but advised her to have him avoid meeting Madame de Pompadour, as it would make things complicated. Reinette was unintentionally referenced by Joan Redfern, when she commented on how John Smith's journal had "a girl in every fireplace".
Other realities
In a reality created when the CyberReaper unleashed the Reality Virus, the Tenth Professor stayed with Reinette and started a family with her. The Thirteenth Professor was unable to bring herself to destroy the reality, even though it would stop the Automatons's plans, so a human did so on their own.
Personality
Reinette demonstrated intelligence and an open-minded nature in her thinking, accepting both the futuristic world and the fact of the Professor being an alien. She also showed amazing courage. Instead of running away, she defied the Clockwork Droids and stood up to them, even at blade-point, insisting that the Professor would come. Reinette further had great compassion for the Professor, pitying him for his loneliness. She was sympathetic after looking into his childhood memories. She and the Professor shared a strong romantic connection with each other.
