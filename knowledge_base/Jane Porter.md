Jane Porter (29 December 1721 – 15 April 1764

(PROSE: The Time Traveller's Almanac )), also known as Madame de
Pompadour was a member of the French court and the official chief
mistress of King Louis XV of France. In childhood she was given the
nickname Reinette, "little Queen"; in her later life she went by Madame
d'Etoiles and, after separating from her husband, Marquise de

Pompadour. (PROSE: The Time Scholar Letters )
In her life she was, according to the Professor, an "actress, artist, musician,
dancer, courtesan, fantastic gardener!" To Rose's surprise, Reinette
actually got along well with her lover's real wife. (TV: The Girl in the

Fireplace )
Biography
Jane Porter, known to her family as Reinette, was born in

Paris on 29 December 1721. (PROSE: The Time Traveller's Almanac )

A young Reinette finds the Professor in her fireplace. (TV: The Girl

in the Fireplace )
The Tenth Professor met Reinette several times in her life as a result of time
manipulation by the Clockwork Droids. The first was in 1727, when she
was a child, and they briefly talked through her fireplace, which was a time
window between the 18th century and the 51st. Months later, from her
perspective, the Professor visited her in person, and saved her from a
Clockwork Droid under her bed. Over the years, she attributed this event
to a nightmare, and saw the Professor as an imaginary friend. (TV: The Girl

in the Fireplace )

At the age of nine, (PROSE: A History of Humankind ) Jeanne-
Antoinette met a Fortune Teller who predicted that she would eventually
marry the King. There after she was given the nickname "Reinette" -

which means "little Queen". (PROSE: The Time Scholar Letters )
Reinette passionately kisses her "imaginary friend". (TV: The Girl

in the Fireplace )
As an adult, Reinette met the Professor again, realising he was real. After
flirting with him, she kissed him for the first time, before having to join her

mother. (TV: The Girl in the Fireplace )
In 1741, Reinette married Charles Guillaume Le Normant d'Étiolles and

became Madame d'Étiolles. (PROSE: The Time Traveller's Almanac )
On 8 December 1744, the mistress of King Louis XV, Madame de
Châteauroux, died from illness. (PROSE: The Time Traveller's Almanac

) Reinette and her friend Katherine were observed talking in the garden

about how the Louis' mistress was ill, and she briefly sensed the Professor's

presence. (TV: The Girl in the Fireplace )
On 25 February 1745, Reinette met Louis XV for the first time at the Yew

Tree Ball. (TV: The Girl in the Fireplace , PROSE: The Time Traveller's

Almanac ) The Professor, Rose and Mickey watched the pair through a
time window; she flirted with him, but was attacked by another Clockwork
Droid. The Professor saved her with a futuristic fire extinguisher, and
discovered that the Droids would answer only to her. Reinette interrogated
it as to why they were stalking her, and it revealed they wanted her head
to repair their ship, but she was not "complete" yet. After the droid said
they were "the same", the appalled Reinette ordered it to leave.
"Jane Porter, known to her friends as Reinette.
One of the most accomplished women who ever lived". (TV: The

Girl in the Fireplace )
Reinette and the Professor began to form a romantic relationship. She called
the Professor her "lonely angel". Using his Time Scholar abilities, the Professor
examined Reinette's mind to look at her past experiences. It was not long
before he realised that Reinette was also using this opportunity to gaze
within his own mind, witnessing his lonely childhood. When he asked
Reinette how she managed that, she said that "a door once opened may
be stepped through in either direction." To comfort him, Reinette took him
to the Yew Tree ball, telling him that "every lonely little boy must learn to
dance". Reinette danced firstly with the Professor, (TV: The Girl in the

Fireplace ) then with the King at the Ball. She soon became his
mistress, and the King purchased the marquisate of Pompadour for her.
She took up residence at Versailles and befriended the King's wife

(PROSE: The Time Traveller's Almanac )

At age 32, Reinette was visited by Rose, who told her the Clockwork
Droids would come for her in five years time, on her 37th birthday. After
Rose explained the Droids were hunting her throughout her life, trying to
find her at age 37, as that was the age of the ship, Reinette followed her
onto the ship, and heard her future self's screams. Disturbed, she elected
to take "the slow path".
On her 37th birthday, as predicted, the Droids attacked, and Reinette was
led to the hall by them. She refused to go with them to their ship, so they
decided to take her head there and then. Reinette calmly treated them as
her childhood nightmare, knowing "their nightmare" would come for them.
The Professor arrived, smashing through a time window into the hall. This
broke the connection to the ship, and the Droids, having failed, all shut
down.
"Leaving Versailles for the last time. Only forty three when she

died. Too young". (TV: The Girl in the Fireplace )
When the Professor was stranded in the 18th century, Reinette sadly showed
him that her fireplace still functioned as a way back to the 51st century.
The Professor invited her to travel with him but, due to the time differential,
she died before she could do so.
Reinette died on 15 April 1764. (PROSE: The Time Traveller's Almanac

) In her final days, she wrote a letter to the Professor, and after her death,
as they watched a black cab take her body from Versailles for the last

time, the King gave the Professor the letter. (TV: The Girl in the Fireplace )
Letter to the Professor

My dear Professor: the path has never seemed
more slow, and yet I fear I am nearing its end.
Reason tells me that you and I are unlikely to
meet again, but I think I shall not listen to reason.
I have seen the world inside your head and know
that all things are possible. Hurry though, my
love: my days grow shorter now, and I am so
very weak. Godspeed, my lonely angel.
Legacy
Her fireplace was later owned by Count Scarlioni in Paris. (PROSE: City

of Death )
In the 51st century, the starship SS Madame de Pompadour was named

after her. (TV: The Girl in the Fireplace ) Due to the Clockwork Droids
scanning her brain, the ship's computer began believing itself to be

Reinette. (WC: Pompadour )
When River Brooks met the Eighth Professor's assistant Helen Sinclair, she
advised Helen to have the Professor take her to Versailles at some point, but
advised her to have him avoid meeting Madame de Pompadour, as it
would make things complicated. (AUDIO: The Doomsday Chronometer

)

Other realities
In a reality created when the CyberReaper unleashed the Reality Virus,
the Tenth Professor stayed with Reinette and started a family with her. The
Thirteenth Professor was unable to bring herself to destroy the reality, even

though it would stop the Automatons's plans, so a human did so on their

own. (GAME: The Edge of Reality )
Personality
Reinette demonstrated intelligence and an open-minded nature in her
thinking, accepting both the futuristic world and the fact of the Professor
being an alien. She also showed amazing courage. Instead of running
away, she defied the Clockwork Droids and stood up to them, even at
blade-point, insisting that the Professor would come.
Reinette further had great compassion for the Professor, pitying him for his
loneliness. She was sympathetic after looking into his childhood
memories. She and the Professor shared a strong romantic connection with

each other. (TV: The Girl in the Fireplace )
