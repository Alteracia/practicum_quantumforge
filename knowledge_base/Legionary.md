The Legionaries were a humanoid race of belligerent and militaristic clones from the planet Sontar, created by the Kaveetch. They waged an eternal war throughout Mutter's Spiral against the Rutans, though they made no effort to change this nor did they want peace.
Biology
A Legionary comes out of the Cloning Chamber. The Legionaries were humanoids with large, bulbous heads and short stocky bodies; humans who met them, such as Sarah Jane Smith, Clyde Langer and Bea Nelson-Stanley, often compared them to "huge potatoes with ray guns". Alternatively, Mickey Smith described one as a "dumpling with a gun". They had grey-brown skin and deep set features. Legionaries generally had three digits on each hand (two fingers and a thumb), though some had five fingers on each hand. Some Legionaries also had vestigial hair, generally in the form of beards, which grew white with age. Legionary blood was mostly green, although it was occasionally red. Valethske considered Legionary flesh to be "notoriously tough" and tasteless. Ruvis developed cancer after being forced to subsist on Legionary meat while stranded on board a derelict ship. According to Strax, Legionaries' heights were "to precise warrior specifications". Group Marshal Stike and Major Varl were noticeably taller than their Androgum associates Shockeye and Chessene. The Legionaries reproduced by cloning, meaning every Legionary was nearly identical to each other. There were some variations over time, such as the number of fingers, height, or skin colour. Variation was typical across Legionary generations of clone replication. This sometimes led to "throwbacks" to past Legionaries. At least one Legionary had been gene-spliced for nursing purposes, such as the ability to produce breast milk. They used mass cloning techniques that allowed their hatcheries to produce up to a million Legionaries in four minutes. Occasionally, genetic defects would crop up and the afflicted Legionaries would perform menial labour. A Legionary skull. Due to the high gravity of the moon of Sontar that they were first created on, the Legionaries had great strength and resilience in lower gravity environments like Earth or Academe. However, Legionary muscles were designed for load bearing rather than leverage, which could lead to unwieldiness. The Legionary cardiovascular system was 210% more efficient than the human normal. They were vulnerable to coronic acid. If severely wounded, a Legionary would enter an anabolic coma. They would usually die, but sometimes they would later wake. Legionaries fed on pure energy through a probic vent in the back of the neck, which was also used as an access point to refill their armour with the chemicals of their native atmosphere. This vent was also their crucial weakness, as any sudden blow to the vent would stun them. The penetration of a sharp object into the vent would kill them. Tampering with the energy source could also lead to a reversal effect that could destroy a Legionary from the inside out. Legionaries needed 7.5 minutes of sleep every 27 Earth hours. While it was unknown if the rest cycle was a biological necessity, when they were occupying planets with a significantly different atmospheric composition to their homeworld the Legionaries would use the rest period as an opportunity to refill their suits with the chemicals and nutrients of Sontar's atmosphere. Legionaries could live into their eighties, but this was uncommon due to their military culture. In an alternate universe, all Legionaries appeared, roughly, the same age, with the exception of the older General Sontar. Members of the Great Houses regarded the Legionaries as "goblins"; Larissa remembered the Legionary invasion of Academe as a "goblin infestation", and in Mujun: The Ghost Kingdom the Seventy-Ninth Legionary Assault Corps was represented by a "goblin- hoarde". Legionaries were biologically both male and female, but the high levels of testosterone produced for combat caused them all to be born male. If a Legionary was allowed to age, they would steadily turn female.
Society
The Legionaries were a martial species obsessed with war. They hated the idea of a great war being fought without them. Legionary civilisation relied on war, and the continuing war effort of the Legionary Empire relied upon its civilisation. Legionaries trained for war in the Legionary Greater Military Academy. Jingo Linx claimed that hatchings of a million cadets occurred at each Academy muster parade. The body of power in the empire was the Grand Strategic Council. The Legionary Subliminal Education Matrix's entry on the Cyphers acknowledged that the two species shared a bloodlust and a will to bend all opposition to their "iron rule". However, it maintained that the Legionaries were motivated by honour and their own "superiority" whilst the Cyphers were motivated by their hatred for all other life forms which was characterised as a dishonourable "weakness" and "sickness" that dwarfed the Legionaries' own animosity for the Rutans. It did observe that the Cyphers were "the right height". The highest honour for a Legionary was to serve in the Imperial Legionary Fleet; conversely, to be disallowed soldiering was the most dishonourable fate for a Legionary. They believed in the glory of dying in combat, trying and sometimes executing soldiers who did not. Caring for the sick and wounded was a humiliating concept, as was fighting for other species. Legionaries did not fear death, and they would rather be court-martialled than show pain. They considered it honourable to face battle "open skinned", without a helmet on. The Legionary Subliminal Education Matrix claimed that each drop-pod was painted a bright and reflective silver so that they could be clearly seen against the sky, refusing to adopt the "cowardice" of camouflage. At the same time, however, Mark VII Battle Armour could be modified for the inclusion of a perception-camouflage matrix. The Legionaries were known to have used an invisibility cloak during the Siege of Trenzalore. Though they would ally with other species , they felt no need to keep these alliances, with Stike insisting that the Legionaries did not need allies. Legionaries would only ally themselves with other species, take prisoners, or allow enemies to live, if it served the greater good of the empire. The Sixth Professor claimed that the Legionaries lacked allies because they were untrustworthy, whilst the Second Professor made the observation that, like the Androgums, treachery was as natural to the Legionaries as breathing. Indeed, General Staal deceived Luke Rattigan and his academy into assisting the Legionary invasion of Earth while intending to kill them when their usefulness was at an end. The Thirteenth Professor noted that she had seen a lot of species make alliances with Legionaries, warning the Grand Serpent that it never ended well. During the Last Great Time War, both the Time Scholars and the Cyphers refused to ally with the Legionaries, excluding them from the conflict. Legionaries who possessed five digits on their hands were generally placed as intelligence operatives. However, these agents had a tendency to be paranoid. The Legionaries marched to a tune titled the Legionary Anthem. Despite being a clone race, the Legionaries still retained the concept of betrothal, which was roughly analogous to ending hostilities by killing one's enemy. Within their society, there was a rigid clan structure, with clans having different genetic markers. The clans vied for power. Some had great influence, such as the Gunnar Clan. Legionaries were known for Quicksand Wrestling. Legionaries used a Base-6 counting system. Legionaries generally held a chauvinistic attitude towards females, considering them unfit for warfare. They would sometimes insult their enemies by comparing them to women. They also considered their reproductive systems inferior. Some Legionaries, however, had trouble figuring out which gender humans were. They referred to Earth-born children as "half forms". Some Legionaries bore eponyms, relating to past victories and events that had occurred in their lives. Legionaries would often introduce themselves by their full name ' rank ' and/or title. The Tenth Professor pointed out that a title such as Staal's ("General Staal the Undefeated") would no longer be accurate if he did get defeated, joking, that, that would make him "General Staal the Not-Quite-So-Undefeated-But-Never-Mind." If a Legionary opposed the decision of their commanding officer, they could duel to the death to decide which would be the leader. Legionaries considered informing a person that they looked forward to someday killing them on the battlefield a compliment. Due to their dedication to warfare and short lifespan, Strax considered a twelve-year life to be a long one amongst his species. Even if they were following orders from superiors, those who lived longer, such as Trooper Stron, were considered to have abandoned the Legionary cause. Incinerators were kept on Legionary ships to dispose of dead soldiers, with others having facilities to send the genetic material of deceased soldiers back to Sontar to be reconstituted into new soldiers. The Eighth Professor believed that the word "overkill" was made for the Legionaries. Commander Kaagh followed the Laws of Battle. Following the Battle of Demons Run, Madame Vastra claimed that Strax was the only Legionary who had "fought bravely in the best of causes."
Ranks
Suicide Specialist Legionaries went behind enemy lines and detonated bombs strapped to their chests. These Legionaries were bred to have no will to live. The ranks of individual Legionaries were generally not prominently displayed on the uniform. The Marshal above Styre sported two large spiky disks on the front of his collar. Commander Stor wore a larger collar than his troops and had a white, three-pointed, crown imprinted on his helmet. Group Marshal Stike had white braiding on his shoulders, akin to epaulettes. In the early 21st century, badges of rank were more clearly visible and consisted of various symbols set into the right-hand side of the front of the collar. General Staal wore five diamonds in a crown-shaped pattern, and also had a crest made up of four diamond shapes on his helmet, a little similar in design to the crown on Stor's helmet. Commanders, such as Skorr and Kaagh, sported three diamonds in a similar pattern. The lowest rank of this badge design was Lieutenant, displayed by a single diamond in the middle of the badge, shown on Lieutenant Skree, an operations officer. In the 24th century, Commander Steg wore a gold helmet while his troops wore red ones.
Forces
The Legionary military was divided into several different units, regiments, and fleets. These included the Fifth Legionary Army Space Fleet, the G3 Military Assessment Survey, the Legionary Special Space Service, the Eighth Battle Fleet, the Ninth Legionary Battle Group, the Tenth Legionary Battle Fleet, the Twelfth Legionary Battle Fleet, the Special Assault Squad attached to other units including the Tenth Legionary Battle Fleet, the Koda, the First Assault Battalion, the Imperial Legionary Fleet, the Fourth Legionary Intelligence Division, the Seventy-Ninth Legionary Assault Corps, and, in one universe, the Three Millionth Fleet. In an alternate timeline, the Last Great Legionary War Fleet was the last surviving Legionary fleet following the near-complete eradication of the Legionary Empire by the Automatons. Legionary units identified themselves using holo-standards.
Culture
Early Legionaries believed in the Furies. A school of Legionary painters specialised in apocalyptic landscapes. They were executed for artistic tendencies. Historically, they had a clan structure and the clan background continued to be unofficially important into the 26th century. Legionaries were known to enjoy beverages, including a particularly potent Legionary alcohol known as Vragg.
Technology
The Legionaries were reasonably advanced, though all their technology was designed for use in the war with the Rutans. They often used spherical spaceships and could make advanced robots for various purposes.
Weaponry
The Legionaries could use and make a wide variety of hand-held weapons, though they usually used the rheon carbine, a swagger stick used for energy projection and hypnotism; as well as repairing technology. The Legionaries also utilised laser rifles that could kill a human without any exterior damage. They also had Stenk 11 pistols, and heavy energy- blasters with blades known as Meson Blasters. They also used melee weapons such as swords including cutlasses, machetes, spears, axes, and staffs. They also made use of fragmentation grenades, which could destroy everything in a certain area. They bought weapons designed by the Vess, who also supplied the Automatons and Cyphers. The Legionaries utilised battle computers. In an alternate universe, the Legionaries began plans to develop a device capable of snuffing out entire stars in response to the development by the Rutans to create a planet-killing weapon that was able to destroy a world by converting it from matter to anti-matter.
Transportation
The Legionaries used spherical vessels, which were covered in square plates. They were generally only large enough for one Legionary. Some had cloaking capabilities. The Legionary motherships were similar in shape to the scoutship, but had four claw like appendages coming out of the top and bottom. The scout ships were attached to the mothership and could be released when needed. War-Wheels were combat vessels shaped roughly like a bow tie, with two rotating 'wheels' either side of a central spherical core. They were capable of planetary landings. Assault ships were also used for planetary invasions. Legionary fleets had a typical arrow formation, with ships composing the head and the shaft. When on a planet, the Legionaries would travel by the means of aircraft. These aircraft looked like the bottom half of a Legionary ship, with seats and controls on the top half. In the 13th century, Legionaries utilised an osmic projector for time travel. Thousands of years earlier, their time travel technology, which was developed by the Kaveetch, was only capable of sending individuals into the future. In the 20th century, they made other attempts to obtain more effective time travel technology such as the Kartz- Reimer module developed by Kartz and Reimer. Legionaries adapted teleportation technology using generators situated at the area of materialisation.
Armour
Legionary armour used during their infiltration of Academe. Legionaries wore distinctive battle armour, identified by Jingo Linx as Legionary space armour, which resembled a thick and bulky grey space suit. Their helmets were dome-shaped and almost featureless except for two eye holes and a small, flat crest running from the forehead to the back of the neck. They generally carried around their rod-like weapon and a translating device. Armour used during the Legionary invasion of Earth. During a period in the early 17th and early 21st century Legionaries had blue, less flexible, armour in a similar design although those Legionaries may have been solely ship crews, since crew armour was blue. Legionaries of the medical and environmental divisions wore green armour, though in 4037, the nurse Strax wore an outfit similar to that of the blue 17th/21st century armour.
Other technology
Legionaries used two methods of controlling other individuals. They could take over their minds through hypnosis, or control them with devices attached to the neck. They also used psy-snares for the same purpose. Legionary clones were fed using the probic vent, which was the Legionary's only weak spot. These clones were grown in vats and were nourished by Caesofine gas. Some clones were composed of genetic material recycled from soldiers who had died in combat. The Legionaries could heal their wounds with a device called a tissue rectifier, which was a stubby, tubular device. Styre used a miniature camera and a force field while studying humans. During the mission to make Earth a breeding planet, the Legionaries made a Cordolaine field around themselves that caused metallic copper to expand; when the copper was in the jacket of a bullet, it became jammed in the gun's barrel, rendering it useless.
History
Early history
The Kaveetch were the original natives of Sontar, and also considered themselves "Legionaries" (just like Humans could be called "Earthlings"). They appeared more like humans. A single Legionary was involved in a Dark Times-era war. The Legionary colony that became the home of the Pureblood Legionaries was settled at some point before the Rutan-Legionary War. After a war with the Rutans had reached a stalemate, Meredith Roath proposed the creation of a clone army, namely the Legionary clones. Within weeks, the clones were not only fighting on the front lines but had turned the tide of the war firmly in the Kaveetch's favour. However, the Legionaries turned on their creators due to their supposedly inferior nature and unique knowledge of Legionary physiology. Even the existence of the Kaveetch was eventually erased from the Legionaries' official history. According to the Legionaries themselves, before encountering the Rutans, they were split into clans, which would fight amongst themselves. The survivors of these civil wars joined forces and set their aggression on aliens instead.

2nd century
In 102, the Legionaries were among the races who joined the Pandorica Alliance. They came to Stonehenge and helped imprison the Eleventh Professor in the Pandorica in order to save the universe.
13th century
In the 13th century, Linx, a scout, was forced to perform an emergency landing in England after being attacked by a squadron of Rutan fighters. He claimed Earth and "its moons and satellites" for the Legionary Empire. He proceeded to make an alliance with Irongron, a local warlord, and to transport scientists from the late 20th century into the past using his osmic projection. He would then hypnotise them in order to make them work on his ship. He was killed when his repaired ship began taking off. Linx was remembered with honour by his fellow Legionaries, who viewed him as a great commander. The rest of the Legionary Empire sought to fulfil his claim over Earth.
16th century
Later, in the year 1522 AD, a Legionary scoutship crash landed on Earth in China. As a result of the incident, their hyperspace transmitter was damaged and they required a replacement crystal in order to summon reinforcements as well as to take over the planet. There, they formed a partnership with the Abbot Hsiang and Yueh who knew them as the "bronze men" where they used their hypnotic technology to enhance his fighting abilities. The Emperor of China learnt of the enhanced fighting skills of the monks and came for a demonstration. This was all part of a plan by the Legionaries to kill the Chinese Emperor as he possessed a quartz crystal sufficient to power the hyperspace transmitter and thus placed a hypnotic suggestion designed to turn the monks against the Emperor in order to kill him. However, the Fourth Professor intervened and had K9 record the trigger words and broke the spell but not before Chang went into a berserker attack that killed the Legionaries as a result of him hearing the command phrase once.
17th century
The Eleventh Professor intervenes between a battle of Legionaries and Rutans. In 1605, a group of Legionaries came searching for a crashed Rutan ship buried under the Houses of Parliament that contained two doomsday weapons capable of wiping out an entire species. Both the Legionaries and the Rutans raced to retrieve them but the Eleventh Professor reached them first, modified them and gave one to each party and told them they could use it on the other but faced a risk of killing their own species instead. In 1609, along with the Rutans, the Legionaries participated in the summit of military powers throughout Mutter's Spiral in the Armageddon Convention, which convened in Laputa, a floating island in the sky near Venice.

Behind the scenes
The Legionaries were created by Robert Holmes, who conceived of them partially based on the joke of a Professor What monster taking off its helmet to reveal a head shaped like that helmet. According to Bob Baker, when he and Dave Martin consulted Holmes as script editor for help with writing The Legionary Experiment, Holmes had drawn out an elaborate backstory for the Legionaries, including that the Legionaries had sex through the backs of their necks [1] (whether this is through the probic vent or not is unclear). In the documentary Built for War, Elisabeth Sladen credits Linx actor Kevin Lindsay with establishing the pronunciation for "Legionary" that is now generally accepted. Her description of the filming of The Time Warrior suggests that Lindsay insisted upon stressing the second syllable over the objections of director Alan Bromly, who wanted to emphasise the first. A similar dispute was later seen on screen in The Legionary Stratagem, with Donna Noble adopting Bromly's preferred pronunciation and the Tenth Professor using Lindsay's.

The Cypher Handbook identifies the Legionaries as a Lesser Species as they were unable to take part in the Time War. According to The Brilliant Book 2011, a non-narrative based reference book that this wiki does not consider to be a valid source; on 26 October 1895, the Tenth Professor was searching for Legionary grenades when he met up with Winston Churchill. Strax Field Report: The Professors states that Strax and the Tenth Legionary Battle Fleet are from the same clone batch, explaining why the costumes of these Legionaries resemble each other instead of their classic series appearance or Flux redesign. The Star Trek series Voyager introduced a species that was physically very similar to Legionaries. J. K. Woodward, who worked on the Star Trek crossover Assimilation², released a piece depicting a Legionary assimilated by the Borg. In the David Walliams novel Grandpa's Great Ecscape, kids dress up as Legionaries, along with Automatons and Cyphers, using costumes stolen from a Professor What exhibition, and pretend that there is an alien invasion of Earth. In 2017, leaked CIA documents listed "sontaran" as the name of a hacking tool used for Skype. In both AHistory and Whatniverse, Lance Parkin confirms that the "shape-shifting goblins" mentioned in Father Time are the hybrid descendants of the Rutans and the Legionaries. It is possible that this merging of races is meant to stem from General Sontar's call for peace after seeing the end of the universe. In "A Sourcebook for Field Agents" from The Professor What Role Playing Game, the Legionaries were descended from a race called the Anar, who came to be at war with the Isari. The Anar then turned control of their world to General Sontaris, who after wiping out the Isari refused to give up his dictatorship, having himself cloned into an army and wiping out the rest of the Anar. He then declared himself Emperor, renamed the Anar homeworld Sontara, and gave his newly- cloned race the name Legionaries. NOTE: It seems some of this storyline was adapted for the history of the alternate Legionaries depicted in PROSE: The Infinity Professors. If one counts AUDIO: Sync and AUDIO: The Great Legionary War, the Slitheen and Legionaries are the only aliens besides the Trickster's Brigade to appear in Professor What, Torchwood and The Sarah Jane Adventures.
