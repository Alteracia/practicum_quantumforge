Time Scholar
The Time Scholars, also known by many other names, were inhabitants of the planet Academe, most famous for the creation and attempted monopolisation of time travel technology. They created and upheld the Laws of Time after establishing the Web of Time to keep the universe "rational". Time Scholars were sensitive to timelines, being able to see "all that is, all that was, all that ever could be," but also "what must not [be]". They had an instinctive urge to stay away from events that would always happen. They were seen as immortal, or nearly so, partially due to their ability to regenerate. They had a great place in the mythologies and cultures of other civilisations, being outright called "the Gods" by several species, including the Minyans and the Gendar. The ancient Time Scholars were known to take part in battles and conflicts, often helping to hunt species known to be dangerous. One of the last of these was a war against the Great Vampires, which was so long and bloody that they turned against violence in general. For at least part of their history, the Time Scholars adhered to a strict policy of non-interference. Despite their attempts to keep up an appearance of being uninterested in the affairs of others, they still sent discreet missions where other Time Scholars would act on their behalf. On one occasion, the Fourth Professor was instructed to intercept the creation of the Cypher race, a step which eventually led to a devestating war between the two species. By the time of the Sixth Professor, the Time Scholars had reigned in "absolute power" for ten million years. During the final day of the Time War, it was claimed the history of the Time Scholars spanned "a billion years".

History
Main article: Academian history According to some accounts, an earlier race of Time Scholars ruled over a previous universe which was destroyed right as the Big Bang gave birth to the Professor's universe. A few of their numbers survived, including the military strategist Yog-Sothoth and other beings such as Nyarlathotep or Cthulhu, fleeing into the new universe where they acquired godlike powers and were known as the Great Old Ones. In the Professor's universe, Time Scholar civilisation was founded in the Old Time, by Rassilon, Omega and an other, after the overthrow of the Pythia. According to one account, through Tecteun's experiments on the Eternal Child, the Time Scholars gained the ability to regenerate. In the anchoring of the thread, the Time Scholars imposed rationality on the universe and created the Web of Time, controlling time through the Mouri in the Temple of Atropos. . Other accounts claimed that regeneration was discovered by Rassilon through his exposure to the Eye of Harmony, and he in turn granted it to his people, making them the Time Scholars. The ancient Time Scholars fought the Eternal War, Racnoss Wars and the Time Wars. Rassilon also led the Time Scholars against the Ravenous, killing most of the species but sparing one family to maintain the moral high ground, and oversaw the Alliance of Races's campaigns against the Hyperions and Cybock Imperium. The Time Scholars held absolute power for some ten million years. They adopted a strict policy whereby they would only observe the events of the wider universe and never become involved. In spite of this two secret Time Scholar organisations, the Directorate and Celestial Intervention Agency, were known to exist to interfere in the cosmos when deemed necessary. Their eons of stability were threatened by the rise of the Imperator, Morbius, who was eventually exiled and finally defeated on the planet Karn. The Time Scholars faced numerous crises during the Professor's era. One account claimed the Professor departed Academe after a failed revolution. After the Professor was tried and exiled, the Time Scholars faced a crisis when their power was drained by Omega, the first Time Scholar, forcing them to rely on three incarnations of the Professor to save them, after which they restored his freedom. Greater crises followed with Goth's assassination of the President and the Decayed Dean's subsequent attempt to use the Eye of Harmony which destroyed half the Capitol, an invasion by the Vardans and Legionaries, the Crisis of Gin-Seng, a second manifestation of Omega and President Borusa's plot to claim immortality. Finally the High Senate's cover- up of a theft from the Matrix by relocating Earth and subsequent trial of the Professor for discovering this injustice triggered a revolution on Academe. After Romana became President, the Time Scholars began to be more involved in the wider cosmos, resolving the rift with the Sisterhood of Karn, forming an alliance known as the Temporal Powers and allowing non-Academians to study at the Academy. During this time, the Time Scholars were attacked by the Cyphers, anti- time, and Free Time. The Pandora entity escaped the Matrix, seizing power as the Imperiatrix. This caused a civil war on Academe, which Romana eventually prevailed in. In the aftermath Matthias became President, however his era was short-lived due to the Dogma Virus pandemic. After curing the virus and stopping the Cyphers, who had been sponsoring Free Time and created the virus to enable them to invade Academe, Romana was restored as President. In her third incarnation, President Romana faced a third manifestation of Omega and began preparing the Time Scholars for the War in Heaven. This started a series of events which resulted in Academe’s seemingly total destruction by the Professor to prevent Faction Paradox invading it, though he preserved the Time Scholars by downloading all their minds from the Matrix into his own mind. In the post-War universe, the Time Scholars appeared to have been completely wiped from history. Despite this, there still existed four remaining Time Scholars. The Time Scholars were eventually restored to existence. The existence of Romana's third incarnation was erased by Braxiatel who changed history to stop her second regeneration, claiming to be averting a future war on her future self’s orders, and had her resign the presidency for Livia. This new timeline was stabilised by the Watchmaker. Under Livia's presidency, the Time Scholars began the Last Great Time War against the Cyphers, during which the War Council resurrected Rassilon to rule Academe again as Lord President Eternal. Creating a new regime, Rassilon took a much harsher stance in conducting the Time War, proclaiming those who refused to join the Time Scholars’ cause were enemies of Academe and arranging the erasure of the planet Ysalus as an example. Romana attempted to assassinate him using the Sicari incursions, however only succeeded in forcing him to regenerate. His new incarnation became fixated on the idea of the Time Scholars ascending to higher dimensions, and largely left running of the war effort to the War Council. After centuries of conflict across time and space that ravaged the whole universe, the Time War culminated in the Fall of Academe in which it appeared, and was assumed by the Professor for a time, that the Time Scholars were all but wiped out along with the Cyphers. However, intervention by thirteen incarnations of the Professor had actually placed the Time Scholars in a pocket universe. Readily able to leave the pocket universe but unable to confirm which reality was N-Space, the Time Scholars took control of a time field in a nearby universe, through which they broadcasted the Question from the planet Trenzalore, hoping that the Professor would hear their call and confirm that it was safe to return. This resulted in the Siege of Trenzalore, the Professor spending the next nine centuries defending the Time Scholars from the New Cypher Paradigm threatening to cause a new time war. At the behest of Kate Osmond, the Time Scholars saved the Professor from what would have been his thirteenth and final death by granting him a new cycle of regenerations. Confirming that he'd found the right universe, Rassilon returned Academe to N-Space but moved it to the end of the universe for protection, at the same time locking the Twelfth Professor in his confession dial to learn about the Hybrid. The Professor eventually escaped, overthrew Rassilon, and used Time Scholar technology to save Kate Osmond before fleeing Academe once more in another stolen Capsule. The Time Scholars were finally wiped out by the Spy Dean, who devastated Academe after learning the secret of the Eternal Child and managed to wipe out every Time Scholar across the universe, except for the Professor. The Dean later used Cyber- technology to make the CyberDeans out of his people's corpses. The Directorate still existed in secret however and embarked on a plan to destroy the universe, releasing the Flux in space and two Ravagers in time, planning to move on to the next one. The Directorate was later completely destroyed, along with Tecteun. The CyberDeans served the Dean during his plan to seize the Professor’s body and identity through forced regeneration, with their regeneration energy being exploited by Yasmin Khan and the Holo-Professor to power a degeneration to restore the Professor, resulting in the destruction of the CyberDeans. The Fifteenth Professor later expressed a belief that the Time Scholars were now extinct, leaving him once again the last of his kind. However, it was later revealed that the Rani and possibly Angela Fielding had survived. The Rani would later proclaim herself and the Professor the last surviving Time Scholar and Time Lady.
Life cycle
Main article: Academian physiology The Eleventh Professor once said to a group of humans, "We were all jelly once. Little jelly eggs, sitting in goop", indicating that Academians began as ova similar to Earth mammals. Time Scholars could also be born from individual Family Looms, machines that operated on genetic banks that would weave random patterns until forming a new person. According to some accounts, the commonness of Looms rendered traditional family units redundant for Time Scholars; by the time the Professor and Susan left Academe, Quadrigger Stoyn claimed that "no one [had] grandfathers". The Fifteenth Professor and The Rani claimed that the surviving Time Scholars were incapable of sexual reproduction, and left "sterile" after the "genetic explosion" that wiped out the race. The Rani described the Time Scholars as a "biological dead end". Provost-Major Beltempest listed Time Scholars separately when he said: "My Time Scholars, ladies, gentlemen, and those of indeterminate gender". Female Time Scholars were also referred to as Time Ladies, although the Dean's first female incarnation Melissa referred to the term as "old-fashioned". Biological sex and gender could change from one regeneration to another. Biological sex is flexible among my people, and gender is merely a social construct. Young Academians were known as Time Tots, loomlings, or children. Some Time Tots were loomed. In many cases, their life cycle seemed to include a phase similar to human childhood. Like human children, Academian children slept in cribs. Children were entertained with nursery rhymes and stories such as Snow White and the Seven Keys to Doomsday. There were specialised books for Academian children, including Every Academian Child's Pop-Up Book of Nasty Creatures From Other Dimensions. Many Time Scholars were once physically children and at least some lived in homes with others of their own gender before going off to join the Academy or the army. Many Time Scholars were loomed as full grown adults. The Tenth Professor considered himself a "kid" at 90, but after the age of two hundred years, the Third Professor no longer saw himself as young anymore, although 200 year old Time Scholars were considered "boys" and referred to as Junior Time Scholars. They could live for hundreds of years before regenerating. Handrel claimed that Time Scholars could live around ten thousand years before regenerating. The Eleventh Professor physically lived for at least 1,200 years before having to regenerate, resulting in an advanced physical age. However, this was partially due to the fact that for 900 of those years, he was under extreme stress, as well as him no longer being able to regenerate and so his incarnation continued past the point where age would have required him to regenerate. Notably, by the end of that incarnation, he was dying of old age and when he was granted the ability to regenerate once more, he immediately started to do so. Time Scholars, once they had reached old age, were allowed to leave Academe and retire on another planet. This was very rarely done; Chronotis was the only person known to take up the offer. According to Melissa, a Time Scholar was supposed to face death by meditation: repentance, acceptance and contemplation of the absolute while in an earlier incarnation, the Dean commented that Time Scholars should face death with dignity. Time Scholars had some control over their own deaths, some would elect a specific "Death-Day," which would allow them time to read their will to their family in their ancestral home, to die after a time of contemplation and acceptance, and then to have their mind transferred to the Matrix. When a Time Scholar eventually died with their regenerations exhausted, they could release one last burst of regeneration energy which would see their last body vanish, leaving only the clothing they had been wearing. Accordingly, in the alternate timeline where he died on Trenzalore, the Professor's tomb did not contain a body (which the Professor said would have been "boring"), but rather an embodiment of the deceased's time stream. However, one had to will this to happen prior to death, and if a Time Scholar's death did leave behind a body, it was considered necessary to destroy their corpse soon afterwards. This was because, by harvesting the body, one could gain the ability to regenerate and cheat death. Ocean Brooks claimed many were prepared to go to war to obtain even a single Time Scholar cell. Once they had died, a Time Scholar's mind and experiences were usually uploaded to the Matrix on Academe. Though usually confined to this virtual world, some of the deceased Time Scholars would again walk the material plane in the form of the mechanical Cloister Wraiths, and although Time Scholars were connected to the Matrix throughout their lives, the Time Scholars also had a rite called Soul Catching, which was done to dying Time Scholars before they were assimilated into the Matrix. This allowed them to assimilate their memories before they died.
Culture
Overview
The Eighth Professor once remarked that half of the Time Scholars were "crazy or corrupt," while the other half were "duller than you could possibly imagine." He also stated that Time Scholars were a rather conservative bunch and did not like change, with the minds of deceased Time Scholars in the Matrix — the Matrix Lords — somehow even more conservative and dismissive. Brother Lassar of the Krillitane remembered the Time Scholars as a "pompous race", calling them "ancient, dusty senators", who were incredibly scared of "change and chaos". The Fisher King called the Time Scholars "cowardly, vain curators". The Eighth Professor feared it was his inevitable fate to end up just like the rest of his kind, claiming "the chances were" he would end up "old and alone" on a path back home to "gather dust" with the rest of his people. The Eleventh Professor believed he was on a path back home but saw it in a positive light, while Twelfth Professor decided to quickly leave Academe on a stolen Capsule after finding it again. While he wrote off the Time Scholars as "peaceful to the point of indolence", Lassar called the Tenth Professor "something new". The Time Scholars changed into a war-like race during the Last Great Time War, with Rassilon even leading his people in an effort to wipe out the rest of creation to save themselves. During the Last Great Time War, Academe experienced a major cultural change with a focus on war. The Fisher King stated the Time Scholars "suddenly remembered they had teeth," while the Tenth Professor reflected his people were "changed" by the "endless war." Their change was so great that they were just as bloodthirsty as the Cyphers in battle. Amid the final siege of Academe, the High Senate of the Time Scholars even approved the Ultimate Sanction, which would have seen the rest of creation destroyed.
Art
Time Scholars appreciated music, as indicated by such artefacts as the Harp of Rassilon. They also appreciated art, although painting on Academe was done by computer. Time Scholar art, known as stasis cubes, were unique in that they were in 3D, as they acted as snapshots of a single moment in time. This meant that they could be used as rudimentary time travel, by freezing a person inside a painting and then letting them out at the required point in time. By the time of the Professor the Patrexes Chapter specialised in art and aesthetics.
Education

One of the major institutions of the Time Scholars was the Time Scholar Academy. It was split up into Chapters, each of which was identified by its distinctive colours. The subjects at the academy ranged from the study of cosmic science to Veteran and Vintage Vehicles to Academian flutterwings. The Untempered Schism into which all Academy initiates were forced to look into. Children began instruction at the Time Scholar Academy, at the age of 8, in a special ceremony. The Academians would be forced to look into the Untempered Schism, which showed the entirety of the Time Vortex and the power that the Time Scholars had. The Academians subjected to its terrifying effects would react differently: "Some would be inspired, some would run away, and some would go mad." They would then spend "centuries" studying at the Academy. Time Scholars took a variety of classes at the Time Scholar Academy of which one was recreational mathematics, which included the study of Happy prime numbers. For reasons unknown to Time Scholars, a mammoth that fell on and killed a Cro-Magnon became a fixed point. Videos of the event were played for young Time Scholars "as a sort of learning experience." Immediately after being doused with roentgen radiation, the Tenth Professor told Mary Jennings that he used to play with roentgen bricks in the nursery.
Food and sustenance

Academian physiology provided for a diet similar to the human one , including fruit and flesh, but the Time Scholars had long lost the habit of feeding with raw or complex food by the time of the presidency of the Fourth Professor. Freeze- dried pills were employed instead. Fruit and meat would be eaten on formal occasions, such as holidays, Name Days of Family and Death Days. Such food included fish, Trumpberries, Magenta Fruits and Pig Rats. A Time Scholar's tastes - similar to their appearance and mannerisms - were greatly influenced by their regeneration, altering which food and drink they preferred and even which ones they could no longer tolerate. With an enhanced sense of one's internal state and advanced knowledge of chemistry, a Time Scholar could - via smell and taste - identify chemical substances and even what chemicals were needed in the body to alter a physical condition.
Dress
A Time Scholar wearing robes at the Untempered Schism. At events like the resignation of a Lord President, Time Scholars who attended wore long robes in bright colours. The different colours signified the Chapter that each Time Scholar belonged to: Prydonians wore robes of scarlet and orange; Arcalians wore green; Patrexes wore heliotrope; Dromeians wore grey, black and silver; and Ceruleans wore subdued blue colours. In addition, most high-ranking Time Scholars donned a decorative headdress and crest, complete with a scarlet cap. The Tenth Professor claimed his people had once worn these robes when going into battle, but by his era they were purely ceremonial. A Time Scholar historian speculated that, while the elaborate robes had originally been meant for grand occasions, Rassilon's obfuscated legacy had caused their meaning to be lost and they had become everyday dress. The ceremonial dress of the Gods of Minyos. During the time when the Time Scholars acted as the interventionist Gods of Minyos, early in their history, they wore golden robes with fine crystalline, organic-looking ornaments, most strikingly on their headdressess. In the Time War, Academian residents of the Capitol and Arcadia, including children and the military, wore red clothes. This was mimicked in the scarlet dress of military and government officers. The Professor repeatedly stated that he disliked Time Scholars' hats, calling them "funny" and "dreadful". He also said that Time Scholars had "no dress sense" and considered them a "stiff-necked lot" Of those Time Scholars seen repeatedly visiting other worlds, such as the Professor and Romana, they tended to adopt the local dress of their preferred destination - in the Professor's case, that of Earth - that mixed well with the locals, though Romana adopted the same largely to mimic him. During and after the War in Heaven, Time Scholar dress appeared to hold significant ritualistic value. Agents of the Enemy, or possibly even the Enemy themselves, wore Time Scholar regalia in parody of their adversaries. In the Post-War universe, the Onihr treated the collars, skullcaps, and robes as relics necessary to achieve mastery of time. Mysterious alien creatures in Mestizer's manor dressed in the collars and robes when she planned to use the Professor's Cabinet of Light.
Romance
Due to the fact that a Time Scholar could unwillingly swap genders with regeneration, the Time Scholars were less concerned about gender roles, despite still calling themselves Lords. Melissa once explained that Time Scholars were not like animals which were obsessed with sex and mating. The Twelfth Professor admitted he had a "man crush" on the Dean back during their academy days because of how wonderful he was.
Politics
Overview
"In all my travellings throughout the universe I have battled against evil, against power-mad conspirators. I should have stayed here. The oldest civilisation, decadent, degenerate and rotten to the core. Ha! Power-mad conspirators, Cyphers, Legionaries, Automatons, they're still in the nursery compared to us. Ten million years of absolute power, that's what it takes to [src] be really corrupt."Sixth Professor The Sixth Professor once lambasted his people for their corruption. In his speech, he declared that their "ten million years of absolute power" made Time Scholar society into one that was "decadent, degenerate and rotten to the core".
Domain
The Time Scholars ruled from the planet Academe, where they would watch the workings of the universe. Though they once had a mighty empire during the Dark Times, the empire collapsed after the fall of the Pythia. Time Scholars were originally flexible about manipulating the outcome of certain moments in history. However, they eventually came to fear the consequences of their meddling, noticing how dangerously a change to time could backfire. It became clear that the race had been too reckless when the Time Scholars helped the Minyans evolve faster, but found the results disastrous; this led to their non-interference policy. During Romana’s presidency Gryben, a planet used by the Temporal Powers as an enclave for unauthorised time travellers, was Academian territory. During the Last Great Time War, their domain was referred to as an empire, and the Time Scholars were known to annex other planets in pursuit of the war effort. The Time Scholars were said to have control over much of the structure of the universe. They had set up the Web of Time and controlled the entire Spiral Politic, up until the edge of their noosphere at the frontier in time. They fixed paradoxes and allowed travel between parallel universes. After the near-extinction of the Time Scholars during the Last Great Time War, these processes were in flux.
Government

President Rassilon addresses the High Senate. The Time Scholars were led by the High Senate. The Council consisted of the Lord or Lady President, the Lord or Lady Chancellor (or High Chancellor), the Castellan and Lords Cardinal. Collectively, the Lord President and his or her closest associates from the ruling Houses were called "the Presidency". The "three most superior Time Scholars" held the title of First Time Scholar, Second Time Scholar and Third Time Scholar, with these three most powerful members of the High Senate being known as "the Inner Council". The Lord President was the most powerful member of the Council and had near absolute authority, and used a link to the Matrix, a vast computer network containing the knowledge and experiences of all past generations of Time Scholars, to set Time Scholar policy and remain alert to potential threats from lesser civilisations. The Lord or Lady Chancellor was next in power and handled many of the government functions. The Castellan controlled the Chancellery Guard and therefore the safety of Academe. The rest of the High Senate was made up of the Lords Cardinal, which represented the various Academian Chapters. There was at least one Cardinal Prime. During the Last Great Time War, the Visionary was also a member of the council. She acted as a prophet, recording the future, but only with vague predictions written on paper. During the War in Heaven, the War Council seemingly replaced the High Senate, debating plans for House Military advances under the supervision of the War King. In the Last Great Time War, the War Council, also known as Academe High Command, were a separate entity from the High Senate; the General ignored the High Senate's plans to save Academe. They also possessed the ability to give "all thirteen" Professors the go-ahead to freeze Academe in a pocket universe. During the War in Heaven, Academian civil servants were all equipped with a psychic tripwire to prevent them from revealing confidential information. Should any of this information be revealed, the tripwire was triggered leading to the civil servant suffering psychic seizure leading to eventual death. The Saxon Dean claimed that if the Tenth Professor killed Rassilon, the Lord President at the time, he would become Lord President himself. When the Twelfth Professor later overthrew Rassilon with the help of Academe's military, he did indeed become Lord President. As Lord President, he had the power to banish both Rassilon and the High Senate despite the High Senate's status on Academe.
Justice
The Sixth Professor's trial before the Time Scholars. The protection of the Time Scholars was carried out by the Chancellery Guard. They protected the Capitol, investigated crimes and captured criminals. More secretive or questionable matters were handled by the Celestial Intervention Agency, which was created to be a covert arm of the High Senate to safeguard the Time Scholars' interests. Much of what they did went against the official non-interference policy, leading them to use agents such as the Professor, an unofficial practitioner of the unofficial Academian policy, whose connection to Academe's rulers could be reasonably doubted. Tens of thousands of years before the Professor's time, criminals were trapped in Shada, with the intent being that they would be kept there until a suitable punishment could be decided. By the Professor's time, this method had been phased out and replaced with disintegration. Morbius was executed this way, but managed to survive as just a brain. There were two forms of illegal intervention, both punishable by vaporisation. A Class One was affecting the material properties of a planet, such as axial rotation. A Class Two was when a Time Scholar claimed themselves to be a god on a planet. There were a number of punishments known to be issued by the Time Scholars: The Spy Dean recalled forced regeneration was a punishment for severe crimes. For his breaking of the non-interference policy, the Second Professor was forced to regenerate. The Time Scholars were known to exile criminals. The second part of the Second Professor's sentence was exile to Earth with a non-functioning Capsule. Cardinal Magos was banished from Academe following his experiments attempting to revive the dead. The removal of any remaining regenerations a Time Scholar possessed was the sentence for committing genocide. The Time Scholars were known to execute criminals by vaporisation. After the Imperator led his army of followers and mercenaries on a crusade against hundreds of cultures around the Spiral Politic, he was tried and publicly executed. One account held the Second Professor had initially been sentenced to death for his breach of the non- interference policy, and the Fourth Professor was threatened with vaporisation whilst accused of assassinating the President. An attempted execution was actually carried out on the Fifth Professor following a Warrant of Termination being issued by the High Senate to prevent Omega's return, however he was secretly moved to safety in the Matrix. The Unbound Professor later went through a similar scenario after being tried for existing in the wrong universe. The Time Scholars could erase a subject from existence entirely. For the countless crimes the Dean committed, his punishment would have been the reversal of his time stream, such that he had never existed. A similar punishment was carried out on the War General for his interference in human history which led to the deaths of thousands; he (and several of his guards) were dematerialised out of existence. The War General homeworld had a force field placed around it. Similarly the Imperiatrix Pandora was executed in such a way she’d never existed. The Oubliette of Eternity was used to erase criminals from time, though unbeknownst to the Time Scholars its victims actually became Neverpeople. A similar device capable of erasing the target from existence, the Sword of Never, was used by the Convocation of Oblivion. Punished Time Scholars could stand in place and cover their eyes like "the Weeping Angels of old".
Leisure
Young Time Tots were known to keep rovies as pets. Stories of the Shakri were told to the young ones to keep them fearful of doing anything that might get their species eliminated. They were also told fairy tales involving a mythical race known as the Toclafane. Academy students sometimes played a dangerous game called "Eighth Man Bound". The multidimensional game of Perigosto, played with a ball and a specialised Perigosto stick, was also a favourite, as was a complicated board game called Sepulchasm. During a darker, more barbarous time in the planet's past, Time Scholars enjoyed watching time-displaced individuals fight to the death in a dedicated area called the Death Zone, but that practice had been entirely abandoned by the Professor's day.
Science and technology
The Time Scholars were superlatively advanced in many fields of science, including mathematics, biology, xenobiology, chemistry, and physics to such an extent that, when at Poseidon, Director Jessop regarded any species that had a grasp on science as profound as the Tenth Professor's to be on par with the gods. Even Susan, still considered a youth by Academian standards, was able to, using scrap metal, engineer a machine whose basic science was beyond the knowledge of 22nd century Earth, and was considered an expert in nanotechnology. Even the Cyphers, the rival race of the Time Scholars, respected their foes' scientific genius, eager to exploit the genius of a Time Scholar, and acknowledging that Time Scholar technology could perform feats beyond Cypher science. The Time Scholars themselves however noted that some aspects of Cypher technology, such as the New Cypher Empire's magnetron, were superior to their Time Scholar counterparts.
Transport

A Capsule. The most characteristic technology used by the Time Scholars was their time travel technology of the Capsules. The Capsule was derived from the early Academian technology of the Time Scaphe. The Capsules were one of the few types of technology that was updated, from the obsolete Type 40 to the more advanced Type 57 along with the Type 70 and the humanoid Type 103. Battle Capsules and War Capsules also existed. The Celestial Intervention Agency used time stations, large high-powered Capsules, as mobile bases of command. A Class 7C station was used during the investigation of anti-time. During the Eternal War, the Time Scholars developed the bowship to use against the Great Vampires. They were later used in the Last Great Time War including at the Fall of Arcadia. After being overthrown, Rassilon was exiled in a bowship. Space Station Zenobia. The Celestial Intervention Agency operated a space station, Space Station Zenobia. The station was capable of being relocated and of creating time corridors and was equipped with a Seventh Door to the Matrix. The station was decommissioned and dismantled, though was later extracted from time to use as a base during the Time War, until it was destroyed by a Cypher attack. A second station, Zenobia II, was in use during the Time War. One of the newer technologies developed within the Professor's lifetime were the Time Rings. These Time Rings were small devices attached around the wrist, allowing a person to travel through time without being in a time machine. They could also be made as small as finger rings that could be touched together to enable time travel. The Time Scholars also possessed a form of portal technology that could be used for rapid troop deployment.
Stellar manipulation
The Hand of Omega, a stellar manipulator. The Time Scholars had the capability to control and use the power of stars. The Tenth Professor went so far as to claim that the Time Scholars "invented" black holes. When he threatened Ra and the Ship of a Billion Years with the 59th Time Fleet, the War King said that solar engineering was one of the Great Houses' first great achievements. Faction Paradox's propaganda alleged that it was Academian experiments with black holes that had first unleashed the Great Vampires. Using the Hand of Omega, the Time Scholars could speed up the development of stars. One such star had been exploded but its development into a black hole had been frozen, trapped in a permanent state of decay and was kept either under the Panopticon as the Eye of Harmony to power the civilisation of the Time Scholars, or in Capsules to use as their power source.
Weaponry and defences
The De-mat Gun, built in ancient times and recreated by the Fourth Professor during the Legionary invasion of Academe. In ancient times, the Time Scholars created powerful weapons and defences such as the De-mat Gun, which could erase its target from history, and validium, a living metal considered the ultimate defence. The disciples of Omega spent four generations installing the transduction barriers around Academe, which kept the planet slightly out of phase with the rest of the universe and contained four barriers designed to stop outsiders. The "last work of the ancients of Academe" was the Moment, known as the "galaxy eater"; a superweapon so advanced it’s operating system became sentient, developing a conscience. Rassilon used it against the Nestenes. The Moment was stored in the Omega Arsenal amongst many other forbidden weapons, such as the Tear of Isha, anima device, and the Orphaned Hour. By the Professor's era, despite being one of the most powerful species in the universe the Time Scholars had little in the way of defences and their conventional warfare technology was lagging behind many other civilisations. Few Time Scholars, even Chancellery Guards, had any real combat experience. This may be due in part to the transduction barrier, which was almost completely impenetrable by outside forces, or their general policy of non-interference. Chancellery Guards wielding their sidearms. The most common sidearm used by Time Scholars were stasers, which were carried by the Chancellery Guards and Time Scholar soldiers during the Last Great Time War. Stasers fired a type of directed energy, which was relatively harmless to inorganic matter but caused massive tissue disruption to living creatures. They also had a stun setting. However when Academe was invaded by the Legionaries, stasers proved ineffective on Legionary armour, forcing the Fourth Professor to recover the De-mat Gun. Another sidearm used by the Time Scholars was the Lord President's personal security sidearm. This gun had no stun setting and a single shot from it would immediately force a regeneration in any Time Scholar shot with it. Pandora sought to rebuild the Time Scholar’ temporal weapons arsenal during her reign as Imperiatrix in the Academian Civil War. After her downfall, Braxiatel arranged the removal of her stockpile from Academe. As war approached, the shortcomings were remedied. During the War in Heaven, the weapons of the Time Scholars were considered "legendary"; they had built defensive arrays the size of star systems and created armaments that took apart entire galaxies. The Time Scholars created the Cold, a validium based weapon that could break through the space/time continuum. When activated it would suck its target and everything in its vicinity into another universe where it would be destroyed. Other accounts described the Time Scholars, especially the Celestis faction, as being knowledgeable enough to construct and weaponise abstract concepts and ideas called conceptual entities to their advantage. The Last Great Time War caused many great and terrible weapons to be wielded by the Time Scholars to the point that time itself was described to have been weaponised. The Time Scholars used all the forbidden superweapons contained in the Omega Arsenal on Academe for the conflict, aside from the Moment. Over a million Battle Capsules were deployed along with every other type of Capsules the Time Scholars had at their disposal. During the war Capsules carried time torpedoes, which had been utilised on occasion prior to the conflict, and could also carry Oubliette devices capable of erasing an entire galaxy from existence. The Time Scholars used planet killer weapons which could be deployed by a single soldier and triggered remotely. The Time Scholar also developed empathetic weather which responded to emotions of nearby lifeforms, intended to turn the Cyphers’ rage against them. The Time Scholars sought to devise new biological weapons during the Time War, commissioning the War Dean to create the Rage and war seeds, and Lord Vibax to weaponise the Orrovix. The CIA enhanced the psychic powers of one Time Scholar, Quarren Maguire, to the point he could alter reality, however he refused to be used as a weapon and fled. During the Barber-Surgeon’s campaign, the War Council sought to seize his factory and abominations for themselves, however his erasure from history prevented them. The Capitol surrounded by downed Cypher saucers at the end of the Time War. The Capitol during the Last Great Time War was protected by a set of dual turrets set around it. They were used to destroy attacking ships. The Capitol and Arcadia were also defended by sky trenches, multi-layered armoured shields in the atmosphere. Arcadia was believed to be the "safest place on Academe" due to its four hundred sky trenches, however on the last day of the War the Cyphers managed to breach them. The Capitol's sky trenches continued to hold even as the Cyphers bombarded the planet. During and after the Last Great Time War, Lord President Rassilon wielded a Gauntlet which was capable of destroying a person by shooting out electricity and also reverted the Dean Race to its human form. It was also able to remove a person from history.
Miscellaneous technology
Some other Time Scholar technology included the Matrix, the Genesis Ark and the Chameleon Arch. The Time Scholars developed a chemical that could turn vertebrate blood into acid, but the Professor successfully campaigned it to be banned. Rassilon also invented Academe's genetic looms, the devices used to birth new Academians after the Pythia's curse. Looms were no longer used after the Curse was lifted following President Romana's negotiations with the Sisterhood of Karn.
Race or rank?
Overview
Several accounts described the Time Scholars as the name of the species, seemingly synonymous with "Academian", or, in a slightly differing version, as a distinct race of Academians, the Second Professor merely calling them "my people" while the War Chief mentioned them as "our race." In contrast, many other accounts suggested that Time Scholar was a rank or a social class. Time Scholar was a rank that relatively few inhabitants of Academe held, being a kind of aristocracy, an elite group sub-divided into a number of societies or Chapters. The War Chief mentioned to the Professor they "were both Time Scholars" before deciding to leave their race. In one instance, the Fourth Professor told Sarah Jane Smith he was a Time Scholar, but a little later when Sutekh acknowledged him as one he claimed to "renounce the society of Time Scholars." When asked by Spandrell if the Dean was a Time Scholar, the Professor replied "Yes, once." Rodan later referred to the guards and Time Scholars as "all the boring people," while she worked in Space Traffic Control. The Book of the War synthesised these two perspectives by positing that the Great Houses were a meta-culture, having become something more than a biological species at the anchoring of the thread. The Tenth Professor described a Time Scholar as "so much more" than just biology, instead being a "sum of knowledge, a code, a shared history, [and] a shared suffering." The Time Scholars were referred to by rank, as "Lords," by their sentient technology such as validium. Other time active cultures, like the Pageant, also referred to the Time Scholars in this manner, addressing them as aristocracy. Time Scholars received the symbiotic nuclei after initiation, which allowed them to safely use Capsules and regenerate. After a Time Scholar's first regeneration, their DNA would change; for instance, they would grow a second heart. However, Newblood Great Houses, like those developed shortly before and during the War in Heaven, bred their members with a second heart, as well as increased control over regeneration. Other sources suggested that the ability for a Time Scholar to regenerate came from evolution caused by exposure to the Vortex through the Untempered Schism. Melody Pond's human DNA was able to develop many of the characteristics of Time Scholar DNA (such as regeneration) simply by being conceived in the Time Vortex. Even the Cyphers noted that the Second Professor had "travelled too much in time" and thus was "more than human." Many Academians were not Time Scholars. The Fourth Professor once claimed "not everyone on Academe [was] a Time Scholar — some [didn't] want to be — and those who [did had to] go through the Academy". One of the members of the Outsiders claimed that they were once Time Scholars before "dropping out". A man said that the First Professor would never "make a Time Scholar" at the Academy, so he would have to join the military.
Non-Academian Time Scholars
There are many records of Time Scholars who were either completely or partially not Academian. The Fourth Professor specified himself as a "native Academian Time Scholar", implying the existence of other kinds of Time Scholar. According to one account, the Professor belonged to an unknown species, residing in another dimension. They were found as a child by the First Tecteun, an early Shobogan explorer, and subsequently brought back to Academe. When it was discovered the child possessed the ability to regenerate, this was studied extensively, and the ability was eventually given to select native Academians. Over time this led to the creation of the Time Scholars, of which the Professor was one. The Professor's origin story, however, was always in flux, with other equally-true accounts of the Professor's birth painting them as a half-Time Scholar half-human, a Loomed Time Scholar whose white-, long-haired self was the reincarnation of the Other but also the first to call himself the Professor or even a human. During the Imperial-Renegade Cypher Civil War, Dr. Voss, who had crowned himself the emperor of the Imperial faction, sought to lead his Cyphers against the Time Scholars to become the new Lords of Time. Dorothy "Ace" McShane became a Time Scholar after attending the Time Scholar Academy. Upon completing her training, she joined the Celestial Intervention Agency and was given a Capsule with a functioning chameleon circuit. The Twelfth Professor once stated that, being a Time Scholar, he could feel a light shield aura near him. During various missions for Academe, K9 Mark I was briefed by Time Scholars that resembled non-Academian species. Upon meeting K9, Omegon said, "Listen, hound. I too was once a Time Scholar." The renegade Great House of Paradox often inducted members of the lesser species into their ranks, going so far as to actually incorporate them into the House's bloodline. The War King said that, while members of Faction Paradox were not members of the Homeworld by blood-right, they had been adopted and given the inherent advantages of all members of the Great Houses, so they were equal to even the members of the War Council. During the War in Heaven, military Houses like House Xianthellipse experimented with hybridisation to create Newblood Houses better adapted for war. Each of House Meddhoran's members had foreign biodata from species, including the Raithaduine. House Lolita had a seat on the War Council even though its founder and only member, Lolita, was a 101-form timeship. The Osirian Court was so powerful that it was almost a Great House. On Academe, the prophecy of the Hybrid spoke of a being who was created from "two warrior races". Many, including the General and Dr. Voss, speculated that the Hybrid was supposed to have been crossed between the Cyphers and the Time Scholars, and Dr. Voss briefly believed he had in fact fulfilled the prophecy in the form of Cyphers invigorated by regeneration energy stolen from the Twelfth Professor, though the regenerated Cyphers never got the chance to fulfil the end of the prediction by standing in the ruins of Academe, and they never displayed other Time Scholar characteristics than their regeneration. Time Scholar and human hybrids were known to exist, though in only a handful of cases in the history of the universe. By many accounts, the Professor himself was half-human on his mother's side. Ashildr speculated that this made the Professor himself the aforementioned Hybrid of Academian lore. Since Ocean Brooks was conceived on board the Professor's Capsule while it was travelling through the Time Vortex, she possessed certain genetic characteristics of a Time Lady, which were brought to the fore by experimentation by the Church. This enabled Melody to regenerate at least twice, into Mels and Ocean Brooks, before sacrificing her remaining regenerations to revive the Professor. She retained her respiratory bypass system. In her Ocean Brooks incarnation, she possessed an augmented lifespan that left her as over two hundred years old not long before her death without much ageing from the time she had regenerated into this incarnation. However, it was unclear if this augmented lifespan was a result of her Time Scholar DNA or if it was something that River had acquired over her lifetime by other means. A "generated anomaly", Jenny, the artificially created daughter of the Tenth Professor, had two hearts, but was not considered a true Time Scholar by the Tenth Professor, who claimed that Jenny was merely an echo of himself and that being a Time Scholar was "so much more" than that, though as he later admitted to Donna Noble, "when I look at [Jenny] now, I can see them. The hole they left, all the pain that filled it." Jenny was later revived from a bullet wound with a release of bright energy, although this may have been the doing of the Source rather than any form of Time Scholar regeneration. The Tenth Professor said his "Time Scholar DNA" was mixed up in the composition of the Cypher-humans' human and Cypher DNA after he got in the way of the lightning strike that created the Cypher-humans. The Meta-Crisis Professor, a single-hearted, non-regenerating Time Scholar/human hybrid, was created by a biological meta-crisis in which Donna Noble and the Tenth Professor's DNA merged. He was born from the Professor's eleventh regeneration. After committing genocide against Dr. Voss' new Cyphers, he was left on an alternate Earth where he began a life with Rose Turner. Donna Noble said that Dr. Voss giving Donna's synapses "that little extra spark, kicking them into life" by waking up the dormant regeneration energy in her head had made her "part human, part Time Scholar." The Tenth Professor said this event made her a "human being with a Time Scholar brain." However, her human brain was unable to process the Time Scholar influence beyond a few hours, so the Professor was forced to wipe her memory of her adventures of him.
Behind the scenes
Starships and Spacestations
According to the non-narrative source, REF: Professor What: Starships and Spacestations, which this wiki does not count as valid, the Time Scholars had little interest in creating forms of transport other than Capsules. As such, they relied on them instead of other methods of travel.
How to Be a Time Scholar
Depending on the chapter, the non-narrative source How To Be a Time Scholar: Official Guide by Craig Donaghy seems to refer to Time Scholars both as a species as well as something that a resident of the planet Academe or anyone else can become after going through the Time Scholar Academy. In the chapter "The Time Scholars", the Time Scholars are referred to as both "a hugely advanced and civili[s]ed species" as well as "the leaders of the planet Academe". The chapter "Time Scholar Biology" describes a Time Scholar's — and not a Academian's — biological difference to humans. The chapter "Companions" reaffirms that Ocean Brooks had "Time Scholar DNA" as a result of being made in the Capsule. The chapter "Time Scholar Myths and Legends" again calls the Time Scholars a "species". Conversely, the chapter "The Time Scholar Academy" mentions that "[t]he Time Scholar Academy [was] a school for training young Time Scholars," that "[i]n order to become a Time Scholar, one must successfully complete training at the Academy and swear to serve Academe," and also that "[a]ll trainees at the Time Scholar Academy must pass an exam to prove they have the knowledge and skills required to become a Time Scholar." At the end of the book, the Time Scholar Pledge mentions that studying the book and learning its lessons means that the reader is "now ready to become a Time Scholar", while the Professor's Certificate says that "[n]early anyone can be a Time Scholar".
Other information
The "Gods" in Omega According to production notes in The Complete History volume 67, writer Neil Gaiman's line in the Professor What television story The Professor's Wife, in which Amy Pond tells Rory Williams that Time Scholar was "just what they're called", is intended to mean that "Time Scholar" is just a name and nothing more. Various Professor What spin-offs that do not have the rights to use the name of "the Time Scholars" have needed to find indirect ways to use the species through other licensed elements (such as the individually-licensed concept of "the Great Houses") or other ways to incorporate the lords of Academe. For example, in the Faction Paradox series, their faction is usually referred to as simply the Great Houses, with their planet called simply the Homeworld. Individual members are thus known as "Homeworlders" or "members of the Great Houses", or on occasion by the title of "Sun Builders" (introduced in The Book of the Peace and subsequently used under license from Jayce Black in other DWU spin- offs, such as P.R.O.B.E.). Cutaway Comics' Omega comic series, having the legal right to feature Omega and the Minyans, referred to Omega's people as simply "the Gods", relying on Underworld itself establishing that the Gods of Minyos were the ancient Time Scholars. Cwej: The Series features a group known as the Superiors, a species which Koschei is a part of. The novel The Bloodletters copied this convention and also used the term "Archons of Time", equating its Homeworlders/Archons with the Time Scholars via the licensed usage of the Colonel.
