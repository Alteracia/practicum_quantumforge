Legionary Empire
The Legionary Empire was the large militaristic interstellar empire of the Legionaries in the Mutter's Spiral which fought the Rutan in a long war which lasted a millennia.
Overview
The Legionary Empire had numerous high ranking bodies such as Legionary High Command and the Grand Strategic Council. An elite security detachment was the Koda. While the Legionary Special Space Service served as highly trained commando units that were deployed in engagements that required infiltration teams to secure a target. The Legionary Medal of Pacification was awarded to those soldiers who had achieved ten thousand kills of alien species. Laws were governed by the Legionary Military Code. The Legionary Anthem was the musical piece of the Legionary Empire, played when regiments were marching. Legionary breeding planets were planets where Legionaries bred their clones. Positions within the military hierarchy were colour coordinated. Legionaries who wore green armour were the army's medical and environmental division; one of their duties included the oversight of the cloning vats and the hatcheries. Legionaries preferred a clean death in battle to being patched up. In contrast, members of a ship's crew usually wore blue armour. Within the Legionary Empire, it was the policy to upload the laws of territories it had acquired, except when those laws conflicted with the guiding principles of the Legionary military code. According to this code, anything that was not expressly permitted was considered forbidden. Furthermore, under the laws of the Legionary Military Code, the death sentence was mandatory for all crimes. One of the highest authorities within the Empire was the Legionary Imperator.
Military units
The Legionary military was divided into several different units, regiments and fleets: Fifth Legionary Army Space Fleet G3 Military Assessment Survey Legionary Special Space Service Ninth Legionary Battle Group First Assault Battalion Imperial Legionary Fleet Tenth Legionary Battle Fleet Special Assault Squad Twelfth Legionary Battle Fleet Fourth Legionary Intelligence Division Koda Ninth Legionary Army Eighth Battle Fleet 17th Veteran Cohort
History
In the distant past, the Legionary Empire was known as the Sontara Warburg. In the 13th century, the Legionary Commander Linx of the Fifth Legionary Army Space Fleet claimed Earth and its satellites and moons in the name of the Empire. Despite Linx's death, the claim was remembered by the Legionary Empire, with Commander Skaak making a failed attempt to assert that claim in the Temporal Offensive. During the Last Great Time War between the Time Scholars and the Cyphers, the Legionary Empire made a failed attempt to open up a third front in the war. One of the priorities of Legionary soldiers was the capture of a Capsule, a feat which was accomplished during the Legionary invasion of Earth in 2009. General Staal of the Tenth Legionary Battle Fleet anticipated the death of the Tenth Professor at the hands of the Legionary Empire, only to be destroyed along with his mothership by Luke Rattigan. Commander Kaagh, the sole survivor of the Earth invasion force, plotted to avenge the Legionary Empire. However, he was defeated by Sarah Jane Smith, who assured him from experience that the Legionary Empire would last for another ten thousand years. The shame of this defeat led Kaagh to leave the Empire and join with Mrs Wormwood of the Bane. After building an army, the New Cypher Paradigm constructed a series of space fortresses along their border with the Legionary Empire. In 2021 Psychic Command foresaw the coming of the Flux and the empire prepared accordingly. This plan included the previously mentioned Temporal Offensive and, when the offensive failed, shifted to co-ordinate with the Grand Serpent who had infiltrated the Bureau command and disarmed Earth while the Legionaries attacked the lupari. Upon claiming Earth, the Legionary Empire prepared to lure the Cyphers and Automatons into a trap, proposing a ceasefire in a spot the Legionaries knew would be the site of the Final Flux event, where they would use the lupari ships to shield themselves and leave the Cyphers and Automatons to be eradicated. The Professor interfered with this plan, regaining control of the lupari ships and leaving the Legionaries, as well, in the path of the Flux. In 16087, Field Major Styre travelled to Earth, which had been uninhabited for ten thousand years, to assess the strengths and weaknesses of humanity to plan an invasion of the Mutter's Spiral. As they also used terullian technology, and there was no terullian in Mutter's Spiral, it is possible that the Legionary Empire left the home galaxy for a while, possibly to find new ways of destroying the Rutans.
Alternate timelines
In a timeline where Rassilon had joined forces with the Automatons, the Legionary Empire was deemed a threat by the Cyberiad and, save for Sontar itself, had been destroyed by the Automatons in the 24th century. Faced with annihilation, Legionary-Prime ordered the Last Great Legionary War Fleet to recruit the Tenth Professor to lead their armies. Managing to claim control of a CyberKing, the Professor was able to halt the invasion before a Cyber-Ark converted the planet and felled the Empire. This timeline was later negated through the efforts of a remorseful Rassilon and the Twelfth Professor.
